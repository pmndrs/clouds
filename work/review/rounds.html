Standing decisions

  • Target: HZD and your photos; Takram/Sky Pro are not the target.
  • Master HZD procedural first, but build the later Nubis version where it's known to be better.
  • Sky weather: Nubis Evolved five-field data field; no rain channel; slow drift by default.
  • Imported VDBs render directly by default; sims going through the Nubis chain are closed at 32 m first.
  • Lit ridges crisp; only thin parts and undersides go soft; cohesion and vapour matter as much as detail.
  • Look work at ultra; a hard performance pass comes later.
  • Both Opus and Fable judge every look change; you're final.
  • Parity first: match Decima on Guerrilla's own clouds before tuning our own; your eye over the statistics.
