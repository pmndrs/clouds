What we learned

  1. Units matter more than shapes. Guerrilla's light sums are counted in density samples. Our short shadow samples were 10× too strong and the light grid 1.44× too strong. Most of the early pitting and over-dark shade came from that.
  2. The round blotches were the cloud's large-scale shape showing through the soft-glow term. Weighting the glow by the actual density and reading it a little deeper fixed them, and also most of Stormbird's slit, ring and pits.
  3. Wind smears the bumps. The detail noise drifts with the wind, and the frame blending only follows the camera. Re-aligning old frames can't fix it, because the bumps change shape as they drift. For the product, the fix is blending fewer frames when the wind is on; this belongs in the performance pass.
  4. The bump shapes were there all along. They lacked a short-range sun shadow at their own scale (4–13 m), and the glow and sky light were brightening exactly the dips. The hollows between bumps come from erosion, not from shadowing.
  5. The sky's horizon band has to be painted from the frames, and kept as background only. Letting it light the cloud over-brightens the undersides.
  6. The pink comes from warming the sunlight without warming the sky. On Stormbird the sunlit faces sit at the top of the tone curve, where the tint has little room to work.
  7. Asset time is ruled out. Guerrilla's cloud files have no animation state, so the silhouette differences are camera and noise.
  8. Process. Rounds that bundled many changes went backwards twice (7, 10). One change at a time, each with its own before/after crop sheet, then blind judging, works. Measures help but don't replace the eye, and yours is final.

Still open

in priority order
  1. Cauliflower hierarchy: Decima has bumps on bumps down to fine grain, with hollows between them. Ours are one size, and Decima still carries about 30% more fine grain, even with matched compression.
  2. Stormbird brightness and pink: the tone curve's shoulder limits the warmth.
  3. The far Parkouring view's base still softens into the haze band, where Decima has a crisp flat underside.
  4. Shadow flaws: the glow disc in the broad Stormbird cloud, ochre blotches, and the faint trunk ring and slit.
  5. Edges: ours are serrated and chalky, with a bright outline; Decima's are fibrous, with a pink rim.
