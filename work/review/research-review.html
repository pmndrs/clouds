What we learned

docs/research/topics/README.md
  • None of our three density paths runs the Horizon Zero Dawn chain. The procedural path is a Takram / Sky Pro port (one semicircle height profile, only the first noise channel, curl on everything). The sky layer and the voxel path follow Nubis Cubed, not HZD. Step one is an HZD reference shaper built from GPU Pro 7, with the real noise set.
  • The real HZD height curves are now known exactly. The Nutshell deck stores them as gradient stops in the slide XML, and the Nubis 2017 in-game strips agree within 0.02. Cumulus is full density from 9% to 65% of the layer height; our profile has no flat core at all. Nubis Cubed slide 18's top and bottom type tables are sampled to data too.
  • Surface detail is being thrown away before it reaches the screen. The noise blur constant (0.01) filters our 20 m billows to grey past about 1.2 km, and every cloud in your photos is 4–12 km away. Our march step inside clouds is 2.5–3× coarser than the deck's. Low and mobile tiers cut near-camera detail before its blend finishes (a real bug, confirmed in code).
  • Noise can only carry the two finest lobe levels. Your photos step lobes about 3.5× per level (300 → 70 → 20 m). The 300 m and 70 m levels have to be in the shape itself, procedural or authored.
  • Guerrilla grew clouds from a thin flat lens at the cloud base (Nubis Cubed s64). The EmberGen CloudPack does the same: one wide lens, one burst, the plume stops itself by cooling and diffusion. Our Pyro sims emit from hot spots, which is why L2 grew chimneys.
  • Houdini 21 has cloud tools we never used: cloud shape generate / replicate / from line / from intersection, billowy and wispy cloud noise, cloud clip for bases, VDB morph and reshape. The authoring guide maps each to a detail in your photos.
  • Guerrilla's Houdini noise generator (cooked headlessly) is a 1 : 1.7 : 2.3 Worley ladder over a chained Perlin-Worley, tiled by blending edges. Its default TGA export brightens two channels by about 35%; use EXR. psrdnoise can rebuild it with exact periodic tiling.
  • Takram is not the target. Recorded in memory, the plan and the agent brief. Every note now compares against the papers. Native ports that claim HZD clouds are surveyed for optional comparison only (Unity HDRP, Frostnova, Volumarcher, Project-Marshmallow).

Santa's Sleigh, read as a scene

docs/research/targets/santa-sleigh.md

The study now treats your hero as several bodies in one line, as you described. Measured from early → middle → end (17:23 to 17:28) and the 105 mm detail pan:

The line

Head nearest, link next, main body about 1.5× farther. The line recedes right at about 34° to the picture plane, bodies 2–2.5 base-heights apart.

Ejection dominates

Main tower height ÷ width went 0.48 → 0.68 → 0.76, a rise of about 3 m/s, all on the side facing the head.

Shear

Every detached turret leans up and toward the head by 30–48°, consistent with the deep body riding faster wind.

One life cycle, staggered

The head at end matches the main at early (H/W 0.50 vs 0.48). One template set with time offsets could cover all bodies.

Edges

Lit edges 3–12 m wide; shaded undersides and tails 20–40 m, 3–6× softer. Lobes step about 3.5× per level.

Frog

Lobing about 2× finer than Santa's. Lit bubbles on shaded faces, a lacy see-through undercut, a dissolving belly with shreds.

One reading to check

Across the five minutes the main body's left edge did not move against the line (within about 1°). The gap closed because the link and the small puffs built into the main, not because the main slid sideways. That may still be the main overtaking along the line of sight.
