Which of these two skies reads closer to a real sky?

Our HZD-chain procedural sky, same camera. The two judges split: Opus prefers B (rounder, separate domes, grey bases), Fable prefers A (B's bodies show stacked horizontal shelves). Your pick sets the direction for the next HZD pass and which judge to weight on skies.

2 · IMPORTED VDBS

Render imported VDBs directly by default?

Rendered directly (their own density, our lighting), EmberGen clouds read as one vapour body and match Karma's outline closely. Through the Nubis detail chain they turn into foam, even with your shape closing. The Nubis chain stays for Nubis-style smooth shapes and our procedural sky either way.

3 · MATERIAL TASK

Start the material task (M1)? It touches lighting.

Every judge, and Karma, says the same thing: our shaded sides are lit almost white (median 0.90 vs Karma's 0.64–0.76), creases are sky-blue instead of warm grey, the thin connective material is dropped by the converter (Karma's clouds cover 1.7–2.9× more of the frame), and there's no crisp lit crust over a darker interior. M1 would fix these against Karma's measured light numbers and your photos.

What we found

in the order it happened
1

Calibration: who judges like you

You and two AI judges scored nine renders blind. Fable tracked you far better than Opus (rank agreement 0.38 vs 0.04; 0.74 vs 0.30 without one outlier). Both missed your main axis, material and cohesion, so the rubric now scores realism of form, vapour vs foam, and connective tissue. You preferred the harsher scale; both judges now score every look change, with you as final judge.

2

Foam is half rendering, half form

Our high preset traced distant clouds at 41% resolution, blurring every edge; and imported sims are already puffy, so each puff got its own density ramp and the gaps eroded to sky. Closing the shape at 32 m (your call: now standard for imported sims) plus the sharper render gave the best version of every cloud: +1 to +2 points from both judges.

3

Pits instead of cauliflower: found and fixed

Our noise is byte-identical to Guerrilla's. The craters came from Nubis's wispy noise landing on dense surfaces, which their clouds never have. A new default keeps dense regions billowy; both judges preferred it in 5 of 6 pairs. Without pits the surface is smooth rather than cauliflower, which the material work has to solve.

4

The Frog from Houdini's cloud nodes

Twin domes with the notch, the long darker belly, terrace and satellites, matched to your photo's outline at 0.82 overlap. Rig: houdini/rigs/frog.hip. The satellites are faked with perspective until we support several volumes.

5

Karma ground truth and direct rendering

Path-traced in Karma, the EmberGen forms read as vapour: one body, thin material joining the lobes, warm-grey shade. Rendered directly in our renderer they come close in shape. So the remaining gap is lighting and the converter dropping thin material — question 3.
