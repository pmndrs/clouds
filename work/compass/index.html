Where we are

551 commits from 16 Sep to 22 Oct 2026, then a two-week pause. The engine is broad; the look is not yet locked, and the process grew heavier than the work.

The short read

We built nearly every piece of the Nubis Evolved 2022 procedural sky and a renderer-agnostic WebGPU runtime (about 25k lines, with a test that forbids three.js imports). What we do not have is a look you sign off on: your last note (22 Oct) says the shaping is drifting back toward foam. Speed was deliberately left for later and is 4 to 6 times over the game's budget. Volumes still come in through an offline Houdini script and a vendored copy of WebVDB, while WebVDB itself is release-ready in its own repo.

Styles: Horizon is one of them

Your d1 answer was "both, case by case": “Horizon gets us there but they have a vibe. The clouds are real and a better weight in some cases.” The early September raymarch looks were excellent, and they stay. So style is a switch on the same sky, not a single target. Each style has its own contract, and the defects list applies to all of them.

The Horizon contract, kept from draft 1:

Horizon: keep it, match it

style
  • Big, cohesive, simplified bodies with readable silhouettes at distance, rather than photo-accurate fractal detail
  • Bright, almost painted lit tops; billows stay crisp on the lit side
  • Shade that goes grey-warm, not blue (you fixed the hue in LH1)
  • Strong silver lining and dark-edge effect at low sun
  • Soft, wispy bases and thin parts; clean cauliflower hierarchy on towers
  • Wide, uncluttered sky with clear lanes between clusters (the Nubis 2017 map)
  • Slow, stately motion; time-lapse is a feature

Defects: always fix, every style

bugs
  • Foam, sprinkles and pitting: small puffs where a body should read as one mass
  • Sparkle on ridges, temporal smear, ghosting behind fast motion
  • Pops and blank frames when streaming, at layer edges or on load
  • Shadows that jump or jitter as they update
  • Banding and blockiness that the upscaler cannot hide
  • Anything that only looks right at ultra and falls apart at the level we ship

Your photos stay the reference for anatomy and weight in every style. We are adding a path-traced reference of the same cloud, using your pathtracer’s volume path (validated against pbrt and Mitsuba). It shows which oddities are Horizon’s treatment and which are our approximation errors.

Render levels: upscaler first

If every project ships with the upscaler, clouds should never be designed for native resolution. The cloud pass renders at an internal size, hands the upscaler colour, alpha, depth, motion vectors and a reactive mask, and the upscaler is the output path. That also unblocks storm rotation, which needs motion vectors.

The ms targets are proposals for the cloud pass on your laptop at 1440p output, measured in the HZD bench sky. Today's sky is 13 to 22 ms at ultra; Nubis Evolved quotes 2 to 4 ms on a PS4-class GPU. Judging rule change: look rounds are judged at Desktop as well as at Cinematic, because a look that only holds at ultra is not done.
