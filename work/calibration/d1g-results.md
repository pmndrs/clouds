- **Round 7 is a regression overall.** Fable picks round 6 everywhere; Opus picks it at distance in 3 of 4 framings and splits up close.
- The disc array is gone only in appearance. Both judges still find faint pale discs in round 7, and Opus notes that the haze hides the features rather than fixing them.
- Round 7 reads defocused and under-dense. Fable measures the lowest band energy of the three sources at 1–3, 4–12 and 12–32 px in all 11 regions, with 30–60 percent of the reference's 4–32 px energy gone (p213 tower 12–32 px: Decima 9.1, r6 6.4, r7 3.2). This contradicts the D1 report's claim that lit-face band energy matches the frames at 2–16 px; the two measurements have to be reconciled.
- Round 7 also loses the dark underside on p213, dissolves the s206 foot and adds green, lavender or yellow casts.

Remaining gaps (both judges, biggest first):
1. **Lit-face surface:** dense florets (Decima 30–80 px at 4k, i.e. 3–15 px in a 1080p frame) and fine mottle on every crest; both renders are smooth below the main lobe.
2. **Shade:** Decima's shade is light, warm and evenly mottled. Round 6 has separate cut-out lobes with discs, a slit and pits, and is too deep and cool; round 7 is flat and hazy.
3. **Colour:** Decima's lit faces are cream to pink (R−B +8 to +22) with mauve shade; ours are neutral. Skies miss the white haze band.
4. **Edges:** Decima's crests are crisp and finely serrated, with wispy feet. Ours are cut-out staircases on the s33t3 trunk, a sparkle fringe in r7 or frayed spikes in r6.
5. **Artefacts in r6:** disc arrays (p101), the whorl and contour terracing (p213, p101), the slit and pit on the s33t3 trunk, extra bloblets.
6. **Light distribution:** Decima puts a bright band on the crests with mid-tone just below, and a dark slate base; ours light the upper body evenly.
