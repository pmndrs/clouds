# H7 blind judging: Opus (2026-10-10)

Set: eight unlabelled 1920x1080 renders in `scratchpad/blind-h7/`, judged at full resolution with 1:1 side-by-side crops. References: GPU Pro 7 figs 4.13–4.15, HZD 2015 slides 36–38, HFW image55/56, owner photos Santa's Sleigh `end.jpg` and Frog `DSC00589-Pano.jpg`. Rubric v2 (8 criteria) plus HZD likeness. I did not open the key, captures, reports, the board or other judges' files.

## Pairs

The pairs are identified by their identical camera and cloud layout:

| View | Version A (streaks) | Version B (haze) |
|---|---|---|
| ground (horizon at y≈680) | h59 | h39 |
| mid (horizon at y≈570, warm haze on right) | h35 | h69 |
| sunset (backlit) | h42 | h66 |
| above (camera over the layer) | h31 | h88 |

The change between versions is the same in every pair:
- **Version A** adds directional, combed wisps and streamers. Some trail off the bodies and some float on their own in open sky. In the near views they also cross in front of the lit faces of the bodies.
- **Version B** has a fine, speckled or mottled veil around the bases and in the gaps. The bodies are cleaner, with clearer domes and stronger terraced shelves.

## Pair verdicts

- **Ground: h59 over h39, narrowly.** The streamers trail off the upper bodies and connect neighbours, which is closer to HZD/HFW wind-sheared skies than h39's faint mottled veil. Against h59: the isolated streak puffs in empty sky, the stripes across the left body's face and more pellets on the upper-left group.
- **Mid: h69 over h35, clearly.** h69 has the best lobe hierarchy and belly shading of the set. In h35 the streak layer runs across the sunlit faces, which makes them look brushed or motion-blurred. Wisps belong off and between the bodies, not painted over them. h69 still has the terraced shelf through the main body and the flat overhead slab.
- **Sunset: h42 over h66.** h42's thin streaks read as high wisps. h66 has blotchy white veil patches between the bodies, which is the "smog" failure. Both are flat backlit cut-outs.
- **Above: h31 over h88, marginally.** h31's broad smears at least flow off the body bases. h88 wraps every base in speckled dust and has more bead pellets. Both are a one-scale suds carpet on top, and h31's smears have no volume.

Version A (streaks/skirts) wins 3 of 4 pairs. It loses where the streaks cross lit faces at close range.

## Three biggest remaining gaps

1. **Connective flow is still not volumetric skirts.** A gives flat, combed 2D stripes: they cross lit faces, float detached in open sky and, from above, look like painted smears. B gives a speckled veil, which reads as smog or dust. The target (HZD 2015 slide 36–37, GPU Pro 7 fig 4.15, HFW image55) is lit, thinning, curl-distorted material attached to the base and downwind flank of each body, with volume and self-shadow, and clear sky elsewhere.
2. **Vertical structure is unchanged.** Flat terraced shelves still cut through the bodies (the h69/h39 left masses). The overhead slab is a smooth grey sheet with contour-line terracing in every ground and mid view. Bellies are pancake-flat. From above the tops are a single bubble scale. The middle lobe rung shows on some near bodies (h69) but not across the field.
3. **Material and lighting.** The bodies read as evenly lit cotton or plastic, with no bright crust over a darker interior and no translucency at thin edges. At sunset the bodies are flat dark cut-outs with a rim only, where HFW shows lit interiors. Pellet or bead fragments still sit on satellite edges and turret tips (worst in h88 and the h59 upper-left group).
