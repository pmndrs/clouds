## Notes

What separates the best from the worst is coherent massing with nested lobes. c94, c54 and c15 read as one body with medium domes carried on a big mass, and real crevice shadows between them. The bottom group either collapses to a primitive (c51's cone, c39's cotton-ball egg), falls apart into fragments (c61, c52, c55) or smears into haze (c42).

Every render misses the same things the photos show:
- **Edges:** lit tops are as soft as undersides. The photos have 3–12 m crisp crusts against 20–40 m soft bellies, and nothing here has that contrast. Several detail views also show a dither stipple on the edges.
- **Shadows:** they are blue-tinted and shallow. The Frog belly and the Santa link are a deep neutral grey under brilliant white lit lobes.
- **Base:** none has a flat condensation base.
- **Fine detail:** none has the final 10–25 m cauliflower octave that makes the crowns look sharp.
- **Satellites:** several scatter spherical blobs where the photos have sheared, connected turrets.

No render is near a pass. The best (c94) would read as clearly CG next to any of the owner's frames.
