Remaining gap (both): (1) contrast: lit crust near white, darker grey belly (photos' belly 80–115 of 255; ours 140–150); a thin bright rim over a slightly darker lit interior; (2) grain everywhere instead of smooth faces broken by sharp creases, grain only at rims and crests; (3) thin edges should go translucent to sky without blurring the dense crust; (4) forms: smoke-sim curls, rounded bottoms, no flat base, one or two lobe steps instead of three; (5) Frog: right arrangement at the wrong abstraction, no flat grey belly sheet.
