# D1 blind judge (Opus)

Set: `scratchpad/blind-d1/d01–d16` (1920x1080). Judged by eye: full frames, then 1:1, 1.6x and 2x crops of the lit tops, shadowed interiors, towers and bases, laid out side by side. I did not open the key, `work/`, `artifacts/research/d1-parity/` or any report.

Scores run from 1 to 10 and measure how closely each image matches the guessed reference. 10 would be indistinguishable from it.

## Groups and reference guesses

| Framing | Images | Reference guess | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: wide cumulus, front | d01 d05 d06 d07 | **d07** | Different sky (grey-blue, no sun bloom, circular banding in the sky gradient, JPEG blocking). Warm peach lit side, mauve-grey body. |
| B: two towers, far | d02 d03 d09 d10 | **d02** | Teal sky that goes white at the horizon. Warm white billows and a smooth grey flat underside. |
| C: tall tower | d04 d11 d12 d13 | **d13** | Same sky and capture traits as d07. Peach and mauve. |
| D: two towers, near | d08 d14 d15 d16 | **d16** | Teal sky like d02. Pink-white billows and a mauve base. |

In every framing the three non-reference images share one sky: a sun bloom at top left, a blue gradient and a grey horizon band. The reference is always the odd one out. I'm confident in all four picks.

The three renders look like the same three settings repeated in every framing. I inferred this by eye; I did not check the key.
- **Clean:** d01, d10, d12, d14. Smooth, bright, low internal contrast, few artefacts.
- **Cool/grainy:** d05, d03, d04, d15. Blue shadows, fine pitting or crosshatch grain, hairy fringes, more lobe relief.
- **Dirty:** d06, d09, d11, d08. Neutral to warm, darker interiors, sepia sparkle flecks and dark smudges.

## A: reference d07

Ranking: **d01 (5.5) > d05 (4.5) > d06 (3)**

### d01: 5.5
- Surface (6): smooth, with no pitting at 2x. However, the lobes are washed out, and it lacks d07's dense small billows with grey creases.
- Shading (5): the body is too bright and flat. d07's interior is a darker mauve-grey with clear lobe-to-lobe shading. No dirt.
- Colour (4): neutral to cool white. d07 is clearly warm, with a peach lit side and mauve shadows.
- Edges (6): the top edge is soft and slightly mushy. The bottom skirt is wispier and more ragged than d07's denser, cleaner bottom.
- Overall (5.5): the same cloud, but it reads as a brighter, flatter, cooler engine.

### d05: 4.5
- Surface (4): the top lobes are well defined, the nearest to d07's relief. But the lower interior shows fine pitting and speckle at 2x (x560–880, y580–760).
- Shading (5): the crease shading on top is right. The interior is a uniform blue-grey with small dark pocks.
- Colour (3): a clearly blue cast across the shadows.
- Edges (5): the top edge is crisp and lumpy, which is good. The bottom fringe is grainy and wispy.
- Overall (4.5): the best relief in this framing, spoiled by grain and blue.

### d06: 3
- Surface (2): yellow and sepia sparkle flecks all through the mid-interior, which reads as dirty mottle.
- Shading (2): large dark smudges at the bottom centre (x900–1100, y750–880). d07 has nothing like them.
- Colour (5): neutral to warm grey, the closest palette to d07 among the three.
- Edges (6): the top is soft and smooth.
- Overall (3): the palette is close, but the dirt dominates.

## Main remaining differences (all variants)

1. **Colour temperature.** Every reference is warm: peach or pink lit faces with mauve-grey shadows. Our renders run neutral to blue, and the shadows are blue in every setting except the dirty one. Our sky and sun setup (bloom at top left, blue zenith) differs from the reference skies (grey-blue or teal, no visible sun), so the ambient and sun colour feeding the cloud is probably off.
2. **Billow relief versus value key.** The references have a lower-key, mid-value body covered in small rounded lumps separated by soft grey creases. The clean setting flattens this into a bright, featureless blow-out. The grainy setting gets the relief but adds noise. None of the three has both clean surfaces and dense lobe relief.
3. **Pitting and grain.** Fine crosshatch or "brushed hair" hatching in the grainy setting (d03 and d15 worst). Sepia sparkle flecks in the dirty setting's shadows. Faint pocks even in the clean setting at 2x (d12, d14). The references show none of these.
4. **Dark smudges.** The dirty setting puts concentrated black or sepia blobs in shadowed volumes and along the base (d06, d08, d09, d11). The references' shadows are smooth gradients.
5. **Edges.** The reference silhouettes are rounded and fairly crisp, with smooth puffs. Ours have hairy, fuzzy fringes, worst in B and D, and wispy ragged skirts at the base where the references end in a denser, cleaner bottom.
6. **Underside.** The references have a smooth grey flat base (B and D especially). The clean setting loses it (white base). The dirty setting overshoots it into blobs.

If one setting has to go forward, the clean one (d01, d10, d12, d14) is the least wrong. It needs the references' warmth and darker mid-value body, plus d04/d05's lobe relief without their grain.
