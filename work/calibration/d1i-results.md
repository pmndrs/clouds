Remaining gaps (both judges):

2. Edges: the frames are grainy-crisp with tiny protrusions; ours are clean and soft, breaking up only as serrated white rims.

4. Colour and exposure: blown flat-white lit tops (the s33t3 dome, the s206 cap); the frames keep pink-cream lit faces and a cooler shade. Skies are too saturated.
5. Lobe junctions: the frame is low-contrast haze; ours cut a dark wedge.
6. Camera: the p101 cloud sits lower and larger, s206 about 15 px low.
