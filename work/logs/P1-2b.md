# P1.2b: fit constants (fitMs, fitAmbient, fitK) by a linear fit to the path trace

- Added `fitKUp` (the open-sky share's K, default 0.15 = old fitK) and `fitDirect` (debug gain on the direct term, default 1); Frame.fit2.zw; Review row inputs. Default fit frames pixel-identical to P1-2a's (sky noon, backlit: max diff 0).
- Capture: work/captures/P1-2b/cap.sh (640x360 linear, converged, fit-noon + fit-backlit per page load, ~15 s per cloud). Reference: the card's 2-seed PT at 1280x720, box-halved (as the P1-2a sheet).
- The image is affine, not linear, in the terms (sky through T, aerial perspective), so a sixth render with every term at 0 is the offset: 6 configs x 2 clouds x 2 suns = 24 short renders.
- Affinity check (sky): direct + 3 (ms0.15 - zero) + (amb - zero) reproduces the default frame to 7e-5 mean relative.
- Fit (work/captures/P1-2b/fit.py; grid least squares of log(model/PT), non-negative) over 4 frames x 5 regions: K 0.15 -> ms 2.20 amb 0.06 (rms log 0.137); K 0.4 -> 2.91, 0.40 (0.152); K 1.0 -> 4.05, 0.62 (0.198). Quadratic in log K: best ~0.178. Extra ms capture at K 0.18: ms 2.30, amb 0.13, rms log 0.134 (best).
- The fit wants almost no ambient (0.13): the isotropic ms term stands in for the sky light. Worst region either way: embergen02 backlit bright20 ~0.71.
- New fit defaults: fitMs 2.3, fitK 0.18, fitAmbient 0.13 (fitKUp 0.15, fitDirect 1). First re-capture still showed the old defaults: vite runs with CLOUDS_NO_WATCH and kept the old module; restarted it and re-captured (cost 30 s of the render budget). Total rendering ~5 min (20 frames + 4 zero + 4 ms at K 0.18 + 2 x 4 final).
- Re-captured frames match the predicted ratios exactly. Sheet: work/captures/P1-2b/sheet.jpg (127 KB), sheet.py; nubis tiles are P1-2a's frames (unchanged model).
- Off by more than 25 %: embergen02 backlit bright20 0.71 (also sky backlit bright20 0.85): the backlit lit parts want forward-peaked higher-order scattering that an isotropic ms cannot give without over-brightening noon. The near-zero ambient says ms is standing in for sky fill; colour (B/R) of shaded sides not checked.
