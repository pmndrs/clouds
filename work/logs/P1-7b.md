With it on: the same (rim 3/255 darker, slight colour shift in the diff). tower2: no silver lining either way; a flat dark-grey body with a 1-2 px pale halo of the same brightness all round the silhouette (5 deg or 25 deg from the sun alike, so not a phase effect; most likely sky seen through the one-voxel soft edge).

3. Limiting factor: the cloud's thin fringe, not the phase and not the ms forward weight. Same lighting, same geometry: the soft-fringed VDB gets the rim, the hard-edged tower does not. The direct (phase) term cannot help with the sun inside the silhouette: the sun ray from edge material crosses the body (tau along the sun ray 36 to 327), so the droplet peak is multiplied by ~exp(-36) or less; it only acts on thin material with a clear path to the sun (sun outside or at the very edge, detached wisps). Raising fitMsFwd lifts the core about 4x more than the rim, so the rim does not come from forward ms either; it comes from how much low-density material the edge has.
