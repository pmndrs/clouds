# P1.3a: splotches on the fit model's shaded base (HZD bench)

- Repro: main's P1-2d fit capture (gp7) already shows them in the overhead base (top-left): flat, hard-edged grey patches tens of px across.
- View: gp7@look:0:50 (pitch 50, the overhead base fills the left half), 1280x720, clean=1&motion=0&proc_lightingModel=fit. Patches confirmed (big flat light blob ~x300-440 y40-190, plateaus lower left).
- converged (c_converged=1): the big blob weakens, smaller hard-edged plateaus remain. Not purely a sampling shortcut.
- tap reuse off (temp shader edit, always recompute): patches unchanged; skyLayer 14 -> 23 ms. Not the reuse. (Note: a literal `true ||` in that condition made the sky layer silently fail to build; a runtime-true uniform test worked.)
- skyTau = 0 (temp): patches unchanged (image bluer). Not the upward taps.
- shadowTau dropped from tau (temp): the patches vanish (faint traces left). The sun taps print them; with reuse already ruled out, suspect the shadowTau >= 32 early-out (skips the remaining cone taps and all far taps; the fit ms term 1/(1+K tau) still separates 32 from ~100, the octave/nubis exp(-tau) do not).
- Cause: the sun taps stop at shadowTau >= 32 (rest of the cone and all 4 far taps skipped). Where the near cone alone reached 32 the sample got tau ~32-50; next to it the full sum (~100+). exp(-tau) hides that; fit's ms 1/(1+0.4 tau) does not, so the stop's on/off map printed as flat hard-edged patches.
- Fix: ProceduralSettings.lightTauCap (P.wind.w, free lane); 0 = auto: 32 for nubis/octave (unchanged), none (1e30) for fit. Tune panel key proc.lightTauCap. After (gp7@look:0:50, fit): patches gone, base smooth and darker overall.
- Cost (fit only): samples whose cone saturated now take the remaining cone taps (up to 5) and the 4 far taps: at most +9 taps per tap refresh, i.e. the uncapped tap count (lightTaps-1 + 4 far + 2 up) at every refresh; reuse unchanged. skyLayer timings in these captures are too noisy to separate (9-14 ms either way).
- typecheck clean; vitest procedural + hzd 73/73.
- Pixel identity (default bench, gp7 640x360, sky_temporal=0&sky_freezeCycle=0, branch vs main's trace.wgsl + settings.ts): max diff 0.
