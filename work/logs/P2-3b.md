- Read: the cone is gone: the big body's flanks are steeper and its top flatter, and the small left tower now shows two rounded heads. But it is broken in one clear way: a straight vertical seam/cliff runs down through the big body where two turret cells meet. Cause: neighbouring cells have different heights, so the top jumps at the cell border, and since the whole column's h is renormalised by top, the jump cuts through the body at every height, not just at the top. turretDepth 0.25 makes the body taller but the seam stays (structural, not a constant). The turrets also lower the big body by roughly a third (mean factor < 1), so it reads as a mesa with one taller block rather than the photo's tower of stacked heads. Small clouds: about the same, a bit lower. Still unlike the photo: no stacked bulging turrets on the flanks, tops smooth and flat, bodies too wide and low.
- Proposed next: make the turret field continuous (smooth-max of per-cell domes, or blend cell heights by a smooth F1 weight, instead of the hard nearest cell), and normalise so the tallest head keeps the H6 height.
