# P2.9a: Karma renders of the Houdini cumulus (sun behind the cloud)

**What:** `scripts/houdini/karma-cloud.py` (hython + husk, Houdini 21.0.792 Indie) renders
`assets/clouds/houdini-stages/cumulus_4_wispy.vdb` with **Karma CPU** through Solaris. Three frames at 960x540:
`work/captures/P2-9a/{sunward,noon,low}.{exr,png}`, the sheet `work/captures/P2-9a/sheet.jpg` (raw, noisy: the reference),
and `sheet_denoised.jpg` / `*_oidn.png` (OIDN on the beauty only, same exposure, shown only so the frames are easier to read).
Rig: `houdini/rigs/karma_cloud.hip`. Settings and camera numbers: `work/captures/P2-9a/report.json`.

```bash
HYTHON=/Applications/Houdini/Houdini21.0.792/Frameworks/Houdini.framework/Versions/Current/Resources/bin/hython
$HYTHON scripts/houdini/karma-cloud.py                  # 3 frames, 32 spp, 180 s cap per frame (husk killed after)
$HYTHON scripts/houdini/karma-cloud.py --reuse          # re-tonemap / denoise / sheet from the EXRs, no render
```

## Scene

- **Volume:** Volume LOP on the VDB as written: metres, y up, base y = 0, 12 m voxels, fog grid `density` 0..1. No rescale.
- **Material:** MaterialX `kma_volume` fed by `mtlxgeompropvalue density`: sigma_t = 0.08 x density **per metre** (scene unit
  = metre), scattering = 0.99 sigma_t, absorption = 0.01 sigma_t, one HG lobe g = 0.8 (no second lobe).
- **Lights:** Karma Physical Sky LOP (KarmaSkyDomeLight + its own distant sun, angle 0.51 deg, turbidity 3, default
  intensities). Azimuth convention checked on the stage: az 0 = sun toward -z, az 90 = toward +x.
- **Render settings:** engine CPU; convergence mode **Path Traced** (uniform spp); volume limit **32**; volume step rate
  **1.0** (12 m steps, about one mean free path at density 1); diffuse / reflection / refraction limits 0 (no surfaces).
- **Tone map:** linear EXR -> exposure (per frame: the 99th-percentile luminance maps to 3.0) -> ACES fit (Narkowicz) -> sRGB.
  EVs: sunward +1.95, noon +3.64, low +4.22. Compare shapes and ratios within a frame, not brightness across frames.

**Sunward camera:** the brief asked for sun 32 deg, 3 deg behind the top edge, *and* 5-6 km away. With a 2 km tall cloud and a
camera near the ground those cannot all hold (at 5.5 km the top edge is only about 20 deg up). I kept the sun angle and the
3 deg rule and solved the distance, exactly as our card bench does (`sunwardCamera` in `examples/lab/reference-pt/cards.ts`:
silhouette tau >= 0.1, view aimed 7 deg below the sun): distance 2.36 km, top edge at 35.0 deg, optical depth along the ray
to the sun 26.8. The sun disc is behind the tower, about row 141, column 480 of the frame.

The sunward frame is the 16 spp full-size calibration render: the 32 spp run of it hit the 180 s cap and was killed.
Render time used in all: about 11 minutes (including the killed runs and small tests).

## The earlier Karma failure (P2-6c)

- **What made today's first try work** is not one parameter. Same material recipe as P2-6c, but: the current 12 m VDB, Physical
  Sky instead of a plain dome + distant light, the camera closer, and Path Traced convergence.
- **Black above the horizon:** re-running P2-6c's `build_render_stage` on today's VDB (320x180, 4 spp) gives the cloud correctly
  shaded on a **black background everywhere**. The plain `domelight::2.0` lights the cloud but is not drawn as the background;
  the Physical Sky sets `renderlightgeo` and is visible. With no ground in that scene, "everything above the camera" is
  just the empty background (likely, not proven: I did not toggle the dome's render-light-geometry switch to confirm).
- **128 m blocks:** **not reproduced.** P2-6c's recipe on the current 12 m VDB renders smooth billows, no blocks. The blocks were
  at the 16 m VDB's leaf size (8 voxels), so the earlier VDB or its x750 transform remain the suspects; not chased further.
- **Speed trap:** the default convergence mode (Variance oracle) took about 38 camera rays per pixel even at spp 1, so the first
  480x270 tries were killed at 60 s. Setting `convergence_mode = Path Traced` makes spp mean spp. XPU on this Mac ran at about
  the same speed as CPU on a small test; CPU was used for the frames.

## What the sunward frame shows

Measured on the linear EXR (luminance, columns or rows averaged over 20-30 px; about 2-2.5 m per pixel at the tower edge).

- **Rim width:** the bright band is about 15 px (~35 m) on the tower edge nearest the sun and 25-30 px (~50-70 m) on
  edges further from it. Mean free path at density 1 is 12.5 m, so the band is a few mean free paths deep.
- **Fade or cutout:** the outside edge is sharp (sky to peak in 5-6 px, about one voxel), but the inside **fades gradually**:
  after the peak it falls back to sky level over 10-25 px and keeps falling into the body over a further 50+ px
  (left lobe: 0.60 peak -> 0.29 -> 0.13 -> 0.07). Not a cutout: a cutout would be a flat bright band ending abruptly inside.
- **Brighter toward the sun:** yes, strongly. Peak / adjacent sky: 5.8x on the tower edge about 2.5 deg from the sun, 3.6x at
  about 6 deg, 2.8x on the left lobe about 12 deg away, and almost no rim on the tower's right edge (about 19 deg away). The band
  also gets narrower as it gets brighter near the sun.
- **Core:** blue-grey, not black. The tower interior is 0.025-0.04 against sky 0.12-0.2 near it (0.2-0.3x the sky, about 1/30
  of the rim peak); it reads as a mid-dark blue-grey lit by the sky and multiple scattering, with soft darker folds.
