# P2.7a: tower3, tower2 reworked to the Houdini cumulus recipe

- Code: examples/lab/reference-pt/tower3-bake.ts (new) + a `tower3` entry in cards.ts (picker label "H tower, Houdini recipe (P2.7a probe)"). `tower`, `tower2` and their bakes unchanged.
- Recipe:
  - Lobes: three bodies instead of one column of nine. Congestus core (x 0, 2.3 km tall, envelope radius 620 -> 360 m, slight lean) and two flanks (x -760 / +720, 0.8 / 0.62 km tall, flatter). Each body is filled with level-0 ellipsoids (44 / 26 / 22) of 0.45..0.75 x the envelope radius, centres anywhere within 0.55 of it, so they overlap at random heights.
  - Level 1: 9..14 children per level-0 lobe on its surface (biased outward from the body axis and up, none steeper than -0.45 down), kept only where they poke out of the level-0 union (CPU check); 0.28..0.5 x parent, about 60..200 m.
  - Level 2 (25..90 m, up-facing level-1 only) exists but is off by default (`?towerLevels=2`): with it on, the surface was uniform fine popcorn; the 50 m push pass covers that scale.
  - Billowy as an SDF push along the normal: 340 / 130 / 50 m features, 75 / 34 / 15 m push; each pass a Worley dome (cap per cell, creased valleys) x 0.8 + fbm x 0.2, domain-warped by 0.3 x the feature size at 0.6 x it; weight 0.45 on undersides, 1 facing up.
  - Wisps as advection: the field is read at p - v, v a mostly horizontal 2-octave value-noise flow at 90 m, 130 m low down / facing down, 20 m on tops.
  - Density: 10 m ramp on up-facing upper surfaces, 75 m underneath and low; fbm erosion only on soft parts that are low or facing down; interior fbm as tower2; ragged soft base (90 m smoothstep with a 45 m fbm offset).
- Bake: 389 ms (tower2 488 ms in the same session), grid 397 x 313 x 243 at 8 m, 115 MB f32, lobes 92 / 406 (level 2 off). Occupancy 0.19.
- Captures (640x360, own vite on :5471, PATHTRACER_DIR = git-archive of pathtracer ad9c01e, --settle 400, converged=1): tower2/ and tower3/ fit-noon, fit-low. Two tower3 tries + one tower2, about 1.5 min of rendering. Tuning between the tries: level 2 off, erosion and wisps limited to low / downward faces (the first try speckled the side faces with holes), core 2.05 -> 2.3 km.
- Sheet: work/captures/P2-7a/sheet.jpg (sheet.py): rows noon / low; tower2 | tower3 | Houdini P2.6c stage 4 crop (top) and the owner's texas-afternoon tower crop (bottom).
- Read, against the photo:
  - Mass: better. One broad body with a wide base and two flanks, much closer to Houdini and the photo than tower2's slim column. But the core reads as a straight-sided, flat-topped drum (a "bucket"), not the photo's tapering, leaning tower with a big shoulder; and the whole is squatter than the photo.
  - Tiers: better. No stacked drums any more.
  - Billow scales: mixed. At noon there are larger billows carrying smaller ones (better than tower2). At fit-low the lit face is an even pebbly popcorn, worse than tower2's low view; the 340 m pass barely reads, the photo's big billows are not there yet.
  - Underside: slightly better. The bottom rim is ragged and softer, less of a hard slab, but still a flat dark base; nowhere near Houdini stage 4's torn rims.
- Proposed next (one at a time): (1) taper and lumpy-profile the core envelope (smaller rTop, random radial bulges per height, a bigger shoulder lobe), so it stops reading as a cylinder; (2) make the 340 m pass dominate: raise its push (75 -> ~120 m) and drop the 50 m pass's dome term to fbm only, to kill the pebbles; (3) wisps on the lower rims need a real multi-step advection, the one-shot warp is too weak to tear.
