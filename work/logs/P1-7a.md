# P1.7a: droplet (Mie) forward peak for the fit model's direct term

- Code: new CloudSettings `fitMiePhase` (default 0 = unchanged, Frame.fit4.w = f[151]). With 1, `cloudFitLightMs`'s direct term uses `miePhase(cosSun)` (the Jendersie & d'Eon 2023 HG + Draine fit, paper constants via the existing `mieParams` at `dropletDiameter`, default 10 um; clamped at MIE_PHASE_MAX 25 / sr like phaseModel 'mie') instead of HG(cosSun, fitG). The ms and ambient terms are untouched. `miePhase` is the body of the old `cloudPhase` mie branch pulled out into a function (cloudPhase calls it; same maths). Both fit routes (voxel march `cloudFitLightMs`, procedural trace `cloudFitLight`) share the one function, so both get it.
- Option used: the preferred HG + Draine (not the two-lobe HG).
- Switches: bench Review P27 (src c, key fitMiePhase, shown with P12 fitted lighting on); bench URL c_fitMiePhase=1; card page s.fitMiePhase=1 (existing s.<key> pass-through, no new parameter) plus a 'mie direct' box in the P1.2a fit row; the card report's `fit` array now ends with fitMiePhase.
- Captures: own vite on :5447, PATHTRACER_DIR = git-archive of pathtracer ad9c01e (node_modules symlinked from the checkout), 640x360, converged=1, --settle 400; tower2 and embergen02, fit-noon and fit-backlit, off/on. About 2 min of rendering. Sheet work/captures/P1-7a/sheet.jpg (sheet.py): rows tower2/embergen02 x backlit/noon, columns off | on | |on-off| x4.
- Numbers (8-bit luminance, on - off): tower2 backlit 2.4k px changed, mean -1.6, max darkening 7, max brightening 0; noon 6.8k px, -1.2. embergen02 backlit 11.9k px, -1.5, max -6; noon 13.2k px, -1.1. No saturated pixels either way. No pixel got brighter.
- Read: no rim. The card's "backlit" view is not close enough to the sun for a droplet peak: the sun is out of frame to the left, about 38 degrees from the view centre, and the nearest cloud edge is about 30 degrees from it. The Mie peak lives within about 5 degrees of the sun; at 20-40 degrees real droplets scatter about half of what HG(0.8) does, and at 90 degrees a seventh. So with the setting on, thin edges get slightly darker (up to 7/255) in the backlit frame, and noon is essentially unchanged (-1/255 on the body; the direct term is a small share next to the fitted ms term there). Nothing blown out, no sparkle. The check cannot answer whether a rim appears, because no frame looks within a few degrees of the sun.
- Proposed next: a card view (or a sun preset) with the sun just behind a thin edge (2-5 degrees off the silhouette, e.g. the backlit camera aimed at the sun with the cloud between) and capture off/on there; that is the only geometry where the setting does anything bright. If adopted, the fit's ms/ambient would need a refit, since side-scatter drops sharply (the path-traced reference uses a single HG lobe too, so it cannot judge the peak).
