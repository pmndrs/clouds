# P2.4a proof: one cumulus tower from a sphere hierarchy (card cloud `tower`)

- Code: examples/lab/reference-pt/tower-bake.ts (new) + a `tower` entry in cards.ts (CARD_CLOUDS, CARD_DEFS, loadCard branch). Spheres 9 / 71 / 454 (L0 r 350..550, L1/L2 0.5x, on the parent surface biased up + outward), polynomial smin k = 0.2 r, value-noise displacement 25 m @ 250 m + 8 m @ 70 m faded toward the base, density saturate(-sdf / w) with w = mix(80, 12, h / 600) widened to 80 where the blended gradient points down, x smoothstep 60 m above base 1500. Sigma 0.45 / m at density 1. ?towerVoxel= and ?towerLevels= on the URL (defaults 8, 2).
- GPU bake (brute force over all spheres with smin early-out): 392 x 344 x 200 at 8 m (3.1 x 2.75 x 1.6 km box), 103 MB f32, 260 ms. Occupied 3.0 x 1.45 km, 2.65 km tall.
- Captures (1280x720, --settle 400, png, ~35 s rendering in all): first/ at camera distance 4800 (tower filled the frame, base cut off), then distance 8000 for fit-noon, fit-low, fit-backlit. Sheet work/captures/P2-4a/sheet.jpg (sheet.py): noon | low | backlit | photo tower crop.
- Read: the silhouette reads as a cumulus tower (stacked column stepping sideways, a lower shoulder, a flat soft base). Close up (first/) the surface reads as dough or cauliflower: a pile of soft balls. L0 and L1 lobes are clear; L2 bumps show only close up. Lit tops are crisper than the base, but the whole surface is smooth and waxy. Unlike the photo: no fine billows or wisps, bumps too even and too round, the tower too slender next to the photo's massive one, and the lighting flat and grey (the lit faces never reach the photo's white). Some contour terracing shows on the lit surface at 8 m.
- Proposed next: a level 3 (the bake costs only 0.26 s) and a varied child radius (0.3..0.6x) and count, then billow erosion at a 20..40 m scale. That makes the detail finer and less regular than spheres alone.
