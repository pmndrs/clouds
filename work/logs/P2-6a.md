# P2.6a: tower2, the P2.4a tower plus a Houdini-style variance stage

- Code: examples/lab/reference-pt/tower2-bake.ts (new) + a `tower2` entry in cards.ts (picker label "G tower + variance (P2.6a probe)"). `tower` and tower-bake.ts unchanged.
- Recipe (one try, no tuning loop):
  - Same nine level-0 placements as `tower` (jitter 120 m, radius x0.9..1.1), so the silhouette stays comparable.
  - Levels 0 and 1 are ellipsoids (axis scales 0.8..1.25, feet 0.7..0.85 tall x 0.95..1.25 wide, random turn about y; iq's ellipsoid bound).
  - Children: radius 0.28..0.62 x parent (skewed small), depth 0.72..0.92 on the parent's ellipsoid surface, 80 % in 2-3 clumps per parent (cluster directions biased up and outward), 20 % free.
  - Level 3: 3..5 lobes of 0.32..0.48 x parent, only on upward-facing level-2 lobes. Lobes per level 9 / 76 / 491 / 1879.
  - Surface: 60 m domain warp, then billow displacement (|value noise|: rounded bulges, sharp creases) 34 / 16 / 7 m at 120 / 48 / 20 m, weighted by up-facing normal x height (smoothstep 60..500 m). Elsewhere an 18 m plain-noise wobble.
  - Density ramp 10 m on the billowed tops, 90 m on undersides and low down; on those soft parts fbm erosion (remap by up to 0.55). Interior x fbm (0.6..1) at 260 m, slight rise with depth. Flat soft base (60 m smoothstep) as before.
- Bake: 593 ms (tower 363 ms in the same session), grid 375 x 336 x 210 at 8 m, 106 MB f32. Occupancy 0.16 (tower 0.21).
