# P2.8a: tower3 at a realistic density, and a soft outer fringe

- Step 1 (no code): tower3 at fit-noon / fit-low / fit-sunward, 640x360, own vite on :5629, --settle 400, converged=1, towerSigma 0.45 (s045/) and 0.08 (s008/). 0.15 not needed: 0.08 is still plainly opaque.
- Step 2: `?towerFringe=1` (off by default; tower3 unchanged without it) in tower3-bake.ts (cards.ts passes it to bakeTower3; card report `fringe`). Outside the pushed body (d > 0): density amp x (1 - d/W)^2, amp ~0.35 (x0.3..1 by a 70 m fbm mask, x0.15 on clearly up-facing upper surfaces), W 60 m on the sides, 100 m low down / underneath, 8 m on clearly up-facing upper surfaces, times 0.35..1.45 by the same fbm, so it is patchy. Grid margin 160 -> 260 m with the fringe on (grid 422 x 325 x 269, about 7.0 M occupied voxels vs 5.8 M; bake ~400 ms).
- Tries: 1st put the fringe before the soft-part erosion, which ate it (barely visible, +4% voxels) and used the tops' crisp weight, which also narrowed the sides to ~30 m (2-3 px at 8 km). 2nd: after the erosion, widths as above (= s008-fringe/). About 1.5 min of rendering in all.
- Sheet: work/captures/P2-8a/sheet.jpg (sheet.py): rows noon / low / sunward; columns 0.45 | 0.08 | 0.08 + fringe | reference (houdini from P2-7b for noon / low, embergen02 from P1-7b for sunward).

## Read
- Density: 0.08 removes most of the grey. Noon goes from grey-beige to white, low's shaded flank from grey-brown to light grey, sunward's body from dark slate to mid grey. It still reads as a solid cumulus: no see-through anywhere, billows keep their shading. A thin pale rim appears in sunward, but it is an even 2-3 px line all round (the same halo as before, brighter), not a silver lining. Recommend 0.08 as the tower default (agent-chosen; not switched here, still a URL override).
- Fringe at 0.08: sunward gets a real bright rim, wider and uneven, strongest along the lower and right edges, weaker at the top, which is the right pattern, closer to embergen02. It is still thinner and more outline-like than embergen02's, whose lit fringe runs into the body. Worse: at noon the side billows on the core go milky and lose definition, a soft airbrushed look; the base is fuller and softer. Tops stay crisp. At fit-low it is close to neutral (slightly softer flank). No uniform halo at noon or low; in sunward the rim follows the whole outline but varies in width.
- Closest to a real cumulus: 0.08 without the fringe for noon / low (crisper billows, white); 0.08 + fringe for sunward. Neither matches the reference yet: houdini's body is whiter and more contrasty at noon, and embergen02's silver lining is broader.
- Proposed next (one at a time): keep the fringe but keep it off the mid-height sides (low / underside / shaded only, side width ~25 m) so noon billows stay crisp, and re-check sunward; the fringe is URL-only (`towerFringe`), it needs a bench switch per controls-not-urls before the owner reviews it.
