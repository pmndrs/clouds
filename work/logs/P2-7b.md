# P2.7b: Houdini cumulus on the card page

- Code: `houdini` (cumulus_4_wispy.vdb) and `houdini1` (cumulus_1_lobes.vdb, not captured) added to cards.ts (CARD_CLOUDS, CARD_DEFS, VDBS) and to the requiredLimits check in main.ts. Loaded exactly as embergen02 (readVdbSource, VDBGridPool, DirectResampler, buildDirectCloud), with three VDBS fields: `repo: true` (fetched from the repo's assets/ via /@fs, not artifacts/research), `widthMeters: 0` (world scale 1, the VDBs are already metres), `base: 0` (lattice base at the ground like `tower`, not embergen's 900 m). Camera as tower2 (8000 m, 150 m). Existing clouds untouched.
- Density scale: sigma_t = 0.08 / m at density 1 (embergen02 uses 0.6 with 4 m voxels at 1.9 km wide; those are a much denser, smaller cloud). The Houdini fog is 0..1 with mean 0.83 over active voxels, so the core is about 0.07-0.08 / m.
- Captures: own vite on :5461, 640x360, --settle 400, converged=1, pathtracer found by the default walk-up (HEAD of ~/Developer/pathtracer, not ad9c01e; git archive was refused in this sandbox; irrelevant to fit frames). houdini and tower2, fit-noon and fit-low, ~1 min. Sheet work/captures/P2-7b/sheet.jpg (houdini left, tower2 right).
- Read: houdini reads clearly as a cumulus: three merged bodies with a tower rising from the middle, a billowy cauliflower surface at several scales, soft shaded undersides and the base. No blockiness, no clipping at the volume bounds. Not too dark or too thin; it is bright and fairly opaque, with a flat, slightly uniform interior shading (little internal shadowing at noon, a bit more at low sun). Differences from tower2: wider and squatter (3 x 2 km, a pile rather than a column), and it sits on the ground plane (base 0), so the horizon cuts it; tower2's grid floats higher, so the frames are not at the same framing. The wispy edge detail is not obvious at this distance. Console: a harmless PT warning about tentative collisions (PT only).
