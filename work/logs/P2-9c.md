# P2.9c: one cloud, one sun, one camera, three renderers (sunward rim)

**Sheet:** `work/captures/P2-9c/sheet.jpg` (sky-matched). Columns: ours (fit-sunward) | our path tracer (pt-sunward, 1024 spp) |
Karma CPU (32 spp, OIDN). Rows: full frame | 3x crop of the rim nearest the sun (about 3 deg away) | 3x crop of the left lobe's
rim (about 15-20 deg from the sun). Companion `sheet_sunmatched.jpg`: the same images scaled by sun irradiance instead (see below).
Script: `work/captures/P2-9c/sheet.py` (prints every number below). Inputs: `fit-sunward.pfm` / `pt-sunward.pfm` (the P2.9b
linear captures, copied in; no re-capture), `card-sunward.exr` (Karma raw) and `card-sunward_oidn.exr` (OIDN, beauty only).

## The change: `karma-cloud.py --frames card-sunward`

- New frame `card-sunward` reads the card page's camera (position, target), `fovY` and sun direction from
  `work/captures/P2-9b/houdini/capture.json` (`--card-capture`), and maps card metres to VDB world. The card resamples the VDB onto a
  lattice whose origin is the NanoVDB world-box min (centre of the lowest active voxel) minus 4 margin texels, then puts that lattice
  at (-nx*12/2, 0, -nz*12/2), y up, no flips. Offset card -> VDB world: **(+48, -36, +66) m** (checked: the computed card min equals
  the page's reported grid min, -1548 / 0 / -906). Camera in VDB world (3017.8, 114.0, 292.8), sun alt 32.49 / az 274.69 (Karma
  azimuth convention), hfov 50.94 from fovY 30 at 16:9, vertical aperture set to the image aspect so no aperture-fit can change the
  framing. Silhouettes line up to about 1 px (rim peaks at the same rows / columns in all three).
- Also new: `--sigma` (0 = sky only, used once to look at the sun disc). The committed rig `houdini/rigs/karma_cloud.hip` is only
  saved for the default P2.9a output folder, so this run did not touch it.
- Renders (Karma CPU, 640x360, Path Traced): **volume limit 512, 32 spp, 69.5 s** (the sheet frame; our path tracer's maxDepth is 512)
  and volume limit 32, 32 spp, 39.7 s (`card-sunward_vl32*`, the P2.9a setting). Plus a 3.6 s sky-only frame and two 320x180 8 spp
  checks (6-7 s each), in scratch. Total rendering about 2.5 minutes.

## Two things that change the reading of P2.9a

1. **The skies are not the same light.** Karma's Physical Sky sun is a normalized DistantLight, intensity 1.234, colour
   (1, 0.752, 0.504): irradiance 0.971 (luminance). Its sky next to the cloud is 0.18-0.20. Ours: sun irradiance 33.05 (card report),
   sky next to the cloud 1.40-1.48. So **sun / sky = 5.1 in Karma, 23 in ours**: Karma's sky is about 4.5x brighter relative to its
   sun. Matching exposure on the sky therefore makes everything sunlit in Karma about 3-4x dimmer than ours, rim and body alike. That
   is a sky-model difference, not a scattering difference. (Karma's camera-visible sun disc is not the light's radiance: integrating
   it gives 0.001, so the irradiance above comes from the light's parameters.)
2. **Volume limit 32 darkens Karma's body.** Same frame at 320x180: volume limit 256 vs 32 brightens the top-lobe body 1.6x and the
   lower body 1.24x; the rim near the sun changes by 0.6%. At 640x360, body / sky goes 0.16 (vl 32) -> 0.24-0.25 (vl 512). P2.9a's
   "core 0.2-0.3x the sky" was partly the bounce cap.

## Plain answers

- **Near the sun:** relative to the nearby sky, ours is *brighter* than Karma's, not dimmer: 16.5x (ours), 20x (our PT), 5x (Karma).
  The gap is the sky (point 1). Per unit of sun light: ours 0.70, our PT 0.85, Karma 0.95. Ours is about 18% under our PT here.
- **Far from the sun (13-18 deg):** 6-7x (ours), 7-8x (PT), 2.1-2.4x (Karma) relative to sky; per unit sun 0.28 / 0.31 / 0.43.
  Karma's extra there is most likely its 4.5x brighter sky. The fall-off with angle is the same in all three.
- **Inward fade:** no, ours does not stop more abruptly. Peak to half-way-to-body is 5-14 px in ours, 4-13 in our PT, 5-11 in Karma,
  and the normalised profiles overlap (A: 1, 0.45, 0.10, 0.06 per 4 px in ours vs 1, 0.49, 0.11, 0.06 in Karma).
- **Body:** at matched sky, ours and our PT are about 0.4x the sky, Karma 0.25x; colour: ours is near-neutral/beige in the core,
  both path tracers are blue-grey (ours has about half the blue tilt of our PT).
- **So:** at the rim our realtime lighting is missing only brightness, about 10-25% against our own path tracer (most at 3-7 deg
  from the sun), with the same width, fade and fall-off with angle. The real look difference is the body's sky-blue: ours is beige
  where both path tracers are blue-grey. Our path tracer agrees with Karma on shape (rim position, width, inward fade, fall-off with
  angle); it differs in sky-relative brightness, and that is explained by the two sky models (sun/sky 23 vs 5) plus, in P2.9a,
  Karma's volume limit of 32. It is not evidence that our physics is wrong. A clean physics check needs the same sky in both
  (e.g. our sky baked to an HDRI for Karma's dome plus our sun irradiance on the distant light); not done here.
