# P1.8a: why the fit model's shaded body is beige where the path trace is blue-grey (houdini, sunward)

**Sheet:** `work/captures/P1-8a/sheet.jpg` (sheet.py). Row 1, houdini sunward: ours today | fitSkySide 1 | fitSkySide 1 + fitGroundAtten 1 ("on") |
our path tracer (P2-9c pt-sunward.pfm). Row 2 (half size): houdini noon off/on, houdini low off/on, tower4 noon off/on, tower4 low off/on.
Row 3: tower4 sunward off/on, houdini body 2x crops off/on. Same exposure 0.35, ACES fit + sRGB everywhere (ours and the PT have the same sky).

## How
- Own vite :5818 (PATHTRACER_DIR = scratchpad pt-ad9c01e), 640x360, converged=1, --settle 400. Per-term frames as capture.mjs evals
  (`gen.py`: setSettings with the other gains at 0, wait for the converged restart, readLinear; P1-4b's method). Term = eval - the all-zero frame.
- `terms.py` splits the body regions (P2-9c's: top-lobe core rows 110-180 cols 280-400, lower body rows 280-350 cols 240-520);
  `resolve.py` does non-negative least squares of (fitMs, fitAmbient, fitGround) on both regions x RGB against the PT, at fitKUp 0.3 / 0.1 / 0.03
  (the sky term re-rendered at each), and checks the rim (pixels where the direct term is > 40 % of the frame).
- Rendering about 5.5 min in all (7 term loads of ~22 s, two view loads of ~90 s). No new path trace. PFMs left in scratch.

## Per-term split, houdini sunward (luminance share of our pixel; colour as B/R)
| region | ours / PT lum | ours B/R, PT B/R | direct | ms (sun, warm) | sky (blue) | ground (warm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| top-lobe core | 0.86 | 1.12, 1.66 | 0 | 0.18 | 0.20 | **0.61** |
| lower body | 1.10 | 1.41, 2.05 | 0 | 0.14 | 0.34 | **0.52** |

- The ground term (fitGround 0.61 x ambientDown, unattenuated, warm: the lower hemisphere includes the sunlit ground) is half or more of the body.
- The sky term is cut by `1 / (1 + 0.3 tauUp)`: 0.28 of the open-sky value in the top-lobe core, 0.57 in the lower body (tauUp about 8 and 2.5 at 0.08 /m).
- The PT body, split into "warm" and "sky" colour, is about 45 % / 55 % sky-blue light; ours is 20-34 %.

## Step 2: can the linear gains do it?
- **At fitKUp 0.3, no.** Best re-solve (fitMs 2.47 held, fitAmbient 0.87, fitGround 0.47): body lum 0.80 / 1.12, B/R 1.33 / 1.74 vs PT 1.66 / 2.05.
  Letting fitMs float only pushes it to 9 and the rim to 1.7x. The two regions need different sky shares (top core 0.70 of open sky, lower body
  0.84) while up-only attenuation gives 0.28 vs 0.57: the **sky term's straight-up depth is the structural limit**.
- With fitKUp lowered to 0.03 it does fit (fitMs 2.47, fitAmbient 0.58, fitGround 0.37: lum 0.90 / 1.06, B/R 1.74 / 1.90, rim unchanged),
  but that is a global near-unattenuated sky: the dense (0.45-0.6 /m) clouds' bases it was fitted on would light up. Not proposed.

## The fix: `fitSkySide` (CloudSettings, Frame.fit5.x, off by default; Review row "sky from sides (P1.8a)" on the card page)
- March only: the ambient's optical depth = mix(tauUp, min(tauUp, tauSide), fitSkySide), tauSide = SDF depth below the surface x local
  extinction x lightDensityScale (one extra cloudDistance read per lit sample when on). The ground term uses the same depth only with the
  existing fitGroundAtten 1. FRAME_UNIFORM_BYTES 608 -> 624. Off = byte-identical behaviour (checked: the off frames match the earlier default frames).
- Houdini sunward, top-lobe core / lower body (PT B/R 1.66 / 2.05):
  - fitSkySide 1: lum 1.12 / 1.24, B/R 1.54 / 1.59 (colour right in the core; too bright, the warm ground is still unattenuated).
  - fitSkySide 1 + fitGroundAtten 1 ("on"): **lum 0.98 / 1.11, B/R 1.69 / 1.71**, rgb/lum core (0.85, 1.00, 1.43) vs PT (0.83, 1.01, 1.38).
  - Re-solve with fitSkySide 1 (ground unattenuated): fitMs 2.47, fitAmbient 0.65, fitGround 0.36 gives lum 0.93 / 1.03, B/R 1.82 / 1.89; so the
    existing gains are fine once the sky can come in from the sides. Rim unchanged in every variant (0.93 of the PT, as before).
- On vs off elsewhere (pixels changed > 1 %, median lum ratio, p90, B/R before -> after):
  houdini noon 0.99 (1.24) 0.96 -> 1.06; houdini low 1.04 (1.56) 0.77 -> 0.91; tower4 noon 1.08 (1.39) 0.98 -> 1.15;
  tower4 low 1.15 (1.87) 0.82 -> 1.01; tower4 sunward 1.21 (1.68) 1.00 -> 1.31.

## Read (from the sheet)
- Houdini sunward: yes, the body now reads blue-grey like the path trace instead of beige, at about the right brightness. The PT still has a bluer
  lower body and a lighter top lobe over it (more contrast between the lobes); ours "on" is a bit flatter.
- Rim: unchanged.
- Noon: little change, slightly cooler shade. Fine.
- Low: the shaded sides lose their dark warm shade (tower4 low most: the brown shaded lower half becomes light blue-grey, p90 x1.87). The colour
  probably moves the right way (P1-6a measured the PT's tower shade at low as B/R 1.25 vs fit 0.72), but the shade depth at low sun may now be too
  light; there is no path trace of houdini or tower4 at low to say. tower4 sunward also loses its dark base and reads flatter. Owner's eye needed.
