# P2.3c tall clouds: continuous turrets (Review P24, hz_topShape=1)

- Code: `hzdTurrets` (shaper.wgsl.ts) is now continuous: each of the 3x3 jittered cells adds a dome h_i * sqrt(sat(1 - (d / rad_i)^2)), rad_i = 0.75 cell x (1 +- 25 %), h_i = 1 - turretDepth r^2; the field is a polynomial smooth max (hzdSmax, k 0.12) of the domes and a floor 1 - turretDepth, capped at 1. Domes reach at most 0.94 cells, so nothing outside the 3x3 contributes. 18 hashes. The P2.3b hard-nearest-cell field is kept, unused, as `hzdTurretsCellsP23b`.
