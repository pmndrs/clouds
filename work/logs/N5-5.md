background = cond[14:17]; blend `(direct*li + es*env)*S + B*es*(1-S)`.

## Surprises

- Nothing disagreed. Behaviours confirmed by the match: x doubling in non-attention stages (10 of them), attention has
  no residual of its own, memory k/v first, scale dim_head^-0.5 = 0.5, reflect pad before the qkv 1x1, skip push after
  block1 and after the attention add, concat order (x, skip).
- ONNX channel order for the cond is the packed 17-channel layout, so `INDICES` apply directly (no re-mapping).
- Weights in the `.tza` are already logical OIHW (the converter handled the channels-last strides).
- Stage "block1/block2" in the dump names are whole ResnetBlocks, matching `downs.N.0` / `downs.N.1`.
