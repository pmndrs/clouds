- Read: B softer (slightly hazier top), C clearly softer (a translucent band along the top), D about unchanged (marginally smoother), E softest (a hazy porous shell, the P2.5d halo). No defined lobes in any; E's lobes read as blur, not as rounded lobes. Nothing sharpened the outline. The P2.5d haze is mostly the lobes (E), with the hardness adding to it.
- Why hardness softens: hard < x below x = cut / (1 - (1 - cut) / gain) (0.17 at 0.12/3, 0.30 at 0.25/6), and most of the visible edge density is below that (it is already lifted by the n3 sharpen pow 0.6 before the shaping); c at those thin outer samples is only 0.6-0.8 (P2.5d), so mix(x, hard, c) does not remove the thin shell, it dims it to about (1 - c) x: the outline stays where it was and the shell turns more translucent over a deeper band. Nothing later undoes it (densityPow 1; veil only below h 0.25).
