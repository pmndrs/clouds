Measures:
- **Stall**: before, a weather change cost **13.2 / 13.3 / 16.4 ms** of bake work in one frame (frame total 15.2-18.6 ms). Now 64 tiles of 32 rows plus one max-mip frame, timestamped every frame (65 of 65). Worst frame per change: **0.48 / 0.51 / 0.37 ms**, mean tile 0.30-0.36 ms, mips 0.08-0.13 ms. All cloud passes on those frames reach at most 3.9-4.4 ms against a steady 2.9-3.4 ms. The full series is in `sky-bake/capture.json` (`bakeMsSeries`); seed 7→8 starts `0.365 0.387 0.409 0.4 0.41 0.459 ...` and ends `... 0.272 0.272 0.078`.
  - In isolation (the generate page) a 32-row tile costs 0.22 ms at worst, a 64-row tile 0.37 ms, and the one-shot map 10.6 ms. Inside a full frame, 64-row tiles reached 1.1 ms, so the default is 32 rows. The new window takes over 65 frames (about 1.1 s at 60 fps) after the change.
- **Swap**: new weather and shaping switch together, in one frame. The swap no longer resets the history, which used to mean a full-resolution trace frame, about +2.3 ms. The new window now replaces the history over one 16-frame Bayer cycle, with fresh-sample blend 1, so the swap adds no trace cost: 0 resets. A swap that also changes the model, such as the shaping or a moved window, still cuts.
- **Shadow map**: the layer exposes only the live texture, so the map sees a new weather texture only on the swap. First stale frame = swap frame on all three changes (64 = 64); never stale while tiles land.
- **Generator 512² map**: 0.51 ms one-shot by timestamp, inside the 1 ms frame budget. P9-B's 1.4 ms was submit wall time. Tiled at 128 rows it is 0.15 ms per tile.
- Tiled output equals the one-shot map: 0 of 4.19 M texels differ, and 0 of 262 k at 512².
- **Parity**: the layer now reads the weather at `xz - lean·(y - base)`, the same lean as the generator (shaders.ts `weatherAt`). It also includes the lean term in the coverage-gradient y component, and in the empty-space skip (the texel exit is computed in the leaned frame). The billow uses `K = A·b·topW·w.b` as suggested; with `storm` B is the base height, so the weight is 1 there (`shapeB.y` flag). Shear goes to `wOrigin.zw`, so the dome and the shadow map get it too.
  - wallEastTop: before, clouds stop at the wall in straight vertical edges, e.g. the lobe at y 450-540 of the lower crop and the right part at y 120-180. After, the outlines continue across the wall.
  - horizon: same layout; small distant puffs are less spiky.
- **Tests**:
  - Undershoot allowance is now per voxel: `3·voxel/64` (three separable EDT passes, each floors to 1/64 voxel) plus `|d|·(2^-10 + 2^-11)` (the f16 shrink and rounding). Worst 0.78 of the allowance, at 261 m.
  - World anchor: SDF compared only where the nearer of the two distances plus 2 voxels lies inside the overlap interior. That is 928 k and 639 k voxels; max |dA - dB| is 0.000 m. NVDF interior check unchanged (max 0).
Open issues:

It is identical in `walls-before/` and stays at `sky_resolutionScale=1`. So it is not the layer and not lean or B. Likely the light grid ends at the box face, which lights the face columns from the side. It needs a runtime/light-grid owner.

- A static box that moves keeps the old window, with its old uv origin, until the new one lands (65 frames). Streaming already uses `weatherOrigin [0, 0]`, where the old window stays world-correct.
- The first window at startup also takes 65 frames before the layer appears. cloudscape-gen's ready gate (layer frames ≥ 20) already covers this.
