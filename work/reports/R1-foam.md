## Answer: both, about evenly, and they are separable
1. **Rendering (surface softness, "shaving foam").** At `high` the far pass traces at 0.41 scale with 4-frame temporal; at the C1 distances (1.6-3.2 km) every edge and the whole up-rez detail are smeared over 20-30 px. Edge crispness (`r1_metrics.py`, |grad| on the silhouette / contrast): C1 c94 0.068, c39 0.060, c55 0.052; owner Frog photo 0.20. Same data at `p.farScale=1&p.temporalFar=1` 0.144, `quality=ultra` 0.124, the A-render set (ultra + `mipDistanceScale 0.001` + `deepAttenuation 0.15`) 0.178 / 0.25 / 0.104. The billows and fine cauliflower only appear once the far pass resolves them (`p1-render-ref.jpg`, `p3-hc1-render2.jpg`); a lower noise MIP alone does nothing at 0.41 scale. Cost (marchFar, c94 photo): 1.8 ms high, 3.9 ultra, 5.2 ultra+mip 0.001, 6.4 far1, 12.5 far1+mip 0.0005.
2. **Form (cohesion, "blobs of blobs").** Confirms the coordinator's reading: EmberGen/Pyro inputs are already puffy at 8-20 m, so every puff gets its own 66 m pack-fit profile ramp; the crevices stay low-profile, erode to nothing and show sky or blue shade between lobes. C94 mean profile is only 0.27. Low-passing the envelope before SDF/profile (morphological closing of the iso mask with a ball, `r1_nvdf.py --close R`) fixes it: lobe-scale/body-scale luminance energy ("mid/low") 0.088 → 0.055 (R 32 m), 0.043 (64), 0.034 (128), Frog photo 0.073; one body with a shaded belly instead of a spatter (`p3-close.jpg`, `c39-a.jpg`). Gaussian density blur (`--env-blur 24/48`) binds as well but rounds the silhouette and pits at 48 (`p3-blur.jpg`); blurring only the SDF that drives the profile (`--sdf-blur 24`) barely helps. R 32 keeps most of the silhouette; 64+ loses satellites.
   - Side effect found: the pack detail-type fit makes deep voxels wispier (`depthShift 0.25`); a closed body is deep everywhere, goes ~all wispy (median type 2/255) and the uncapped wispy erosion carves round pits ("sponge", `cand2-c39.jpg`). `--depth-shift 0` (height-only type) reduces it; some pits remain in c39's wide concavities.
   - The "connective fill" option (`--fill`) exists as a labelled comparison but was not needed or run.
3. **Settings-only levers (phase 1, c94, `p1-*.jpg`).** None changes the blob structure; they move brightness and contrast. densityScale is the strongest: 0.15 smooth cotton (mid/low 0.050, lit:belly 1.15), 0.6-1.2 harder separate lobes (0.13-0.14). lightDensityScale 0.6/3, msStrength 0.5/3, ambientStrength 0.4/2, glowFront 0, deepScatter 0, detailBias ±0.5: mid/low within 0.07-0.11, look nearly unchanged. `lightingModel=octave` changes the image by 0.27/255 mean: the lighting model is not the cause. Shade colour: our bellies are sky-blue; `deepAttenuation 0.15` (multiple-scattering floor survives depth) greys them (fixed patch B/R 1.54 → 1.25; `p1b-shade.jpg`); `deepScatter 3` over-brightens to cotton.
4. **Sharpening pow / S^4 (phase 2).** Not exposed as settings; reached through the density-scale channel. The pack scale fit (0.04 → 0.75 with height, × (0.5 + 0.5 density/p99)) gives S' = S^4 ≈ 0.06, so the exponent sits at its softest (≈0.32) and lifts every eroded fringe into haze. Height trend rescaled to top 1 (`--scale hconst:1`) → more relief and darker cores (`p2-scale.jpg`); on un-closed data it also hardens the blobs (mid/low 0.118), so it only helps together with the closed envelope. `const:1` too dark/hard. Profile ramp D 24/40/120 m (`p2-ramp.jpg`): modest; 120 softer and slightly more cohesive (0.078), 24 harder lobes.
5. **Parkouring (Guerrilla's own asset) still reads soft at C1 settings.** The form path does not apply (its fields are already a smooth envelope); the render set doubles its edge crispness (0.052 → 0.104), and that is the part of its "too softened" verdict that is ours.
