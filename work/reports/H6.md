## Steps (WIP commits 8eedea1, fb2304d, 22bf43d, f38544f, a58d08f, 684a8f3)
1. **Connective material.** Below the coverage threshold a thin veil continues each body (`veilWidth` in base x gradient units, `veilDensity`, only below `veilHeight`, streaked by the curl-displaced detail fBM^`veilWisp`): the n3 s162 "skirt" as a formula. It gives the low-density flow between bodies (sheet s1). Tried and left off: N17 p.56-57 Perlin-Worley footprint (`ndf.footprint`; islands, i.e. *more* isolated, and it halves coverage) and gp7 p.107 "small clouds lighter" moved after the erosion (`coverageDensityPow`; invisible at our extinction).
2. **Surface detail.** Our remap divides by coverage, so GP7's 0.2 erosion only nibbled a thin edge band (toon look); 0.35-0.5 in GP7's place turns bodies into popcorn (H1's foam). HFW p.15-17's order (detail erodes the noise *before* the coverage remap) keeps bodies intact and puts billows on their flanks: `erosionOrder 'hfw'`, strength 0.3, wispy switch at h 0.2 (hag eq 14), detail tile 400 -> 300 m (finer crust).
3. **Terraces.** Not mainly the summed gradients: pure cumulus and `points` still terraced at stage 1. Cause: the Nutshell top fall is 15 % of the column, at type-dependent heights, so every body gets a flat ceiling and neighbours stack as ledges. `topRound 0.7` moves the fall's start toward the rise (hag eq 6 falls over 0.2 wh..wh) + smoothstep ramps (`profileSmooth`).
4. **Flat-cut tops.** From above, H5's tables are gone after step 3; `heightFromCoverage 0.4` (ref 0.4, hag p.13 / ne22 per-column top) with NDF maxHeight 0.9 +- 0.2 varies tops per cloud (high view `step4/high-*`).
5. **Coverage / horizon.** Ring coverage 0.4 -> 0.3, ring fade from 50 % of ringEnd; veil off beyond `veilDistance`. Tried NDF coverageHi 0.9: emptied the overhead sky (upper-frame cover 0.15 vs Fig 4.16 0.54), reverted.
