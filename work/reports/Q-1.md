Diagnosis (A/B captures, Disney `edge`, time 15):
- H4 (march resolution): `stepScale` 0.4-0.5 (step floor 1.2-1.5 m + 0.1 sqrt t) changed nothing visible and cost +50% far march. `mipDistanceScale` 0.001 only added silhouette rags at 4.7x the far cost. Not the cause.
- H2 (noise amplitude): a deeper erosion (`detailBias`) adds clefts but on its own the body stays a smooth white mass.
- H1 + H3 are the cause, together. Direct-light-only renders (`msStrength=deepScatter=ambientStrength=glowBoost=0`) show that the density field already has crisp cauliflower relief and Beer-Lambert shadows it sharply. The full render buries it because the glow terms (ms, deep octaves, ambient) carry most of the lit radiance and vary only with the smooth profile and a 0.25-relaxed, 8-48 m-sampled tau, so a lobe's shadow side glows as much as its lit side. The direct term alone cannot carry the look (phase at 90 deg is 0.06, and its clefts go black).
Fix:
- `microDist` (4 m): one full-density sample toward the sun from every lit step, never reused, fetched only within 40 m of the SDF surface (deeper, the sample's own density stands in). Its optical depth joins the direct tau; the first 16 m sample is shortened by it so nothing is counted twice.
- `microShadow` (1): the glow terms (ms, deep) are multiplied by `exp(-microShadow * tau(0..16 m))`. Rationale: multiply-scattered light diffuses metres in dense cloud, not the 48 m the relaxed term implies, so the shadow side of a lobe glows less. The relaxed grid-fed tau stays, so deep interiors keep the inner glow and backlit pockets.
- `detailBias` (0.5): noise * 1.5 before the erosion, so billows cut further into the profile shell (0 = Nubis).
- `stepScale` (1): diagnostic knob, left at the P3-E tuning.
- All colours still sky-derived; nothing hard-coded. Nubis-neutral is `0, 0, 0, 1`.

Open issues / what remains vs offline (honest):
- Jitter dither is more visible in the deep shadow pockets now that neighbouring samples differ more (1:1 crops, cumulus and stormbird); fine at display scale, a TAA/temporal filter would clean it. `microShadow` 2 is crisper but sparkles; 1 is the default.
- The glow occlusion is a heuristic (near-field tau as a proxy for the diffusion length), not an ms solution; the lit/shadow contrast of Hyperion's lobes and its 5-20 m lobe sizes are still beyond an 8 m voxel asset with 100 m-tile noise. Hyperion's flat dark base and wispy tail are asset/profile matters (P4-G), not lighting.
- The ambient term still has no crevice occlusion (profile-based only); clefts get their darkness from the glow terms. A cheap AO from the micro sample could follow.
