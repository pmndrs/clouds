# T4 Layered sunset scene
Status: ready for review
Changed: new `examples/lab/sunset/` (index.html, main.ts), its line in `vite.config.ts`, a `sunset` card in `examples/lab/gallery/cards.json` plus its thumb `gallery/thumbs/sunset.webp` (from `scripts/gallery-thumbs.mjs`), and a new `layered` preset in `generate/presets.ts` (existing presets untouched).
Scene: the TALL 6.1 km x 2 km field. The low layer is stratocumulus bands and lumps (species 0.22–0.3, bands along the wind, N–S). Altocumulus cells sit at 1.8–2.0 km. The camera stands on the ground at the field's east side looking west, so the bands lie across the sun's arc (deck-scenes §2.6). Bookmarks: `golden` +4°, `sunset` +1°, `afterglow` −1°, `side` (+1°, sun off to the left, the DSC00415 view) and `zenith` (−1°, looking 38° up). Each bookmark has its own exposure, as a camera would. Seed 11 by default; `?seed=5` gives a near-overcast deck with one bright gap. The usual `?c_ ?sky_ ?w_ ?sunel ?sunaz ?lookaz ?pitch ?fov ?exposure` overrides work.
Page tuning: `bodySunFloor` 0.4 (C1 default 0.05). See gap 1 below; `ab-bodyfloor.jpg` shows default, 0.4 and 1.

Measures. Method: cloud pixels from the render minus a clear-sky render, sky above 5°, ACES inverted for the luminance ratio. The low and alto layers are split with low-only and alto-only renders. Photo targets come from `photo-species-specs` §11.

- Warm area: `sunset` 19% (target 15–25%). `side`: alto 23% against low 2% (thin cells peach, thick deck mauve, as in DSC00415). `afterglow`: 8% in total; the alto cells are pink-lit from below (median #B07C91) and the low cloud is mauve-slate.
- Lit:shadow p90/p10: `side` 5.6 (target 4–6); `golden` 4.7; `sunset` 3.3; `afterglow` 2.6. The last two are under target (gap 5).
- Cost at 2560×1440, high: frame p50 and p95 16.7 ms (60 fps vsync) on all four views tested. `cloudsTotal` is 6.2–6.8 ms (sky layer 2.1–2.2, light grid 0.8–1.0, dome 0.5–0.9, marchFar 0.6–0.8). That is over the 3.5 ms tier budget, which is P9-C3's problem.
Matches (photo by photo):
1. High cloud stays pink after the low cloud goes dark (owner's note; afterglow vs `A_20220815`/`A_20220827`): the earth shadow per altitude puts the low layer in shade while the alto stays lit from below.
2. Dense bodies are sky-lit mauve/slate and not cream (owner's note; `A_20220827`, `A_20220425`).
3. Thin cells peach over a slate deck (`side` vs `A_20220710-201409_DSC00415`).
4. The twilight ramp turns purple and pink at −1°, with a warm haze band at the horizon in which the far clouds fade.
Gaps → proposed engine tasks:
1. **Depth-keyed body handoff** (lighting/march/sky-layer). C1 sets the sun's share of the ms body by sun elevation alone. At low sun, thin alto cells therefore lose their glow and are only rim-lit (default 0.05: sunset warm area 9%, alto 5%). Key the floor on the optical depth toward the sun: thin cloud keeps the sun body, dense cloud goes sky-lit. Then drop this page's 0.4.
2. **Sun-side silhouettes** (C2 carry-over). Toward a +1° sun the far bands read pale and hazed, not dark with gold rims (`A_20221016`). There is no visible sun disc or glare through gaps and no crepuscular rays. Proposal: forward-lobe/rim lighting on the far field, the sun disc composited through cloud transmittance, and god rays (P10).
3. **Altocumulus look** (generate/sky-layer). Cells render as separate round puffs, like small cumulus. The photos show flat, flocculent cells merging into a field. The layer is capped by the 2 km volume, while real alto is at 3–6 km. Proposal: a sky-layer-only alto at 3–5 km with a flat, optically thin profile. Also, `alto.cover` scales density but not patch area; this page needs `patches` 2.5 to fill the sky.
4. **No cirrus layer**: the pink streaks of `A_20220815` are missing (the existing cirrus task).
5. **Bands**: the cluster fBm is isotropic, and only the per-cell Sc elongation follows the wind, so long sheared bands across the whole sky (SY s67) are rare. Proposal: a wind-anisotropic, sheared cluster field ("type map with directional stretch", deck-scenes T4). Low contrast at sunset and afterglow (2.6–3.3) may improve together with gap 2.
6. Quarter-resolution sky-layer speckle in the far band near the horizon at sunset (C2 stipple carry-over).
Open issues: the owner's sign-off is still pending. The ground is a flat plane with no tree line, so the photos' dark foreground is missing (scene dressing, not engine).

## Orchestrator review
Committed as work in progress; it does not pass the bar yet. The colour family matches: mauve and slate bodies, pink high cells in the afterglow, a purple twilight. But next to the owner's photos (`work/captures/scratch/T4/side-by-side-final.jpg`, local):
1. **Composition and structure.** The photos are dominated by large connected decks and bands with gold-lit gaps. Ours is scattered small puffs with a few blobs.
2. **Silhouettes.** Ours are smooth "potato" blobs. The photos have textured, torn and cauliflower edges, and bands with ragged undersides.
3. **Toward the sun.** The photos show dark masses with bright gold rims, glare through gaps and crepuscular rays. Ours shows pale, hazy bands and no rays.
4. **No cirrus** (A_20220815 pink streaks).
Items 1–3 feed Q2 (look parity), 3 also feeds the god-ray work (P10 follow-up), and 4 feeds 9-E. The `bodySunFloor` page override (0.4) should become engine behaviour: body light keyed to thickness toward the sun (agent's gap 1).
