# H3 Dark smudges on the flagship ground view (bug)
Files touched (declared before editing): `packages/runtime/src/shaders/march.wgsl.ts` (ambient grid tap), `packages/runtime/src/shaders/lighting.wgsl.ts` (ambient falloff), `packages/runtime/src/runtime.ts` (one doc comment only), `work/reports/H3.md`, `work/captures/H3/`, `work/BOARD.md` (H3 line).

Status: ready for review
Changed:
- `lighting.wgsl.ts`: the directional ambient's sky attenuation goes from `exp(-tauUp)` to the diffuse (two-stream) falloff `1 / (1 + tauUp)`. The floor and the `ambientExtinction` default (0.08) are unchanged.
- `march.wgsl.ts`: the ambient's up sum is read from the light grid at the sample (`lightGridSampleClamped(ps).y`), not at the sun tap 48 m toward the sun.

Cause (bisected on the ground shot, sheets `bisect-terms.jpg`, `bisect-settings.jpg`):
- With `lightDebug` 1–5, the blotches are sharpest in the **ambient** term (4) and also show in ms (2). With `s.ambientExtinction=0` they are gone completely. `temporalNear/Far=1`, `microShadow=0` and `glowBoost/glowFront=0` leave them in place, so temporal history, billow occlusion and the inner-glow/dark-edge terms are ruled out. `densityScale=1` makes them worse and `lightDensityScale=0.6` weakens them, so they scale with grid optical depth.
- The asset: Parkouring's density-scale channel (B) is about 1 in two compact cores and about 0.24 elsewhere. Column sums of profile × B (density·m) are: median 0.65, p90 24, p99 96, max 140. The two dense columns, one under the left lobe and one in the centre, match the two blotches.
- Mechanism: `above = mix(floor, 1, exp(-0.0288 · S_up))` stays near 1 for nearly every column and drops to the floor (0.165) only over those cores. The up sum is a 16 m trilinear field over a smooth 8 m NVDF, and ambient has no surface-scale term, so each core shows on the lit base as a round, soft dark disc. Seen from below with a 60° sun, ambient is most of the light on the base, so the discs read as smudges.
- A second, smaller error: the ambient read the up sum at the sun tap (`ps + sun·48 m`, about 42 m up and 24 m sideways at 14:00). That tap lands inside the neighbouring tower or core, so the darkening was placed by the wrong column.
- Physically, sky light reaches a base by multiple scattering. It falls off about as 1/(1 + 0.75(1−g)τ), not exp(−τ). This is the same law the X3 octave model's diffusion tail uses.

- Attribution: changing the falloff alone removes most of the blotch. Moving the tap alone spreads it into broader underside shading but keeps it dark. Both together give the best result (`bisect-settings.jpg`, lawOnly and tapOnly).

Measures: blotches gone or faint on ground (pass, by eye). Real self-shadowing on edge and above is kept (pass). Cost of the extra trilinear grid fetch per lit step: the march's GPU median is within run-to-run noise. Two A/B pairs: old tap 0.71–1.95 ms vs new tap 1.75–2.02 ms on the same shots, so at most about 0.2 ms.

Open issues:
- The falloff change is in the shared lighting model, so it also brightens thick-base ambient in the sky layer, the dome and the procedural `nubis` lighting (H1 trace, file untouched). H1's captures taken before this commit will differ slightly in base brightness.
- The dome (`dome.wgsl.ts:197`) and the sky layer still read the up sum at their own sun-offset taps (reflections and distant clouds only); not changed.
- The left lobe keeps a soft, detail-free grey because the ambient term has no billow-scale occlusion. A follow-up could modulate ambient by a short upward in-line occlusion, the way `microOcc` modulates the glow terms.
