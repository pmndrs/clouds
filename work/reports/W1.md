## Step 1: Nubis 2017 map recipe (`ndf.recipe`, now `'n17'`)
**What it does.** Coverage is gate × footprint (n17 p.54–57):
- **Gate:** one low-frequency fBM, 3 cells per 16 km tile, put through `smoothstep(gateLo, gateHi)`. It is open over about 60 % of the map, which gives clear-sky regions and clusters.
- **Footprint:** the 4-octave Perlin fBM, mapped `footLo..footHi`.
  - `footprint` 0 = Perlin, connected (the default).
  - `footprint` 1 = Perlin-Worley islands. This now *dilates*: `cov + islandGain·(w − 0.5)`. H6's carve halved the coverage.
- **Type:** the low-frequency type field, sharpened into areas by `typeContrast`.
- **Heights:** unchanged.
- **Coverage scale:** the ranges keep H6's percentiles (p50/p90/p99 0.30/0.52/0.67 against H6's 0.35/0.50/0.65), so the shaper sees the same scale.

**Defaults, all ours.** No paper gives these values.
- `recipe 'n17'` (**this is the default change**; `'h6'` brings H6 back)
- `gateCells 3`, `gateLo −0.2`, `gateHi 0.05`
- `footLo −0.4`, `footHi 0.6`
- `islandGain 1`, `typeContrast 2`

**Sheets:**
- `artifacts/research/w1/step1-n17-recipe.jpg`: n17 p55, p56, p57 and p58, and hzd15 s40, above three rows (A = H6, B = n17 Perlin, C = n17 Perlin-Worley). Each row shows the map, ground, top, world and high.
- Ours only: `work/captures/W1/step1/sheet-ours.jpg`
- Sets: `base/` (H6), `step1/`, `step1-pw/`, and the `maps/` statistics.

**What matches the papers.**
- The top and world views now show what p55/p56 describe: open regions holding clusters, separated by wide clear lanes. H6 is one even field of bodies.
- From the ground, B gives a large connected mass overhead plus separate rows of cumulus, the composition of p57.
- C gives more isolated bodies with thin links, as in p58.
- Measured clusters: B has fewer and larger ones (the largest is 72 % of the covered area, against 49 % in H6). C has 14 smaller ones, the largest 18 %.

**What doesn't match.**
- The papers' maps are mostly saturated (coverage near 1). Ours stay on our shaper's coverage scale. This is deliberate, since H5 showed that HZD's 0.8 regime does not suit our remap.
- Type areas barely show in the render. `typeMin 0` was tried and changes little, because the curves blend reads 0.4–1 much like 0–1. A stratus-to-cumulus transect is L2's job.
- The 16 km tile repeat can be seen in far rows from 9 km. A 32 km tile at 1024² with doubled cell counts looks equivalent: `hzn_tileMeters=32000&hzn_resolution=1024&hzn_gateCells=6&hzn_coverageCells=18&hzn_typeCells=4`. It is not the default.

**Cost.**
- The map is generated once, in under 1 ms.
- `skyLayer` is unchanged or slightly lower. Interleaved reruns on a loaded GPU gave H6 15.3–16.8 ms and n17 12.2–15.0 ms. Absolute numbers are inflated by other GPU work on the machine.

## Step 2: authored maps (`ndf.image`, `ndf.heightImage`)
**What it does.**
- **Input format:**
  - `image` is an RGBA PNG URL holding R = coverage, G = top type, B = bottom type. A is ignored.
  - `heightImage` (optional) holds R = min height and G = max height.
- **Decoding:** images are decoded without colour conversion or premultiplication, and resampled into the NDF with bilinear filtering that wraps.
- **Placement:** the image centre is the world origin. Up is north (the default view direction) and right is east. The image spans `tileMeters`. Layers without an image keep the generated fields.
- **Loading:** `proc.load({hzd:true})` waits for the image. Changing the URL later regenerates the map and then paints over it.
- **Compare page:** works without changes, e.g. `?hzn_image=/clouds/ndf/boss-fight.png&hzn_tileMeters=24000`.

**Example maps.** Our own drawings in `assets/clouds/ndf/`, documented in its README:

| Map | Recreates |
| --- | --- |
| `desert-morning` | stratus mottle with holes (n17 p64 / nut s17) |
| `desert-afternoon` + height | scattered fair-weather cumulus (n17 p65) |
| `alpine-congestus` + height | isolated tall columns and a stratus band (n17 p66) |
| `boss-fight` | dense mixed map, a stratocumulus-street wedge, an opening ahead toward the sun, painted holes (n17 p68 / nut s19) |
| `cutscene-gradient` | coverage-against-type gradient (hzd15 s47) |

**Sheets:**
- `artifacts/research/w1/step2-authored-maps.jpg`: each paper frame beside our map, ground, sunset, above and top.
- Ours only: `work/captures/W1/step2/sheet-ours.jpg`

**What matches the papers.**
- Each map reads as its intent: sparse afternoon cumulus, few isolated columns, a dense sky with a hole toward the sun, coverage rising toward the view.
- Painting works as the papers describe: painting black removes cloud.

**What doesn't match.**
- **Plateaus become slabs.** Painted plateaus of coverage turn into flat-topped slabs or columns. Maps must be soft gradients; the first afternoon and alpine drafts showed this, and the README says it.
- **Stratus renders as broken cloudlets.** p64's thin pink veil is a shaper and lighting matter, not a map one.
- **The ring overrides painted maps far out.** HZD's horizon ring (15–35 km) still replaces painted maps at distance, as HZD did. `hz_ringStart` can move it.

**Cost:** nothing per frame. The decode and resample happen once.

## Step 3: map-advection policy (`hzd.ndfMotion`)
**What it does.**
- **The five policies:**
  - `'drift'`: the map moves at `ndfDrift` and both noises scroll freely. This is the default, unchanged (owner, 2026-10-10).
  - `'static'`: Nubis. The map stays still and the noise flows through it.
  - `'rigid'`: Häggström. The map, shape and detail are all anchored and the upward bias is 0: the clouds are carried whole.
  - `'shape'`: the map and shape are anchored and the detail is free (`detailSpeed 0` makes the detail stay in the world).
  - `'detail'`: the map and detail are anchored and the shape is free (the detail rides with the cloud while the forms evolve).
- **Anchoring:** an "anchored" noise moves with the map. This is the SD7 volume-local anchor; every shaper lookup goes through `qShape`, `qDetail` or `ndfSample`, so anchoring is exact.
- **Implementation:** done entirely in the `hzSpeed` and `hzWind.w` packing. No shader change, no cost.

**Clips:** `work/captures/W1/step3/{ground,high}-<policy>/clip.mp4`, with strips in `sheet-ground.jpg` and `sheet-high.jpg`.
- The wind runs at `c_animSpeed=100` (335 m/s), and frames are spaced by wall-clock time.
- `rigid` translates the clouds unchanged. `static` keeps the forms in place while they change. `drift` and `detail` move and reshape them. `shape` is close to `rigid`.
- The near clouds smear in these clips. That comes from temporal reprojection at the exaggerated wind speed, not from the policies.

**Not judged.** Stills cannot settle this. It needs the owner's time-lapses at real speed.

**Known limit.** In the split modes the anchored noise still rises by the upward bias. The bias is one term in `shaper.wgsl.ts`, which belongs to the forms agent.
