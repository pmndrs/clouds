Design notes:
- `GpuTimer` (@clouds/runtime, no three imports): `beginFrame()`, `passTimestampWrites(name)`, `endFrame(encoder)`, `averages()`, `last()`. Ring of 3 readback buffers; `mapAsync` is started at the next `beginFrame()` (so the caller must submit the encoder first); results lag 2-3 frames; no-ops without `timestamp-query`. The page creates one encoder per frame, hands it to the cloud hook, then `endFrame` + submit.
- P1 hook: `examples/lab/flagship/cloud-hook.ts`, `cloudPass.current(ctx)` is called per frame after `sky.update()` and before three renders; synchronous, record-only. ctx has renderer, device, camera, scene, sky, timer, encoder, quality, renderSize, time, frame.
- Placeholder volume in `bookmarks.ts`: `CLOUD_REGION` 4096x512x4096 m centred at y=1750, `CLOUD_BOX` 800 m wide, base 1500 m. P1 replaces these constants. `backlit` places the camera opposite the sun using `sky.sunAzimuth` (north set to -Z, so east = +X).
- Sky: exposure left at the library default (40), ACES tone mapping with exposure 1 gives a sane sky; `sky.createSun({physical:true})` lights the ground (sun colour from the sky LUTs); ground albedo 0x34461f. Ground plane is 2000 km, follows the camera in x/z; camera far 1500 km, near scales with altitude (0.5 m at the ground to 200 m high up), FOV 70.
- Controls: orbit (default) or fly (F key / button; drag to look, WASD, Q/E down/up, Shift x5, wheel scales speed 1 m/s to 100 km/s). Panel: time, render scale, quality (low/medium/high, passed to the hook only for now), animate, bookmarks. URL: bookmark, time, scale, quality, animateTime.
Open issues:
- With animateTime=1 the "scene" GPU entry rises to about 10-11 ms. The sky re-bake and IBL compute run inside three's render timestamp window when the sky changes every frame; it is not a frame-time problem here, but P1 budgets should read `scene` with animateTime off, or subtract it.
- Console shows "Multiple instances of Three.js being imported" (OrbitControls pulls `three`, everything else `three/webgpu`). Harmless warning, the harness does not fail on it.
- The capture settles 90 frames per shot, so `frameMs` in capture.json is a rolling window over the last 240 frames, not per-shot.
- Ground is still fairly pale under the physical sun; adjust the albedo when the cloud shadow lands (P1-07).
