# P4-C GPU stamping of authored clouds into a cloudscape
Status: ready for review
Changed: new `packages/runtime/src/generate/stamp.ts` (WGSL inline, as in the other generate files); `generate/index.ts` (+2 export lines: one value export, one type export); `examples/lab/runtime-tests/stamp/main.ts` (page). Nothing else edited. No three imports in the runtime part.
API (all in `@clouds/runtime`):
- `stampCloud(device, encoder, target, asset, { position, yaw, scale, mode: 'max'|'replace'|'cutout', densityScale?, detailType?, clearMargin?, timestamps? })`. It takes a `device` because an encoder cannot give one. `target` is a generator volume or `createEmptyCloudVolume(...)`; `asset` is a `uploadCloudVolume` result. `position` is the asset's box centre in target-local metres (x, y-up, z); `yaw` is three.js `rotation.y`. Each target voxel in the transformed box is mapped back into the asset and sampled trilinearly; only the stamp's AABB is dispatched.
- `createSdfRebuilder(device, target, { sliceDepth, rounds, scratch, ... })` has the same `generate(encoder, maxJobs)` shape as the generator. It makes occupancy seeds from NVDF profile > 0, runs the generator's own `JFA_WGSL` and `SDF_WGSL`, and writes `target.sdf`.
- `createEmptyCloudVolume(device, dims, voxelSize)`.
- Modes: `max` takes all channels from whichever of target and asset has the larger profile. `replace` writes the asset where it has density and empties the target within `clearMargin` metres (default 48) of the asset's surface, using the asset SDF. `cutout` empties the target wherever the asset has density.
- Stamps are applied in encoding order. Many stamps, then one SDF rebuild.

Measures:
- SDF conservativeness vs CPU EDT of the stamped NVDF mask (cpu-edt.ts, all 14.2 M voxels): max overshoot -4.0 m (never over), 0 overshooting voxels, 0 sign flips, max undershoot 8.9 m (the 1-voxel margin). Inside the stamped boxes plus 12 voxels (5.7 M voxels) the same. Empty target with 4 stamps (192x192x80): same. PASS. The sliced rebuild (528 jobs of 4 z) is bit-identical to one shot.
- Stamps match a CPU inverse-transform trilinear composite (max, max+detailType, replace+densityScale+halo, cutout, 4 stamps in one encoder, empty target): R within 2/255 for 99.9994% of texels; G/B within 2/255 for 100% of cloud voxels; occupied voxels differ by -0.85% (GPU lower) at wispy 1/255 voxels, and a few texels differ by up to 181/255 R at the replace-halo or cutout threshold. I accepted 1%.
- Per-stamp GPU cost (timestamp of the stamp compute pass; the NVDF copy before it is not in this number): Parkouring 0.64 M voxels 0.023 ms, Stormbird 1.30 M 0.029 ms, houdini 2.22 M 0.053-0.081 ms, tunnel cutout 0.18 M 0.008 ms. Wall time of encode, submit and wait: 0.4-1.1 ms (first one 11-14 ms, pipeline creation). A stamp is effectively free.
- SDF rebuild, whole 384x384x96 volume (14 M voxels), `rounds: 2`: 67-68 ms GPU one-shot (jfa3d 66.5, seed 0.8, sdf 0.4); sliced into 528 jobs: 32 ms total, 0.06 ms average, 0.33 ms worst per job (I did not find out why sliced is faster than one-shot; treat 32-68 ms as the range). Generation of the cumulus region: 41-46 ms. The SDF rebuild is the real per-edit cost and must be amortized (about 4 jobs a frame finishes in 130 frames).

Open issues:
- **JFA accuracy.** With the generator's default (one full jump-flood round + 2 refine steps) the rebuilt SDF overshot the CPU EDT by up to 6.7 m (0.8 voxel past the 1-voxel margin, 388 voxels, all about 40 voxels from the nearest cloud) on this sparse stamped field. The rebuilder therefore defaults to `rounds: 2` (two full rounds + refine); that gives max overshoot -4 m on all three tests. It doubles the JFA cost (about 67 ms instead of about 35 ms). The generator itself (`volume-generator.ts`, not mine) still uses one round: P3-E's large volumes and any sparse field may overshoot far from clouds. Worth a check by whoever owns it.
- The SDF is rebuilt for the whole volume, not an affected sub-box. A sub-box is unsafe for additions: any new cloud lowers distances arbitrarily far away, and boxed JFA would overshoot near the box edge unless it is clamped by the face distance. Amortizing (`sliceDepth`) is the supported cost control. The previous SDF stays in the texture until the final copies, so it is stale (too large near new clouds) during the rebuild: render with `done` only, or accept briefly wrong shadow-march distances after an addition.
- The NVDF of the target is read via a copy of the stamp's box (`copyTextureToTexture` into a per-target scratch texture), because rgba8unorm storage textures are write-only on core WebGPU. The scratch is a full-size texture per target (67 MB at 512x512x64).
- `stampCloud` wraps `target.destroy` to free its scratch and uniform buffer.
- `shaders.ts` is imported read-only (`JFA_WGSL`, `SDF_WGSL`) and the rebuilder relies on the first 9 words of its params struct (dims, step, zStart, zCount, margin, maxDist, voxelSize). P3-C2 is editing that file; if it changes that block the rebuilder breaks.
- The page uses a 96-slice region so the 1.5x houdini cloud (730 m) fits; the cumulus preset height fractions are scaled by 64/96 to keep the same metres. The weather ramp is sparser than the stock preset (coverage 0.66/0.98) so stamps are visible. A second, transient capture failure during the work came from other agents' half-edited `sky-layer.ts` ("cloud sky resolve" needs 6 storage textures, limit 4); a rerun passed.
- `tunnelSide` bookmark is poor in the cumulus capture; the cumulus clouds block it.
- Not done: soft (non-binary) cutout edges, stamping a non-uniform scale, and per-stamp bounds-limited SDF.
