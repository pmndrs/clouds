## 2. Render settings the deck gives (and where ours deviate)
Deck: step `max(1, 0.08√t)` with animated jitter below 250 m and static beyond (s150); 2 in-line light samples, then a 256×256×32 summed-density grid built from `P·S` every 8 frames (s82, s113–114); MS `P·exp(−S_sun·Remap(sun_dot,0,0.9,0.25,ValueRemap(sdf,−128,0,0.05,0.25)))` (s123); ambient `sqrt(1−P)·exp(−S_up)` (s126/s128); direct `T·phase` with "several HG" (s28; g ≈ 0.2 from SY); MS attenuated over height (s28 notes, no formula); no temporal reconstruction, near/far split 200 m at 1:2 for 40/60 Hz (s157–160). The deck gives **no** extinction, no phase weights, no ambient/MS gains, no exposure or tonemap, and the light sums are of density *samples* (names in s123/s128), not density·m·extinction.
Our art defaults vs the deck (checked in `runtime.ts` and the shaders today):
1. Detail type lifted to the density scale, `typeFromScale 1` (deck: raw type). 2. Light grid optical depth `lightDensityScale 1.2` × extinction × metres (deck: sample sums; the frames want ≈0.1 of that, see §4). 3. Inner glow: `glowFront 1` relaxes toward the sun from every side, `glowBoost 12`, `msStrength 1.4` (deck: toward the sun only, no boost). 4. Ambient: `AMBIENT_GAIN 1.5`, `ambientFloor 0.15`, diffuse `1/(1+τ)` (H3), `ambientExtinction 0.08`, ground term via `ambientSkyBias 0.9` (deck: sky only, `exp(−τ)`, no floor). 5. Additions not in the deck: `edgeDarkening 0.5`, deep octaves `deepScatter 1.2`, billow micro-shadow (`microDist 4`), C1 low-sun handoff, C2 haze 0.05/km. 6. Jitter animated at every distance (deliberate, Q2). 7. Temporal reprojection on high and below; split 240 m at 0.5/0.75 on ultra. 8. No MS height term. 9. Light grid rebuilt over 16 frames, not 8. Matching: sampler (s109 = pack, incl. HHF gate, now `max(detailDistance,150)`), noise as shipped, step on high/ultra, `wispyBias`/`detailBias` 0.

## 5. Remaining differences and their causes
- **Silhouette (sampler: matches).** IoU 0.81–0.93 with the camera solved from the silhouette alone; the residue sits in the faint base skirts and at the 8 m profile's edge, where the reference mask and our matte threshold differ. The noise convention is confirmed: reading the wispy channels inverted drops p101 to 0.64. Higher extinction lowers IoU (0.3: 0.851, 0.6: 0.839, 2: 0.81) because the faint fringe turns opaque.
- **Colour (sky/atmosphere + white balance, ~70%; ambient weight, the rest).** Their sky B/R is 1.2–2.0, ours 2.2–2.5; with our sky balanced to theirs the shade B/R falls from 1.44–1.75 to 0.86–1.51. Our sky ambient carries most of the brightness (s206 ambient-only median 0.54 of 0.64), while their frames read sun-lit. Turbidity 6–12 lowers B/R to 1.2–1.4 but brightens the cloud.
- **Cloud vs sky level (sky/tonemap).** Their cloud:sky is 1.0–2.8, ours 2.2–2.8 (deck) and up to 3.7 (art): their skies are brighter relative to the cloud (s206's is near white-grey). Tonemap unknown; ACES 0.28 here.
- **Body lit through (lighting units).** At the integrated light depth (`lightDensityScale` 1) the deck look's shaded side goes blue-black (shade p10 0.18 at extinction 2); the deck's sample sums need ≈0.1 to match the frames' through-lit bodies.
- **Edge crispness and billows (sampler resolution / jitter / lighting).** Theirs 0.25–0.30, ours 0.12–0.19. Single frames with animated jitter look frosty; accumulation fixes that. Deck-look in-line taps give lobes (corr up on 7 of 9 frames vs art) but our fringe stays softer and finer-grained than their smooth cauliflower lumps; the noise MIP constant (unpublished; tried 0.002–0.015) trades grain for plastic smoothness and does not close it. Unknown remainder.
- **Dark/beige blotches** in the Parkouring turret (p101, p213) and p161's interior, in both looks, never in the deck: the warm sun-fed glow on dense density-scale cores next to blue ambient (H3's blotches). Lighting.
- **s129 does not pin the ambient's directionality:** their "with" frame is brighter at the base than "without" (bottom/top 0.82 vs 0.74), while `exp(−τ_up)` can only darken; the "without" baseline must differ in another way. Left at 0.2.

## 6. Recommended default changes
1. Treat the light grid's optical depth in deck units: `lightDensityScale` 1.2 → about 0.1–0.3, and take the art brightness back out of `glowBoost`/`deepScatter`/`AMBIENT_GAIN` (re-judge; the deck look is closer in median brightness on 7 of 9 frames (one tie) and in lobe pattern on 7 of 9).
2. Lower the sky-ambient share (gain 1.5 → about 0.75, or weight it toward the sun as in C1) so clouds read sun-lit; this, not cloud extinction, is the blue shade.
3. Sky: evaluate turbidity 3–6 and a brighter, less saturated zenith for the reference look (a sky task, not a cloud one).
4. Keep: noise as shipped, raw sampler, deck step on high/ultra, `densityScale` 0.3–0.6 (both fit), matte-based silhouette checks for authored clouds.
Open: the turntable's sun (fixed to the camera) is an assumption, supported by corr 0.75/0.79/0.59 at t = 3/6/9 s; MS height term untested; tonemap unknown.
