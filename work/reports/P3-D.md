Measures:
- **World anchoring: PASS.** Weather and shape noise are sampled in world coordinates (weather via `window` uv, shape noise via a new `origin` generator option; origins are voxel multiples). Test page: two 256x256x32 cumulus windows offset by 1/4 window (+x, and diagonal), `runtime-tests/generate` section 2b:
  - occupancy mismatches in the overlap: 0 of 1.57 M voxels (x) and 1.18 M (diagonal);
  - interior (> 12 voxels from a window edge): NVDF max |delta| = 0/255 (byte identical), SDF within 2 voxels everywhere (0 violations);
  - near the window edges: max delta 122-177/255, 1921-3290 voxels (0.1-0.3% of the overlap) differ by more than 1/255, SDF 509-1842 voxels more than 2 voxels apart. Cause: profile and SDF come from a jump flood that only sees its own box, so a cloud cut by a window edge has different depths. These strips are at the edge of one window and 1.5-2 km from the camera at swap time.
  - Not 1/255 everywhere, as the board wanted; exact in the interior, differing only within about 12 voxels (100 m) of an edge.
- **Streaming window: PASS.** `createStreamingCloudscape(device, {preset, dims, voxelSize, threshold, jobsPerFrame, border, ...})` in `generate/streaming.ts`. No three import. Two generators alternate (one shown, one generating), sharing one scratch (new `createGeneratorScratch`: seeds+staging, ~300 MB, so total memory is 1x not 2x). `start(x,z)` (generate and show; also a teleport), `update(x,z,dt?)` per frame (encodes `jobsPerFrame` jobs of ~0.4 ms GPU, regenerates when the camera is more than `threshold`, default 1/4 window, from the centre; the new window is placed at camera + velocity x measured generation time), `requestWindow`. The page calls `clouds.setVolume(u.volume, {position})` when `swapped`. `generator.restart({weather, origin})` retargets a generator without recompiling pipelines.
  - Generation costs per window: 112 frames at 2 jobs/frame (1.85 s), CPU encode+submit 4.5-5.2 ms total, worst single frame 0.3 ms. No GPU/CPU stall seen (frame trace below). One-time stalls only: `start()` at page load and on backward seeks (~50 ms GPU).
- **No pop at a swap: PASS (with one caveat).** Fixed pose at t = 20 s (above the tops, looking east so both windows cover the view), window A then window B (new centre 700 m ahead), 2560x1440, HUD excluded: mean |d| across the swap = 0.87/255 full frame, 0.20/255 in the central 50% of the width (p99 3, 0.001% of pixels > 8); same-window repeat (noise floor) = 0.000. The full-frame number comes from the frame edges, which see beyond window B's z extent (new clouds appear). From looking forward along the flight line, the cloud field ends at the window edge, so the far end appears or disappears across a swap (see Open issues). `swap/` holds the captures.
- **Light grid across a swap** (runtime read-only; measured):
  - `runtime.setVolume` destroys and recreates the light grid (2 textures, a compute pipeline, 8 param buffers) and the volume bind groups. The new grid is a "first-ever" build: all slices in one dispatch in the same frame before the march. So there are no stale frames (the grid is never reused with the wrong box) and no blank frame, but each swap pays the full rebuild in one frame.
  - Measured with `Clouds.lastTimings`: lightGrid pass 3.0-4.8 ms at the swap frame vs 3.0-4.8 ms for any full build (it is already one dispatch of the build), at 2560x1440, sun fixed. CPU `setVolume` call 0.1-0.4 ms (the pipeline compile does not block).
  - The swap-frame frameMs is 16.6-16.8 for all four frames after each of the 3 swaps in a 40 s play run (see below). Cost: a ~3-5 ms GPU spike once per ~10 s, and about 33 MB of grid texture alloc/free per swap.
  - Runtime change that would remove even that (for another agent, `runtime.ts`/`light-grid.ts`): `setVolume` should reuse the light grid when the dims are the same: keep the textures and pipeline, update the volume uniforms, and call `scheduler.invalidate()` so the 8-pass amortized rebuild runs instead of the one-shot (cost: about 8 frames of the old grid applied to the shifted field, so only worth it if the shift is small; probably not worth doing). A `moveVolume(position)` for same-content shifts (no regeneration) would also avoid the rebuild.
- **60 fps / p95: PASS.** `--query play=1`, 2560x1440, whole flight, 2401 frames (`work/captures/P3-D/play/capture.json`): frame p50 16.7 ms, p95 16.8 ms, max 50 ms. 3 swaps (t = 16.0, 24.9, 35.9 s), all frames after each swap 16.6-16.8 ms. Caveat: 38 frames (1.6%) took 33 ms between t = 13.5 and t = 16 s, and four earlier play runs had 0, 7, 40 and 51 such frames. Those are not the swaps: that stretch is the climb under and into the base of the big cloud, where the cloud passes cost 20-24 ms GPU at 2560x1440 (the static `t15` bookmark: marchNear 3.6 + marchFar 5.0 + lightGrid 3.0 ms rolling = 12.4 ms; in play the view and motion cost more). The numbers also vary with other agents' GPU load. It is the runtime's near-cloud march cost, not generation; a lower `quality` would fix it. Over the rest of the flight the total is 6-7 ms.
- **Demo:** 40 s flight at 120 m/s from t = 10 (standing start, ramping); ground looking up at a cloud, climb through the layer t = 10-19 (the line goes through cloud columns at t = 15-19), cruise at 1200 m above the tops t = 20-30, descend t = 30-40 through a gap (probed: 0 in-cloud columns for t = 32-37, `lat=50` keeps it in the middle). `?t=<s>` steps the stream in 1/60 s increments from 0 (so swaps are deterministic; time frozen after), wind noise is `windOffset = f(simT)`. Bookmarks t0..t40, swapA, swapB. HUD: per-pass GPU ms, t, pos, window centre, swaps. `report()`: gpuMs, frameMs p50/p95, playback stats (+ slow frames with the clouds GPU ms), swapEvents (frame times before/after, setVolume ms), swapGpu (light grid peak), stream generations. `?probe=1` prints cloud columns along the path (a path-placing tool).
- **Capture reads (2560x1440).** t0: lying on the ground looking up at a field of cumulus, bright rims, dark flat bases, good. t5: horizon low, clouds above, ground a smeared olive strip. t10: 160 m, bank of clouds high above the horizon, ground patchwork, cloud shadows on it. t15: at base height, inside the dark underside of one big cloud, almost black (the base is dark by design of the profile lift). t20: cruise at 1075 m, clouds at the bottom edge, empty sky; the field ends before the horizon (no clouds beyond the window). t25/t30: looking down at the cloud tops, tops are soft and rounded, shadows on the ground. t35: descending next to a large cumulus (close, fine detail), more clouds beyond it. t40: below the base, ground below, cloud underside bank at the top. The cloudscape is continuous through the swaps at t = 16, 25, 36 (no visible seam in the near field).
Open issues:
- The window is the whole world: beyond 2 km the sky is empty and cut clouds end in a wall at the window edge (visible from cruise altitude looking along the flight line). The distant sky layer (P3-C1) would fill it, but it bakes its weather relative to the box (`uv = (xz - boxMin) / extent`), not world uv with a moving window, so it needs a `weatherOrigin` option and a re-bake on swap; I did not enable it. `?border=` fades coverage at the window edge (default 0 = exact overlap, hard cut).
- The page's cloud base is a flat 600 m, and the cumulus preset's weather is tuned for a 4096 m window (`weatherExtent` option).
- Edge differences (above) mean a thin strip of clouds near the old and new window borders changes at a swap.
- Cold-start `start()` blocks about 50 ms GPU on load; a backward `seek` repeats it.
