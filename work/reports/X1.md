**What was built.** A parallel density model behind `densityModel: 'procedural'`, traced from the camera to the horizon (curved shell, 120 km) through the sky layer's targets, 1/16 temporal resolve, composite, aerial perspective and C2 haze. March and light cone are Sky Pro's (coarse x4 steps, step back, surface-adaptive fine steps; 25 m x 1.7^i taps, cheap density after 50 % opacity, HZD s75-84) plus 4 far cheap taps to the shell top (HZD s83) so a low sun is shadowed. Two shapers: **takram** (HZD 2015 layer model as Takram ships it: per-layer weather channel, semicircle height gradient s34, coverage remap s35-36 / N17 p.40-44, 128^3 Perlin-Worley shape and 32^3 Worley detail erosion inverted at the base s37, turbulence offset s33, using Takram's textures) and **skypro** (ported from SkyProClouds.js: height-field top = weather + coverage - 1 + noise dilation, flat-base floor mask, 64^3 noise erosion, its weather kernels and bounds skip). Formulas and slides are cited in `procedural/density.wgsl.ts`; credits in `procedural/NOTICE.md`.

**Verdict: the procedural model closes most of the *shape* gap; what remains is mostly *lighting*.**
- Structure (`sbs/ground`, `above`): the NVDF field is rows of separate blobs low on the horizon. Procedural/Takram-shaper gives connected fields of flat-based clouds receding overhead and the same patchwork from above as Takram (same textures, so nearly the same structure). Against the humilis photo (15:15) layout and scale read right.
- Lighting A/B on the same density: with **our** lighting the sky is soft and hazy-white at midday with blue-grey shaded bases, and at sunset/dusk the bodies are pale pink-grey. Swapping only the per-sample lighting function for **Takram's** (8 Wrenninge octaves, HG 0.7/-0.2, sky irradiance/4pi x height gradient, powder; same sun, sky and optical depths) gives Takram's look: dark bodies with gold rims at sunset/dusk, crisper sculpted bodies at midday (`sbs/sunset`, `dusk`). Causes on our side: our ambient is about 4-6x Takram's (1.5 x mean radiance vs irradiance/4pi x 0.5-1) and blue; the N3 glow, edge darkening and relax terms need an SDF depth and NVDF in-scatter profile, which a per-sample model only has as a proxy (`profileDepth`; sweeping it and `ambientStrength` moved little).
- Sky Pro shaper vs Takram textures: **Takram's wins** for the photos and the Takram view. Sky Pro at Tidewater defaults (shell 4-9.2 km, 29 km weather) gives a sparse, high "partly cloudy" sky like the Sky Pro pane; lowered to 1-3.6 km it collapses to thin wisps (its constants are tied to the 5.2 km shell). Its march and light cone are what the trace uses.
- Still short everywhere, Takram included: the mediocris photo's crisp cauliflower outlines and hard sculpted shading (fine detail is a 32^3 Worley at 5 m texels on a half-res layer; 0.75/1.0 layer scale looked the same, +1.6/+3 ms). Our C2 haze also hides the far cloud band Takram keeps at the horizon.
Measures (cost reported, not gated; GPU ms of our cloud passes at 1920x1080 with Takram rendering beside it, noisy): cloudsTotal NVDF 5.8-9.8, procedural 4.8-7.6 (Takram lighting 5.2-7.6, Sky Pro shaper 5.7-7.7). The trace itself is 1.1-2.6 ms (1.5-3.1 with Takram lighting; 3.5 for Sky Pro from above) vs the NVDF layer's 0.8-1.8 plus 0.6-0.9 of march and light grid; `skyResolve` reads 2-5 ms in both modes (same pass; looks like timer contention). Takram total 8.8-12.0.

**What a full switch would involve** (for the owner's decision):
1. Lighting for a per-sample model: either adopt the octave lighting for the procedural density, or give our N3 lighting a real depth (e.g. a coarse distance field from weather + profile) and retune ambient level and colour. This is the larger lever.
2. Our own noise and weather textures (Takram's are a dev-time stand-in, loaded from node_modules), and weather from our generator/species: type channel and NE p.20-29 top/bottom profiles, HZD s45 horizon rule.
3. Ground shadows and reflections: the shadow map's sky term and the dome do not see the procedural model yet (the Takram pane has shadows off, so the compare is fair, but `above` shows none).
4. Hybrid with heroes: today the voxel march is off in procedural mode; partition the box as the sky layer does so NVDF heroes/VDB sit inside the procedural sky.
5. Packaging: a `@clouds/runtime/procedural` subpath (package.json, build-release) and opt-in in open-water, plains-flight, sunset, species (`attachProceduralClouds(runtime, device, { urls })` is ready); bundle headroom first.
