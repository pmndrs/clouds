Design notes and deviations:
- **The loader is in core**, because the flagship could only change its whitelist.
  - `loadCloudVolume` reads `cloud.json` `fine`, walks the contiguous Fp8 leaf array (`hero/nvdb.ts`, no tree descent) and packs bricks.
  - `uploadCloudVolume` attaches `.fine`; the volume owns it.
  - Option `{ fine: false }` skips the download.
- **G/B are taken from the coarse voxel holding each texel (nearest), not trilinear.** A GPU trilinear fill cost 0.9 KB of the bundle and pushed `@clouds/three` to 60.3 KB. Nearest also keeps outside G/B from bleeding into surface voxels.
- **Stamp.**
  - The snap puts the box min corner on the target's 8 m lattice (not 4 m) and the yaw on a quarter turn, at scale 1. The coarse stamp is then an exact copy and the reduction rule holds.
  - Fine heroes must use `replace`; with `max`/`cutout` or scale ≠ 1 the stamp is coarse-only, warned once, and `result.fine.dropped` says why.
  - Re-bricking runs on the GPU. Each texel replays the coarse stamp's decision for its voxel: hero-owned texels read the hero's pool exactly; the rest keep the previous brick or take the coarse R, zeroed in empty coarse voxels.
  - Each stamp grows the overlay (new textures, old slabs copied in). Stamping takes 7-10 ms wall per hero. The old textures are left to GC.
- **Importer.**
  - `fineVoxelSize` converts once at 4 m. Margin and noise use `corrMeters` (24 m, so 3 voxels at 8 m, unchanged).
  - The coarse layer is `reduceFineLattice`, whose G/B are now the means over the set voxels.
  - vdb-drop gains a `fine` picker and a "download fine.nvdb" button.
