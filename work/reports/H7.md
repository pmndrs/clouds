## Steps (WIP commits f478121, 27d7512, 9035a40)
1. **Skirts and wispy bottoms** (`skirtMode 'skirt'`). The H6 veil was a dilated shell around the lower flanks and read as grey smog from above. The skirt (n3 s162) keeps H6's footprint and adds the same footprint 500 m upwind, so material trails downwind of each body. It sits in a thin band (peak h 0.08, gone at 0.18) and is streaked by the detail fBM read 3x coarser and stretched 2.5:1 along the wind; values below 0.25 are cut to 0 (blue gaps). Bottoms (gp7 p.108, hzd15 s37): curl grows to 2.5x below the wispy switch with half of it vertical, plus a second erosion of 0.5 in gp7's order (on the density, after the coverage remap) that frays the base ramp while the hfw order keeps flanks intact. Tried and dropped: the body-column footprint (a sheet over half the sky, +10 ms) and the 75 m detail cells as wisps (popcorn).
2. **Lobe rung** (`lobeStrength 0.6`). One billowy octave between the body and the grain: the detail texture's coarsest channel at a 1050 m tile (about 260 m cells, 3.5x the 75 m grain). It erodes the low-frequency noise (hfw order), fades in from the wispy switch to h 0.45 (smooth bellies) and scales with sat(cov / 0.5)^2 so small clouds do not shatter into pellets. Visible as mid domes on the tops and rims (sheet s2). The effect is moderate; stronger values cut bodies apart.
3. **Shelves and the slab.** `typeBlend 'hybrid'`: one rise from the lerped stops times 'curves'' summed falls. The summed rises gave mixed-type bodies two base heights (cumulus full at 0.09, stratocumulus at 0.16), and the threshold cut that into a base shelf. `baseLump 0.04`: the base ramp is read higher where the 600 m Worley fBM is high, so each lobe hangs its own underside (santa D3, frog F10). The slab's underside is lumpier but still reads smooth and grey, which is mostly lighting.

Open issues:
- Cost: baseLump's early-out reads the gradient at the largest offset, and the lumps add mass at the bottoms. If the judges do not see the lumps, `baseLump=0` is the cheap fallback.
- The skirt streaks are long and fairly straight. In the sunset view they show as dark brush strokes against the sky. More curl on the skirt lookup is the next lever.
- Detached pellets at body edges remain (fine erosion near the threshold). lobeCoverage keeps the lobe rung from adding to them.
- For D1 (lighting): the slab and the overhead bodies read as smooth grey plastic from below, with no lobe self-shadowing. Sunset interiors are flat card. Thin skirts over dark ground read white from above here, but grey in H6. Sunset ground is much darker on the current renderer than in the H6 captures (the renderer changed under these captures; both final sets use the same state).
- `typeBlend 'hybrid'` and baseLump add a little mass at the bottoms (cover is unchanged by the mask measure). `hzn_coverageHi=0.87` takes it back if the judges call it crowded.
