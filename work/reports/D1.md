## 1. Method
One variable at a time against the matched reference, measured by `d1.py stats` (D0's IoU / corr / median / lit p90 / shade p10 / cloud:sky / shade B/R / sky B/R / edge, plus **pit** = mean black-hat under a 15 px closing inside the eroded cloud mask, **grain** = 1–2 px texture energy, **relief** = 5–25 px shading energy) and by 1:1 crops of every result next to the reference. Our silhouettes come from the page's matte shot (exact), the references' from D0's sky fit. 18 capture rounds (`work/captures/scratch/d1/t0..t18`, ~250 renders).

## 2. Causes found (each is a named setting; what the two looks did is in §4)
1. **The pits are shading, not holes.** The matte of the pitted deck render is solid where it pits. `lightDebug` isolates them to the sun taps.
2. **The in-line sun taps were in the wrong units (10×).** The deck's light sums are sums of density *samples* (s113/s114/s123); D0 put the light grid in those units (`lightDensityScale 0.1` ≈ one sample per 16 m cell) but left the two in-line taps at the view extinction. Every 8–20 m erosion hole of the up-rezzed field (P1: the wispy Curly-Alligator blobs; they survive every noise mip, so the MIP constant is not the lever: tested 0 / 0.0005 / 0.001 / 0.002, identical stats) then cast a hard shadow, and one sample standing for 16–48 m printed a 10–25 m mottle. `inlineLightScale` 1 → 0.1: p101 pit 0.013 (art) / 0.023 (deck) → 0.003–0.005 (reference 0.008), IoU 0.77 → 0.83. In the deck's units a lobe's own 30–60 m still shades it; the holes do not resolve.
3. **D0's suns were 90–170° off**, fitted under an ambient-dominated model where corr is meaningless. Term decomposition shows the deck frames are *not* front-lit: s78 (p101) is lit from in front and high (el 40, az 210; the deck's own s124–125 "the backlit body fills with warm glow"), s181/media33 (Stormbird) from behind-right and high (el 45, az 60; the sun turns with the camera on the turntable), the pack renders s191 at el 30 az 120 (Parkouring) and el 40 az 220 (Stormbird), s129 at el 30 az 240. Suns were re-fitted on the full model, by eye over the crops with corr as a check (0.57–0.83).
4. **The blue and the flatness were the sky ambient carrying the body.** `lightDebug 4`: the ambient (gain 1.5, turbidity-1 sky, B/R 2.2–2.5) was the brightest term and isotropic; the direct term alone already had the reference's lobe pattern. The deck frames have *different skies*: a clear gradient on s78 (B/R 2.1 at the top), a mid-blue one on the pack renders, a near-white haze on s181 (sky L 0.64, B/R 1.24, cloud:sky 1.0). Per frame: turbidity 3 / 6 / 10 / 20, exposure 0.16–0.26, sun colour (1, 0.96, 0.9) (reference lit B/R 0.91–0.95). Cloud side: `ambientStrength` 1 with `ambientSaturation` 0.4 (the sky light a cloud sees is greyer than the zenith; Decima's ambient is a whole-sky colour including the white horizon band), `msStrength` 1 (the deck's weight; at 2.5–3 the ms term, profile × relaxed Beer–Lambert, prints the profile's steps at the eroded surface as bright mottle and amplifies jitter grain: `lightDebug 2`), `diffuseSun` 0.15 (M1's isotropic diffused sun) lifts the darkest 10 % to the reference's level and keeps it warm. Shade B/R 1.6–1.75 → 0.96–1.18 (reference 0.99–1.16).
5. **Ambient directionality in the deck's units.** s128's `exp(−summed_ambient_density)` is `ambientExtinction` 1.0 in the grid's units; it prints Parkouring's two S = 1 cores as dark columns no frame shows (H3's blotches). 0.5 keeps the undersides (relief 0.029 vs 0.030 on p101).
6. **Silhouette crispness** at constant light units: `densityScale` 0.6 → 1.0 with `lightDensityScale` 0.09 and `inlineLightScale` 0.06 (products unchanged): edge 0.13 → 0.17 (reference 0.17–0.24).
7. **Stability.** Six near-consecutive frames at p101, per-pixel luminance std inside the cloud: decima 0.0030 (2.6 % of pixels above 0.01), art 0.0042 (6.6 %), deck 0.0055 (13.8 %). The deck's static far jitter (`jitterStatic 1`, s150) gives 0.0026 (2.1 %) on top; left off by default (Q2's hatch). Most of the instability was the high-contrast taps, not the jitter.
Not causes (tested, no effect on pits or grain): the noise MIP constant and mip 0, profile-only taps (`inlineProfile`), mip-biased taps (`lightMipBias`), static jitter, the up-rez formula (the sampler matches the pack line for line; both looks use the same field). NVDF upload (rgba8unorm linear, 8 m texel centres, SDF r16float rounded down) and the noise (bit-equal to the pack TGAs, P1) are as shipped; BC6/BC1 decode differences are below the 8-bit quantum.

## 5. What remains and why
- **Body brightness on the hazy Stormbird frames:** our median 0.68–0.74 vs 0.56–0.62 at matched lit level and sky; their shadow side is a flat grey darker than the sky, ours still carries more ambient. The sky itself: @pmndrs/sky at turbidity 20 puts a large forward-scatter halo around the sun that Decima's gradient sky lacks (s206 / s33t3 top corners); the frames' sky is not a physical one, so a per-frame `skyf` tint stays available.
- **Relief on Stormbird** 0.015–0.019 vs 0.021–0.030 and **edge** 0.10–0.18 vs 0.15–0.24: our lobes are slightly softer and the silhouette fringe longer. The reference's fine fluff (grain) now matches; what is missing is lobe-scale terminator contrast. Candidates: the ms secondary phase (the deck's "several HG" is unpublished), the view-ray extinction above 1.0.

### What made the dirt (each found by term isolation `lightDebug` and 2-3x crops)
1. **Smudges** = the sun-fed body terms dying under Parkouring's two S = 1 cores and in the Stormbird's thick centre: `exp(-tau_up)` with no floor (ambient), `diffuseSun 0.15 / (1 + 0.11 tau_sun)` and `msStrength 2.5` all go to 0 where tau is 10-30x the rest, and what is left is the desaturated (ambientSaturation 0.4) ambient under a yellow-warm sun: brown. Fix: the art look's bounded ambient (`ambientFloor 0.15` with the deck's exp law), no glow/deep terms, `msStrength 1`.
2. **Sparkle flecks** = the ms term (`profile x exp(-tau relax)`) evaluated at jittered sample positions at msStrength 2.5 (lightDebug 2 shows the speckle alone). At 1 it is below the reference's shadow grain (0.003).
3. **Fibre / "brushed hair" / fingerprint whorls** (the deck look, and faintly every look at 3x) are not the noise texture: they survive noise mips 0.002 → 0.02 unchanged, and profile or mip-biased sun taps do nothing. They are the interleaved-gradient jitter pattern itself: at temporal rate 1 (ultra) the march wrote the history directly and the resolve never ran, so every frame was one jittered sample set. New `temporalAccumulate` (on in the look): at k = 1 the march writes a full-size trace and the resolve averages it into the history at `temporalBlend`. The whorls and the dotted fringe vanish at 3x; six-frame flicker std 0.0029 → 0.0019 (art 0.0035, deck 0.0052; 0.8 % of body pixels above 0.01 vs 3.9 % / 12.4 %). Decima's frames are TAA-accumulated.
4. **Brown instead of mauve.** The references' lit faces have G = B (s206 0.91/0.85/0.82: a pink sun) and their shade R = B > G (0.53/0.51/0.53). Under a pink sun ((1, 0.95, 0.93) clear, (1, 0.92, 0.88) hazy) with `ambientSaturation 0.4` the shade lands at 0.52/0.50/0.51 (s206) and 0.53/0.53/0.58 (p101, reference 0.56/0.56/0.60); the art's is 0.56/0.57/0.64 (blue), round 1's 0.52/0.51/0.50 (brown).

### Findings
1. **The missing detail was the view extinction, not sampling.** In the s78 tower at native 4k the reference has twice our band energy at every scale from 4 to 32 px (2-16 m): b4/b8/b16/b32 0.0015/0.0045/0.0156/0.0307 vs round 2's 0.0008/0.0022/0.0098/0.0212. Noise mip 0, half march step, the HHF fold at all distances, temporal accumulation off, the far-pass scale (the 4k and 1080p renders have identical spectra at 1920) and the lighting weights (ms 2.5-4, phase g 0.5, the upward tap) all leave it within 5 percent. The direct term alone (`lightDebug 1`) already has the reference's spectrum, so the lumps are in the field; they were washed out because at densityScale 0.7 the up-rezzed density (0.1-0.18 where S is low) has an e-folding length of 8-14 m, the lumps' own size (12-24 m). At **densityScale 2 with the light units held** (lightDensityScale 0.045, inlineLightScale 0.03: unchanged 0.09 / 0.06 per density-metre) the tower's spectrum lands on the frame (0.0038/0.0070/0.0117/0.0190 under the matte mask vs 0.0040/0.0076/0.0132/0.0208) and the nested lumps appear at 2-3x; at 4 the hard surface brings fibre back. D0's lower fit came from the IoU, which the opaque fringe lowers; the look is right at 2.
2. **Parkouring's shadow value and the base smudges: the lighting read P x S where the deck reads P.** The deck's in-scatter probability (`ms_volume = dimensional_profile`, s123) and ambient occlusion (`pow(1 - dimensional_profile, 0.5)`, s126) use the profile alone; we passed `mProfile` = profile x density scale, the quantity the light grid is built from. Where S is low (most of Parkouring, S about 0.24) the glow was 4x too weak, and the two S = 1 cores stood apart from the rest under the base: the smudges. ### 1. Shading: the sun from the frames' shading, then the light units
**The sun from the shading.** `scripts/d1/sunsweep.py` renders a bookmark under 84 sun directions (7 elevations x 12 azimuths, 960 x 540, `work/captures/scratch/d1/sweep6`) and scores each against the frame inside the cloud mask by the lobe-scale luminance correlation plus the IoU of the lit halves (pixels above each image's own median: where light and shade fall), then a fine grid around the best. s78: 35/210 (corr 0.63 / lit IoU 0.56; the round-1 fit 40/210 ranks 4th at 0.43 / 0.49); s191: 20/100 (0.64 / 0.64; the round-1 30/120 ranks 5th); t3 keeps 45/150 (rank 1); s181 45/30 edges 45/60 by 0.03 and keeps the turntable's fixed sun-to-camera relation. The decisive number is the third column: at every sun direction the shade-to-median ratio of our render stays below the frame's (s78 0.73 best vs 0.85; s191 0.81 vs 0.89; t3 0.78 vs 0.85; s181 0.84 vs 0.93), so the deep shadow sides are not a sun-direction error; they are the term balance.

**The balance from the frames.** `scripts/d1/termfit.py` renders the four Nubis terms alone in linear (`lightDebug` 1-4, `@post:none`) and solves, by non-negative least squares over the cloud mask at the lobe scale, the weights that best explain the frame's display-linear luminance (both at the fitted suns). s78: direct 0.32, ms 0.47, diffuse 4.3, ambient 1.27 (residual 0.375 -> 0.263); s191: 0.50 / 1.05 / 3.5 / 1.18 (0.289 -> 0.215): the frames want far more light carried into the body by the sun's multiply-scattered share and no more direct. Tested one variable at a time on both frames (`scratch/d1/bal6*`, residual of blurred luminance against the frame, corr, levels):
- the light grid's units. The grid's sun sum was 2 x 0.045 = 0.09 per density-metre where one density sample per 16 m cell (the deck's grid, s113-114) is 0.0625; the in-line taps were already at 0.06. **`lightDensityScale` 0.031** (= 0.062 per density-metre with densityScale 2) alone takes the residual from 0.149 to 0.133 (s78) and 0.115 to 0.103 (s191) at unchanged corr and lifts the shade p10 from 0.49 to 0.53 / 0.55 to 0.59 (frames 0.57 / 0.62). This is the "light grid over-darkens" of the brief, by 1.44x, and it was hiding behind the taps' units since round 1.
- on top of it, **`msStrength` 0.7** (from 0.5): residual 0.134 / 0.100, corr 0.61 / 0.63, shade p10 0.55 / 0.61 (frames 0.57 / 0.62), shade RGB .54/.55/.60 vs the frame's .56/.57/.60 on s78. The isotropic diffuse term at the fitted weight (diffuseSun 1.0-1.6 with exposure x0.4-0.6) reaches the same shade level but flattens the lobes (corr 0.58-0.61) and was not taken; the deck-structured glow (P exp(-tau relax)) lifts the shade with its own lobe structure. `ambientTap` 16 lowers corr; `lightDensityScale` 0.031 combined with the diffuse mix overshoots (shade p10 0.65).
On the s181 frames the same change overshoots the median (t3 0.657 -> 0.722, frame 0.615) while matching the shade; their exposures were refitted with the look (below), the per-frame exposure being the one free parameter the frames are known to differ in.
