Measures (acceptance):
- 5 x 2 reference frames: 10/10 rendered, tone-mapped (exposure refit 3.238 on the final 02 photo, stored in `times.json`), paired and sheeted. `python3 scripts/vdb/reference/sheets.py` rebuilds the sheets.
- Vapour vs foam, against end.jpg and the Frog pano: **the Karma frames read as vapour, not foam.** At photo framing each cloud is one cohesive body, lobes merge through low-density tissue, the shaded body is a soft warm grey with a brighter sun-side flank. So the forms are not foamy. At detail framing they are too soft (steam / cotton wool, never foam): the crisp cauliflower tops of the photos are missing, partly the data (4 m voxels, smooth plateau) and partly OIDN (raw crop shows lumpier edges, same large-scale read).
- What makes ours foam, from the pairs: (1) **the converter drops the connective tissue.** Voxels below the 0.05 iso are 27-39 % of active voxels and 7-12 % of the mass, but at physical extinction a 200 m wisp at density 0.03 is still τ ≈ 3.6. Karma clouds cover 1.7-2.9x more of the frame than ours (02: 24 % vs 13 %; 03: 18 % vs 6 %); 03's tower and left wisp and 10's bridge exist only in Karma, ours shows their dense cores as separate puffs. (2) **ours lights shaded faces near white**: cloud-pixel luma median 0.90 / p99 0.97 vs Karma 0.64-0.76 / 0.86 under the same sky; the sun here is side/back-lighting (70° left, 20° ahead), so most of the visible body should be grey. (3) **crease colour**: ours has blue-tinted, high-contrast creases between lobes (the foam cue); Karma's creases are a gradual warm grey.
