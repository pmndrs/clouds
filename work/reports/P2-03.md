Design notes:
- Field: 4.1 km square, base 150 m, centre 2.3 km north (-Z) of the origin; camera looks north. Weather map from `encodeProceduralWeatherMap` (scale 12, coverage 0.5..0.64, seed 7) with `useMapHeights:false` (base 0.08, top 0.18..0.5 by coverage). With the default map heights the clouds were full-height pillars; with large scale a continuous slab. All tunable by query (`wscale, lo, hi, seed, base, tmin, tmax, jobs`); `?cloud=parkouring` uses the asset instead; `?ocean=0` hides the water.
- Camera is lifted above `ocean.heightAt` as in the ocean page.
Open issues:
- Adapter gap: `Clouds.setVolume()` still rejects a GpuCloudVolume (its own guard, packages/three/src/clouds.ts:118) although the runtime now accepts it. The page calls `clouds.core.setVolume(g.volume, { position: <min corner> })` instead (position is the volume's min corner there, the adapter converts from the base centre). Fix: remove the guard and keep the centre-to-corner conversion using `volume.dims`/`volume.voxelSize`. The generator must stay alive (caller-owned volume).
- Reflection API needed: `clouds.reflection(dirNode: vec3): vec4` in the adapter (not present). It must expose `runtime.dome.texture` (rgba16float, octahedral, rgb premultiplied radiance, a = T) as a three texture node (share the GPUTexture like the cloud StorageTexture) and sample it with the octahedral decode in `domeDirectionToUv` terms. The ocean also needs a hook to blend `env * T + L` into its Fresnel reflection (a `reflectionNode`/`extraReflection(dir, fresnel)` option on `createOcean`, or the page appending to `ocean.mesh.material` emissive). Neither exists. The runtime dome is already running (dome pass 0.1-0.2 ms in the HUD).
- Cloud art: the procedural weather map gives boxy, flat-topped cumulus with stepped/vertical-streak sides (visible in the horizon and flight shots) and no fine cauliflower detail at close range (flight). A hand-authored or noisier weather map or a taller detail mapping would help; the generator (P3-A) notes already say art quality was not its goal.
- Ocean shows a crosshatch tile pattern at 300 m (flight) and faint repetition at the shore (known from P2-A).
- No `sky.applyHaze` on the water (not tried); distance haze on the ocean would help the horizon.
