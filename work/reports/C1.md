# C1 Sunset and low-sun colour
Status: ready for review
Changed: `packages/runtime/src/shaders/{lighting,march,atmosphere,sky-layer,frame}.wgsl.ts`, `packages/runtime/src/runtime.ts`, `examples/lab/compare/{main,shared,panes/ours}.ts`. `frame.wgsl.ts` is outside the listed files: it holds the lighting uniforms (`_pad2` became `lightDebug`, plus `body` and `mie` vec4s, 336 to 368 bytes).
Diagnosis (before any fix): `?c_lightDebug=1..5` renders only the direct, ms, deep, ambient or environment term, without aerial perspective. At HEAD the cloud luminance at sunset was ms 60%, deep 16%, direct 11%, ambient 12%. At dusk it was ms 55%, deep 14%, ambient 28%. ms and deep had r/g 2.2–2.7 in linear light. So the warm cream came from the phase-less ms and deep terms taking the full `sunRadiance` at every elevation. The research hypothesis is confirmed.
Does:
1. **Body handoff** (B08). The sun share of ms and deep is `mix(bodySunFloor 0.05, 1, sat(sinEl / bodySunKnee 0.4))`.
   - At low sun it also falls with the optical depth toward the sun, by `exp(-tau * bodyDepth 0.1 * (1 - sunUp))`. This is the owner's "dense faces are sky-lit".
   - An isotropic environment-lit body (gain `bodyEnv`) replaces exactly the share the sun lost. The direct term is untouched.
2. **Ambient.**
   - The sky-light pass reduces the sky with a weight toward the sun, `1 + envSunWeight * cos²` (RDR2 S36).
   - It adds a ground bounce of `groundAlbedo * (sun * mu / pi + sky)` plus the lower hemisphere (H16 gradient, B08 "brown below").
   - The ambient pair leans to this environment pair by `ambientLowSun * (1 - sunUp)`.
3. **Per-altitude sun.**
   - Log sun radiance is sampled at the base of the lit part and at the volume top, and interpolated geometrically per sample (march and sky layer).
   - A flat-world earth shadow height `R/sqrt(1-mu²) - R` is applied with a sun-disc penumbra. Negative elevations work: the cloud above the shadow line stays lit from below.
   - Nothing in the cloud lighting cuts at `sun.y <= 0`. `cloudShadow` and the shadow map only skip the ground shadow, and the ground is unlit then anyway.
4. **`phaseModel: 'hg' | 'mie'`** with `dropletDiameter` (Jendersie & d'Eon port, clamp 25). It stays `'hg'` because the A/B did not win: tighter glare, dusk warm area down 17 to 12%, midday flatter.
   - Compare page: new `dusk` bookmark (3°, side sun) and `afterglow` bookmark (-1°).
   - New overrides `?sunel ?sunaz ?lookaz ?pitch ?c_<setting> ?phase=mie`. The sun slider now goes down to -6°.
