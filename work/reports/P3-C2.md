# P3-C2 Sky layer quality and temporal amortization
Status: ready for review
Changed: `packages/runtime/src/{sky-layer.ts,shaders/sky-layer.wgsl.ts,shaders/composite.wgsl.ts}`, `examples/lab/cloudscape-gen/main.ts`; minimal `runtime.ts` (a third sky target `back`, composite binding 11, `COPY_DST` on the sky targets, three extra args to `skyLayer.encode`); outside my list: `generate/weather.ts` (`mipLevels` option, bake unchanged), `generate/shaders.ts` (`valueNoise3` moved into the shared `BILLOW_WGSL` chunk, generator output identical).
Verified:
- `pnpm check` green (typecheck, 168 tests, build). Density parity page `pass: true` (formula max 2.8e-7, e2e 4.3e-3).
- Flagship ground/edge, layer off, HEAD (throwaway worktree) vs this code, 1920x1080: mean |d| 0.034 / 0.044; run-to-run floor 0.105 / 0.059. Unchanged.
- captures: `work/captures/P3-C2/{horizon,ground,seamNorth,above}.jpg` (2560x1440, `preset=cumulus&sky=1`), `side-by-side-*.jpg` (left P3-C1, right P3-C2), `motion/view*.jpg` (5 consecutive shots 3 frames apart while panning 0.4 deg/frame from `horizon`, `?pan=0.4`).
Root causes (debug views `sky_debug=1..11`: first-hit distance, step count, depthMax, no billow, no jitter, depth at first hit, sun/up tau):
1. Cones: not the billow. P3-C1 faded billow octaves 1-2 out at 0.3-2 km, so past ~1 km the layer showed the naked `s - h^p` field, whose top is `s^(1/1.6)` of a Worley cone: a tent with concave flanks. The voxel path hides that under three octaves of billow plus Euclidean SDF erosion. Octaves now fade at 3.5-7 km and 8.4-17 km (`lodScale`).
2. Cliffs: the depth proxy `f * 200 m` ignored the gradient. The billow switches on over 0.25 of f (`near` ramp), where |grad f| is up to 5x the smooth field's, so one 25 m step across the surface landed 60 m "deep" and fully dense (dbg 7 showed red first-hit depths). Depth is now first-order Euclidean: the surface position in smooth-field units is solved (4 Newton steps on `x + K near(x) = 0`, K = the frozen local billow) and the distance measured with the gradient of that quantity (smooth gradient from one `textureGather`, billow gradient from the Worley offset vectors, kink ramped), capped by the column's vertical distances to its base and top (thin lobe skirts have |grad| -> 0 and rendered as slabs). The profile also gets the generator's multiplicative depth noise.
3. Dot chains / wall band: a single mean distance cannot order interleaved layers; P3-C1's per-pixel `skyDist < voxelDist` test flipped along contours. The layer now writes two premultiplied layers (before / beyond the voxel box) and the composite merges front-over-voxel-over-back per tap, then interpolates (interpolating the layers first leaves cross terms where a cloud moves between front and back across adjacent texels: a 1 px stepped line).
4. Box edge: the trench came from both renderers fading coverage over the rim (two half-height clouds). With `sky=1` the page gives the generator `border: 0` and the layer `rimWidth = 0`: one continuous field, hard partition at the wall; light samples are not masked, so sky cloud next to the wall is shadowed by the cloud inside the box. The old cross-fade stays available (`rimWidth > 0`).
5. Shading: `cloudInScatter` now gets the cheap term (profile * scale) like the march; sun tau = 2 in-line up-rez samples (billow reused from the view sample) + 6 segments of the smooth field to 470 m, ambient 3 up samples to 272 m.
Temporal (Nubis 16-frame): `trace` renders one pixel per 4x4 block (Bayer order) into a sparse trace target; `resolve` reprojects the previous resolved frame through the block's fresh mean distance (sky pixels: far), blends fresh samples at 0.3 (jitter changes per cycle, so the history converges to the jitter average: no hatch), and variance-clips (mean +- 1.5 sigma of the 9 fresh samples) in proportion to how far the pixel moved (a static view keeps the converged history). Cuts (camera jump > 25 m or turn > 15 deg, sun > 0.5 deg, any model/setting change, bake, resize) trace every pixel that frame (~5 ms once at 1440p). The resolve writes the ping-pong history (storage-texture limit of 4) and copies it to the composite's targets. Reset count is in `skyLayer.info`; the page's `ready` waits for the layer's first cycle.
Measures:
- Continuity: the four bookmarks read as one domed cumulus field to the horizon; no cones, cliffs, hatching, wall band, dot chains or stepped line (compare `side-by-side-*`). seamEast/seamSouth/seamHigh also clean.
- Cost at 2560x1440, 1/4 res, sparse: the GPU was shared with 2-4 other agents' captures for the whole measurement window (voxel passes read 2-3x their quiet values). Last quiet run before the final cost tweaks: horizon 1.48, ground 0.98, seamNorth 1.20, above 0.88 ms; contended runs after them: 1.7 / 1.3 / 1.8 / 1.1 ms with marchFar 1.3-2.5x quiet. Scaling by the same factor puts the horizon at about 1.0-1.1 ms and the rest under 1 ms: target met except borderline at the horizon; needs a quiet re-measure. Resolve 0.2 ms (contended). Full trace (not amortized) 7 ms.
- Motion: 5 frames while panning show no ghost trails; the layer is softer in motion (bilinear history resampling) and newly revealed screen-edge pixels use the nearest fresh sample until their history exists (blocky strip at the trailing edge).
Open issues:
1. The sparse pass is bound by its longest rays, not thread count (16x fewer rays gave 3.4x less time), hence the coverage max-mip skip (levels 1-6 of the window) and the step cap of 256. Further gains need cheaper Worley (shared with the generator; not touched).
2. Shading parity is close but not equal: the layer's shadows are harder than the light grid's (point samples at 0.3/m extinction vs a 16 m trilinear grid), and its near surfaces are grainier (16 m steps vs ~5 m in the far march; finer steps cost 4.7 ms, a step-tied noise mip unmasked ring structure). Parity check: `sky=1&sky_rim=-1&c_densityScale=0.0001&sky_densityScale=10000` renders the box with the layer alone; shapes match the voxel render, shading differs as above.
3. The voxel light grid stops at the box wall, so voxel cloud at a sunward wall is lit unshadowed while the layer beyond it is shadowed (visible as a brightness step on the voxel side only where sky cloud lies sunward of the wall). Fix belongs in `light-grid*` (P3-E): march the analytic sky field past the wall.
4. `seamHigh` (camera 100 m from a lobe's Voronoi valley) shows that valley as a near-vertical wall; this is the weather field's cellular structure (same in the voxel field up close), not the layer.
5. New settings: `lodScale`, `temporal`, `temporalBlend`, `debug`; `depthScale` removed. Page: `?pan=D`, `?c_<CloudSettings>=N`, `sky_<setting>` (booleans as 0/1).
