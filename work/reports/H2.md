Measures:
- SD1 `mipDistanceScale` 0.01 → **0.002**. At 4–12 km (open-water horizon/flight) 0.01 smears billows into soft blobs; 0.001/0.0015 are crispest; 0.002 is nearly as crisp with one more mip of margin against shimmer; 0.005 is a middle soft state. Sky layer has no constant of its own (uses `frame.mipDistanceScale`), so it follows. Shimmer is temporal and was judged from stills only: re-check in motion.
- S5 `wispyBias` 1 → 0, `detailBias` 0.5 → 0. Flagship ground/edge/above and open-water horizon: neutral gives rounder, slightly fuller lobes, bases not forced wispy; the old values only added extra erosion on ridges. No concrete reason to keep either (old-vs-new sheets, plus single-knob variants detail05/wispy1).
- SD4 gate now `ts < max(frame.misc.x, 150)`: low (100) and mobile (60) no longer cut the 50–150 m ramps mid-blend. Compiles and runs on low; marchNear 0.56 → 0.55 ms (flagship low).
- SD2 high/ultra (`stepScale <= 1`) use `max(1, 0.08·√t)`; low/medium keep `max(3, 0.2·√t)·stepScale`. Cost (high, 1080p, 90-frame settle, noisy): flagship marchFar 1.39/2.29/1.35 → 1.62/1.73/1.69 ms (ground/edge/above), marchNear inside 1.52 → 1.64; open-water marchFar +0.5–0.7 ms (0.9 → 1.6) but cloudsTotal unchanged within noise (5.47 → 5.26). Visual change is subtle: finer crust on near/mid billows.
