Does:
1. **Hero shadow.** The light grid's sun channel (.b, the part the march and dome add to .r) now also integrates the procedural model toward the sun from each texel (in-box too: in the union its field runs through the box), with the model's cheap density, carve and extinction (`P.misc.x * y`), times `ProceduralSettings.heroShadow` (default 1; `?proc_heroShadow=0` is the A/B). Divided by `densityScale * lightDensityScale` so the march's tau equals the model's own.
   - March: from the shell entry, one grid cell growing 10 %/step, 64 steps, reach 20 km (= the model's own far light taps, PROC_FAR_REACH), stops at tau 12.
   - Texels no voxel reads (no reduced density within a cell of the 48 m segment away from the sun) skip the march.
   - One build pipeline per shaper (async). The first full-grid build never includes the model (no stall); it arrives via a sliced rebuild.
   - Rebuild key: model version, wind offset minus box position (8 m), so wind or onsen's drifting box re-slice the grid; a build in progress finishes first. Only in `hybrid` (in `procedural` the march does not run).
   - NVDF path: procScale 0, stub function. Pixel diff vs the pre-X4 light-grid files (compare page, 5 shots): mean |Δ| 0.41–0.58 (above 1.05–1.24), new-vs-new run noise 0.22–0.39 (above 1.08); diff images show the same edge-noise pattern, no structure.
2. **Uniform lighting.** Both no-sky paths render the procedural and hybrid models with no errors: the core `lighting: {kind: 'uniform'}` (dome test page with a temporary, uncommitted procedural opt-in: procedural, hybrid, hero shadow applies, mean Δ 1.33) and the three adapter's synthetic atmosphere (open-water `?uniform=1`, added; the clouds' sun follows the bookmark). Nothing to fix. Expected differences: no aerial perspective, so a white horizon band.
3. **Cruise "spike": cause.** Not a renderer bug. Open-water generates its field with `border: 0` so the sky layer can continue it seamlessly; clouds at the box walls are cut flat. The NVDF sky layer hides the cut face; with the layer's density at 0 or in hybrid (the procedural sky does not continue the voxel field) the face shows, at the south-east corner in `cruise`. It looks blocky because the jittered samples straddle the hard density step at the wall (high variance), which the k=4 temporal amortization leaves as trace-lattice blocks (`open-water-sky0-corner/corner-zoom.jpg`). Fix (open-water, hybrid only): the field's coverage fades over the outer 400 m (`border`), and `boxCarve` fades the procedural field in over the same 400 m: a cross-fade (`?fade=` m).
