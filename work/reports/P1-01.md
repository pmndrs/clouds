Design notes / deviations:
- Density is the line-for-line port; hfDetails = (t < 150). Noise wind is xz; `settings.windVelocity` default (3,0,1.5) m/s so noise animates.
- March depth texture is `rg32float` (x mean cloud distance, y scene distance marched against), not `r32float`: the composite needs the ray's scene end to reject taps that did not cover a pixel (silhouettes). Spec table should say rg32float.
- Scene depth is bound as `texture_depth_2d` (sampleType 'depth'); works for depth32float and depth24plus, so `depthKind` is informational. The march ends at the farthest scene depth in the covered 2x2 block.
- Composite: bilinear 4 taps; a tap marched against a nearer surface than the pixel is skipped; a tap whose cloud lies behind the pixel's surface counts as "no cloud".
- `runtime.resize(w,h,output?)` accepts a caller-owned output texture; the flagship passes three's StorageTexture native texture (through `sharedBackend(renderer).texture`) so a QuadMesh blends `scene*a + rgb` and three tone-maps (ACES, exposure 1, same as P0-03). Scene colour is a HalfFloat RenderTarget with a FloatType DepthTexture. Order per frame: renderScene -> runtime.encode -> submit -> quad.
- Temporary lighting: 6 sun samples (25 m, x1.6) on profile*densityScale; HG 0.7*HG(0.2)+0.3*HG(0.8) (1/4pi normalised); ms inner glow from nubis-sources s4 (isotropic, /4pi, msStrength); ambient pow(1-P,0.5) * mix(bottom, top, height). Beer/ms use `lightDensityScale` (3) because the cheap y term underestimates the up-rezzed density.
- Sun radiance = sun.light.color * intensity (sky physical sun = luminanceScale * T(sun)), direction from the light/target. Ambient is a stand-in until P1-02: `sky.baker.sky.luminanceScale` and the sun's 1-T tint give sky ambient = 0.035 * luma(E) with that tint; ground bounce = 0.03 * E * sun elevation. The two fractions are unitless tuning constants, not colours.
- Extinction: default `densityScale` 0.3 /m (spec started at 0.1; 0.1 looked like thin translucent fog, 0.3 a soft solid cumulus). All knobs are settable by `?s.<name>=<value>` (densityScale, lightDensityScale, msStrength, ...).
- Placement: `placeCloud` centres the volume at x=z=0 with base 1500 m. Bookmarks frame the occupied-voxel bounds (FOCUS_BOX); `inside` = the voxel with profile > 0.5 and SDF < -40 m nearest the centre. `?cloud=parkouring|stormbird`, `?noground=1` (debug) added.
Open issues:
- Inside lacks structure and costs 9 ms: lighting is nearly isotropic there because the in-line sun term uses smooth profile density. P1-05 (light grid + in-line full density) should add shadows; the step cost needs P1-06 split or a cheaper in-cloud step.
- Parkouring is small (~300 m core in an 864 m box), so far bookmarks show a small cloud; camera distances are 3.4x the half-width.
- Spec: the "depth r32float" row and "depth in as GPUTextureView" are fine, but the spec should state the march depth is rg32float and that depth views must be `aspect: 'depth-only'`.
- Pre-existing and harmless: "Multiple instances of Three.js" warning.
