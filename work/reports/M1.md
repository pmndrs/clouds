## What changed
1. **Direct is the default for imported VDBs.** Flagship `?cloud=local/NAME` loads `local/direct/NAME` when it exists (`?chain=nubis` = NVDF chain, `?material=m1` = M1 balance on it). `vdb_to_direct.py` documented as the import path (README "Imported VDBs"); the Nubis chain stays for envelopes and saturated 0/1 fields. New direct assets: CloudPack 03, 10 (`--like`), Frog (`--up y`).
2. **Thin material:** the direct path already keeps it (`--empty 1e-4`, half-float density, SDF of density > 1e-4; light grid integrates the same R; transmittance cutoff 0.005 at ultra). Checked on 03/10, where C1 lost it: cover 03 0.127 (C1) -> 0.249 (Karma 0.233), 10 0.176 -> 0.307 (0.294); IoU vs Karma 0.54/0.60 -> 0.93/0.96. 03's tower + left wisp and 10's bridge are present. Remaining sub-thresholds: lighting skipped below segment optical depth 2e-3 (density < ~0.001) and the grid's R clamps above p99 (1 % of voxels); both negligible.
3. **Shade values / balance.** Sweeps (60 variants, 02/09) showed the shading was carried by art boosts and sky ambient: ambient alone reached luma 0.54 (Karma's whole cloud 0.62), `glowBoost 12` lit the body white, and the in-scatter weight saturate(density/p99) + 4 m micro shadow printed the sim's 4 m texture into the radiance. New Nubis-model terms (default neutral): `diffuseSun` (isotropic sun with falloff 1/(1 + `diffuseFalloff` tau): sunlight that diffused through a lobe), `directInScatterFlat` (direct volumes: in-scatter weight 1). `DIRECT_VOLUME_SETTINGS` (applied by setVolume to direct assets, restored for the next non-direct one): lightDensityScale 1, directInScatterFlat 1, microShadow 0, glowBoost 0, ms/deep 0.2, diffuseSun 0.5 @ 0.06, ambient 0.5. `directDensity` stays V1's physical 0.6 (1.0 matches 02's shade exactly, 0.851 vs 0.854, but is a fudge).
4. **Crease colour:** `ambientSaturation` 0.5 and `ambientWarmth` 0.7 (sky light the cloud sees, toward grey and the sun's chromaticity at equal luminance) plus the diffuse sun's share.
5. Frog: Nubis chain + `MATERIAL_SETTINGS` is the page default now (`?material=nubis` = old). Direct Frog: its VDB is a saturated 0/1 sphere union (p50 = max = 1), so direct shows the spheres; not recommended.
