Design notes:
- Fine lattice is nested in the coarse one (fine array padded to multiples of the ratio, fine crop = coarse crop x ratio, `offsetFineVoxels` always 0). Coarse R = mean of the 2^3 fine profiles, floor 1/255 where any is set. SDF is the exact EDT of the coarse mask, so it is conservative by construction. Coarse G/B as before from the coarse mask and block-mean density.
- `fine.vdb`: Houdini volume -> `convertvdb`; index 0 sits exactly at the crop min corner. Houdini stores uniform 8^3 blocks as active tiles (16 in Disney) and upstream `buildFromVdb` drops tiles, so `buildFromVdbTiled` (ours, in vendor/webvdb/src/entry.ts) expands them to leaves. Without it the first parity run would have failed.
- Vendored WebVDB at commit 9a8c1a5: builder/quantizer/writer, CPU readValue, NanoVDBFile unchanged; the `.vdb` parser is the Blosc-capable copy from packages/runtime/vendor (upstream rejects Blosc, which Houdini writes). `vendor/webvdb/webvdb.mjs` is an esbuild bundle (`build.mjs`), NOTICE + LICENSE included.
- `fine.vdb` is not shipped (`--drop-fine-vdb`, reproducible; 3.7 MB at 4 m, 21 MB at 2 m).
