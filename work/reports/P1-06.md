- `animateTime=1` at 2560x1440: p50 16.7, p95 16.8 ms (ground and inside). Headless is vsync-capped at 60 Hz, so this only shows no spikes; GPU margin is large (clouds <= 4.5 ms).
What changed
- Two march passes (same shader, `passMode` uniform): near [0, split) at nearScale, far [split, inf) at farScale. Composite upsamples both depth-aware and merges `near.rgb + near.a*far.rgb`, `a = near.a*far.a`. Far pass skips pixels whose 4x4 near-pass neighbourhood has T < 0.02.
- Presets (`CLOUD_QUALITY_PRESETS`, exported): high near 0.33 / far 0.5 / split 200 / 768 steps / cutoff 0.01; medium 0.25 / 0.4 / 200 / 512 / 0.02; low 0.2 / 0.3 / 160 / 320 / 0.03. Copied into live `settings` (maxSteps, transmittanceCutoff, splitDistance) on setQuality.
- Review #3: march depth now stores the real end distance min(volume exit, scene, pass cap); composite recomputes the pixel's own end and rejects taps against it (a pixel whose ray misses the box accepts all taps).
- Review #4: Lipschitz SDF reuse while deeper than 6 m (refetch at least every 24 m); sqrt/mult instead of pow in the HF branch, profile^0.25, densityScale^4 and ambient (density.wgsl.ts, algebraic only); ray constants hoisted; lighting skipped when sigma*ds < 2e-3; min step 1->1.5 m and sqrt coeff 0.08->0.12; cutoff per preset. Cutoff exit renormalises radiance by 1/(1-T).

Quality (compared with P1-05 captures in scratch/p106-base, Read side by side)
- Far clouds: unchanged in sharpness (ground, edge, backlit crops). No halos or stair-stepping against horizon or ground.
- Near: cloud within 200 m is marched at 1/3 res; inside looks the same fog as before, slightly softer. Stormbird inside matches baseline after the cutoff fix.
- Found while testing: a cutoff of 0.03 on high produced a hard-edged dark shape at the horizon in stormbird `inside` (residual sky zeroed abruptly). Fixed by cutoff 0.01 on high (+0.1-0.2 ms) and the renormalisation; medium/low use 0.02/0.03 and are not visually checked in `inside`.
Open issues
- Composite now costs 0.4-1.2 ms (was 0.4-0.7): two upsamples plus a per-pixel ray/box test. It is the main cost on medium/low and could be optimised (skip far upsample earlier, drop the box test).
- Seam at the 200 m split is not visible in captures but both passes integrate separately; a very dense cloud crossing exactly 200 m could show a faint band.
- Per-pass GPU names are `marchNear`/`marchFar` (no `march`); harness consumers summing "march" must add both.
