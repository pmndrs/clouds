## Binding: no new slot
The pack noise is the runtime's up-rez noise. It is already bound in the volume group as `cloudNoise` (`volumeWgsl(0)`, binding 3) in all four passes that compile a procedural model: trace, shadow map, light grid and dome. The bench loads it with `clouds.setNoise(loadCloudNoise('/clouds/noise/'))`. The shaper reads it directly, so the sampled-texture count (F2's limit of 16) does not change, and the HZD detail texture keeps its slot. The veil, skirt, lobe rung, sheets and fractus still read the HZD detail. With 'nubis3', a body sample in the veil still reads the HZD detail fBM for the veil's streaks, so the veil looks the same.

## Step 1: the pack noise for the erosion (79ffac1)
**How it erodes.** The deck's up-rez (`shaders/density.wgsl.ts`, n3 s95-s109) is applied to the HZD sky's coarse density P. P is the dimensional profile after the coverage remap, base fade and gradient.
- wispy = lerp(R, G, P), billowy = lerp(0.3 B, 0.3 A, P^0.25), noise = lerp(wispy, billowy, T)
- The HHF fold blends in over n3HhfDistance/3 to n3HhfDistance (900 m). That is the deck's 50-150 m scaled by the tile ratio 600/100.
- ValueErosion: sat((P - n) / (1 - n))
- Then multiply by S^4 and apply the sharpen pow(d, lerp(0.3, 0.6, S^4)), with S = `n3Sharpen` (default 1, so the deck at full density scale is pow 0.6).
- `erosionOrder` still applies:
  - 'hfw' (the default) erodes the base noise by the same composite before the profile and the coverage remap.
  - 'gp7' erodes P itself, which is the deck's order.
- The composite fades out with HZD's detail distance, as before.

**Type mapping (my choice).** T = sat(h / wispyHeight) × lerp(n3StratusType, 1, sat(2 × HZD type)).
- Every column is wispy below `wispyHeight` (0.2) and billowy above. This is hzd15 s37 / gp7 p.108's switch, and n3 s83-88's "falling density curls".
- Stratus (type 0) is half wispy throughout (`n3StratusType` 0.5). Stratocumulus and cumulus are fully billowy above the switch.

**World scale.** One pack tile per `n3Tile` m, read at the footprint's mip. No extra octave: the pack's LF/HF pair, blended over P, already gives two frequencies.

**A/B** (`work/captures/N1/s1/`): hzd, then nubis3 at 300, 600 and 1200 m in 'hfw' order, then 600 m in 'gp7' order. Two interleaved reps.
- `sheet.jpg`: ground, gp7, horizon, above.
- `sheet-close.jpg`: tops, thin, abovetop. The close-ups are `tops=gp7@look:23:27@fov:12`, `thin=gp7@look:16:8@fov:12` and `abovetop=above@look:2:-5.5@fov:15`.
- 1:1 crops: `crop-gp7.jpg`, `crop-tops.jpg`, `crop-thin.jpg`, `crop-abovetop.jpg`.

What shows:
- **The pilling comes from the HZD detail erosion, not the shape.** A stage-2 capture (no detail) of the `tops` view has no orbs. With the HZD detail, the 'hfw' order hollows the top of the left tower and leaves detached round pellets along the right edge. Those pellets are the inverted-Worley cell centres that survive where the base is thin.
- **nubis3 at 600 m:**
  - The tower keeps its top.
  - The edge is a run of attached cauliflower billows. One or two small fragments are left at the far right.
  - The big body's flanks gain billowed texture where HZD had smooth, streaky flanks.
- **300 m** gives a fine, busy grain.
- **1200 m** is smoother, but shows hatching (the march dither) in thin parts.
- **'gp7' order** is crisper and busier. In `abovetop` it reads as foam.
- **Chosen: 600 m, 'hfw' order, sharpen 1.**

## Step 2: the pack noise in the shape (ff5122f)
- **What it does:** `shapeNoise 'nubis3'` replaces the shape's dilating Worley fBM (G/B/A) with the pack's billows, 1 - (0.625 B + 0.375 A), read with an `n3ShapeTile` tile. The values are mapped onto the Worley fBM's mean and spread (pack 0.608/0.157 → 0.69/0.10), so the coverage threshold keeps its meaning. The read is tricubic where the shape read is. The Perlin-Worley R stays.
- **A/B** (`work/captures/N1/s2/`): hzd, nubis3 erosion, and nubis3 erosion plus pack shape at 2 km and at 4 km. Two interleaved reps; `sheet.jpg`, `sheet-close.jpg`, `crop-gp7.jpg`, `crop-ground.jpg`, `crop-tops.jpg`.
- **Result:**
  - It reshuffles the bodies and gives somewhat less lumpy outlines. There is no clear gain on the tops or thin parts, and the 2 km tile keeps a few edge fragments.
  - It costs +6 to 10 ms of skyLayer: a second 3D texture in every sample, and 8 taps when the read is cubic.
  - **Stays an option, off.** Perlin-Worley + Worley fBM stays the shape noise.

## Step 3: the default
- **Against the papers** (local only, `artifacts/research/n1/s3-refs.jpg`): our hzd and nubis3 renders from the reference cameras, beside n3 s37 image55/56, s99 image124 and s101 image127. Ours only: `work/captures/N1/s3/sheet.jpg`, `crop-img124.jpg`, `crop-img56.jpg`.
- **What shows:**
  - nubis3 gives the billowed, cauliflower surfaces of s99/s101 and the HFW frames: crisp lit ridges and rounded lobes attached to the body.
  - HZD gives smooth blobs with detached pellets. They are visible in the backlit image56 view too.
  - `n3Sharpen 0` is softer and loses the crisp ridges. Keep 1, per the owner's edge-crispness rule.
- **Decision:** nubis3 is closer and removes the pilling on the tops, so it is now the default.
- **Final check** (`work/captures/N1/final/`, 3 interleaved reps):
  - The default renders as step 1's 600 m arm (mean 0.3/255).
  - `hz_noiseSet=hzd` renders as the pre-N1 baseline.
  - `sheet.jpg`, `crop-tops.jpg`, `crop-gp7.jpg`.

## Is the pilling gone?
- **Tops:** yes in the close-ups (`tops`, gp7's big body, image56's backlit edge). The detached orbs are gone; what remains are billows attached to the surface. One or two small fragments are still left on the most eroded edge.
- **Thin and low-density bodies:** the smooth round blobs become textured cauliflower fringes, not orbs.
- **Not judged blind.** It needs the owner's eye.
