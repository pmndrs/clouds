**Tool growth:**
- **Pyro buoyancy was off in every P4-F/P9-F recipe.** The solver's lift is `-buoyancylift*(T1-T0)/T0*g`, and those recipes set T0 = 0. Sims at lift 12 and 60, and with buoyancy disabled, came out identical; with turbulence off nothing moves. The library's shapes came from turbulence plus `remap.stretch`, which explains its flame streaks, mounds and soft "connection". With T0 = 1 we get real rising towers and domes. Documented in the sim README; `sim_clouds.build` now warns.
- The lid (`cap`) is added every frame, so it settles at about -strength/cooling. The library's 0.08–0.3 values act as hard ceilings; useful values are 0.005–0.025.
- New levers: per-emitter `heat`, `stretch_from`, `taper`, `--set emit.I.F=V`.
- New tools: refinement rounds (`--refine parents`), the target page with a matching camera (assumed 46° vertical FOV, base 7.5° / crown 29.5° elevation, per-candidate distance) and a `sky` plate bookmark, plate-based segmentation of renders, best-yaw search, and `promote.py`.
