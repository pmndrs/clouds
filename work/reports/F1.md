## 1. Type variety: NE/N3 s18 tables
- `profileMode: 'tables'`: top(tt, h) × bottom(bt, h) / peak(tt, bt).
  - Reuses H4's smoothed tables and pair peaks (`generate/profile-tables.ts`).
  - Stored in ProcParams at 3 bytes per float: exact in f32 and never NaN, so the per-frame change test in `index.ts` stays stable.
  - The column base maps to the table floor 17/64 (H4's decision).
- The HZD type maps to the table's top type piecewise through `tableTypeStratus` 0.14, `tableTypeStratocumulus` 0.27 and `tableTypeCumulus` 0.4 (NE's insets).
- Bottom type = NDF + `bottomTypeBias` + `bottomTypeVariation` × a low-frequency world field (`bottomTypeTile` 24 km).
- Nutshell stays the default and is untouched.
- What I see (sheet s1):
  - 'tables' at Cu 0.4 gives flatter, smoother, lower bodies with flat crisp bases. They read like NE p.24's stratocumulus more than p.23's cumulus, and they lose surface texture.
  - `tableTypeCumulus=0.75` gives towering, tiered congestus from the same NDF (HFW image55-like towers).
  - Ragged (bias 0.4 + variation 0.6) softens and lifts the bases, but only a little: dividing by the pair peak narrows the crisp-vs-ragged gap (0.64 vs 0.94 at h 0.1).
  - None is clearly closer than H6, so the default stays off.
- Cost: +5 ms ground, +8 ms above (20-21 vs 13-15 ms, about +40-60 %). This is three bilinear reads into a uniform array per gradient call. Divergent constant-buffer indexing is a likely cause on Apple GPUs. A texture or storage binding would be cheaper but needs `index.ts` and trace bindings, which I don't own. I already removed the duplicate gradient evaluation when `baseLump` is 0.

## 2. Cumulonimbus with anvils (S7)
Settings:
- `hzdAnvil`: n17 p.46-50's `pow(cov, remap(h, .7, .8, 1, lerp(1, .5, bias)))`. `anvilClamp` (default true) clamps the exponent to [lerp(1,.5,bias), 1].
- hag eq 17 `anvilFlatten`: the gradient ^ sat(remap(h, .65, .95, 1, 1 - a)).
- hag eq 18 `anvilDensityFix`.
- A placed cell: `stormStrength`, `stormX/Z` (default -6 km, -15 km: in the ground/horizon views), `stormRadius` 2.5 km, `stormTop` 10 km, `anvilSpread` 3, `anvilShear` 4 km.
- Type-driven storms: `stormTypeStart` < 1. NDF top types above it ramp to storms.

What the cell does:
- Type goes to the hzd15 s34 cumulonimbus gradient (0.01/0.08/0.74/0.96), or the tallest table type.
- The top goes to the storm top, and the march shell rises with it.
- The column is a cone, wider low (photos §3-4).
- In n17's 0.7-0.8 band the footprint widens to 3× and shears downwind. The upwind side is a third.
- The plate follows the cell more than the noise, so it is smooth and flat-bottomed.
- The upper 40 % erodes wispy at half strength ("glaciated").
- NDF clouds around the core and under the anvil keep their own tops.

What matches and what doesn't:
- Matches: in `stormview` it reads as a Cb. A cauliflower column (w:h about 0.6) under a flat-bottomed mushroom anvil, about 2.5× the column width, sheared one way. The hag terms make the plate softer and taller (hag fig 13 direction).
- Unclamped n17 fills everything above h 0.9: a halo ring at the envelope edge, as shape.md predicted. Clamped is right.
- A global `anvilBias` on ordinary cumulus does nothing visible: the Nutshell and topRound gradients are near 0 in the 0.7-0.8 band. N17's anvil needs a tall type.
- Doesn't match:
  - Where the anvil sits between the sun and the column (the default sun at az 150 and wind (2,1)), the column reads as translucent blue. The lighting gives the shaded body sky colour only, so this is for D1.
  - The anvil edge is still lumpy at distance, not fibrous.
  - No mammatus, rain shaft or dark base. The dark base is lighting.
- Cost: storm on, 14-22 ms vs 15 ms. The shell to 10 km is roughly neutral in the views, within noise.

## 3. Mid-level and deck layers
Two sheet slots, `mid*` and `deck*`. Each has: form (`off` / `altocumulus` / `altostratus` / `stratus`), base, thickness, coverage, cell size, elongation along the wind, extinction and erosion. They are merged with the low layer by extinction, and the shell widens only when a slot is on.
- **Altocumulus**:
  - Jittered Worley cells: the shape texture's G/B read flat with a cell-sized tile, stretched 1.5:1 along the wind.
  - Flat bases and domed tops, sizes varying about 3-5:1, irregular spacing.
  - Coverage moves by ±0.35 over about 40 cells, giving patches and gradients.
  - Defaults: 5400 m + 450 m, cells 550 m, extinction 0.05.
  - Against photos 0511/0616: the layout and elongation read right. The cells are too white and opaque, and the photos' grey centres with bright rims are missing (partly lighting). No wave bands yet.
- **Altostratus** (query preset: 5500 m + 1000 m, coverage 0.85, extinction 0.03): a soft mottled veil. Too faint against photo 0811's milky overcast; it needs coverage 1 and more extinction for that sky.
- **Stratus deck** (600 m + 400 m, coverage 0.45): a flat-based deck with a lumpy top and holes. From below it is near-overcast, and from above it is a white deck with holes and cast shadows.
- Cost:
  - Ac about +1.5 ms.
  - As about +2.5 ms.
  - The deck makes ground views cheaper (2-3.5 ms, because it is opaque) and leaves the above view unchanged.

## 4. Fractus attached to each body
`fractusDensity` (0 = off):
- A sample below the low layer's lower part looks `fractusTaps` (3) taps upwind, 0 to `fractusTrail` 900 m, for a body base. The test is the coarse body at belly height `fractusBelly` h 0.08 (`hzdBelly`, which mirrors the main column).
- Material only:
  - hangs below the belly, up to `fractusDrop` 220 m, sinking with trail distance;
  - is cut where a body is directly overhead, so it streams from the lee edge instead of lining the whole base;
  - has its hang height wandering ±0.4 drop.
- The shreds use the wispy form of the detail fBM (1 - fBM: the web between the cells), 3× coarser, curl-displaced 2×, stretched 1.6:1.
- It is evaluated only for full samples where the main density is 0.

What matches and what doesn't:
- Matches: it is attached. Pieces exist only under and downwind of real bodies. The belly line is a hard ceiling, so crowns are untouched. That is the H7 failure both judges named, fixed in kind.
- Doesn't match:
  - In the `side` view (camera at 900 m, near the band) the pieces are lens-shaped, smooth and bright.
  - Thin ones show a dotted dither at their edges: a thin band under the trace's jittered steps and the 1/16 temporal resolve. I can't fix that without trace changes.
  - From the ground (`gp7`, `low`) the effect is subtle.
- Stays off.
- Cost: +3-4 ms at density 0.2: up to 3 NDF + 3 shape reads per sample in the band, within 15 km.
