## Step 1: speed (d439ad5)
**What changed:**
- New setting `hzd.tableSource: 'storage' | 'uniform'`, default `'storage'`.
- The tables (top, bottom, peaks) live in a read-only storage buffer bound at `PROC_BINDING_OFFSETS.lut`.
- Each texel's u32 holds its whole bilinear quad (`packHzdLutQuads`). A filtered lookup is one load plus `unpack4x8unorm`, where the old path did four reads with a divide by 3.
- `'uniform'` keeps F1's `ProcParams.hzLut` path unchanged (`P.hzType.x` 1 = uniform, 2 = storage).

**Why not a texture:** a sampled texture takes the trace to 17 sampled textures in one shader stage. The default limit is 16, so the pipeline layout fails to validate. The offset is +8, not +6, because +6 and +7 are the trace's voxel depth bindings 15 and 16. +8 is free in all four passes that bind the model.

**Results match:** uniform against storage differs by mean 0.08 to 0.17/255 and p99 1.0 to 1.7. Two runs of the same arm differ by mean 0.07 to 0.41 and p99 1.0 to 4.7.

**Timing:** 3 interleaved reps on a heavily loaded GPU, skyLayer ms:

| arm | ground | gp7 | horizon | above |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nutshell (H6) | 21.9-22.7 | 25.7-26.9 | 19.7-21.7 | 23.0-24.2 |
| tables, uniform (F1) | 25.1-26.4 | 29.7-32.5 | 25.6-27.4 | 31.4-33.7 |
| tables, storage (F2) | 23.1-25.5 | 26.9-29.1 | 22.4-23.2 | 28.2-29.7 |

- Storage saves 2 to 4 ms against F1's path. It runs +1.2 to 2.8 ms over Nutshell on ground, gp7 and horizon, and +5 ms in the above view.
- **Lookup cost against content:** I ran a temporary build that computed the table profile but returned the Nutshell value, so every arm rendered the same clouds. That build was never committed.
  - The lookup itself costs +1.3 to 2.5 ms (storage) against +3.5 to 4.3 ms (uniform).
  - The rest of the above-view gap comes from the clouds: the tables' bodies have more semi-transparent material, so rays march further.
- At step 3, with a lighter GPU load, the tables default is within +1.6 to 2.5 ms of H6 on the ground views (table below).

## Step 2: per-area bases on the map (f26842e)
**What changed:**
- The NDF bottom type is now a field in both recipes, `n17` and `h6`: `bottomType + bottomTypeRange × 1.6·fBM(bottomTypeCells) + bottomTypeCorrelation × (topType within typeMin..typeMax − 0.5)`, clamped to 0..1.
- Range 0 with correlation 0 gives the old constant.
- Only the tables profile reads it.

**The pairing (NE p.26-28):**
- NE's only pairing evidence is p.27: its cumulus field at bottom type 0, with flat crisp undersides.
- So the correlation is negative. Cumulus areas get crisp bases, and stratiform areas get soft, rounded ones that rise slowly.
- The pairing strength, the range and the cell count are ours.

**`bottomTypeVariation`** (F1's shader stopgap) stays an option. Its default was already 0.

**Painted maps:** all five `assets/clouds/ndf/` maps have a constant blue channel: morning 0.6, alpine 0.2, boss 0.1, afternoon 0.15, cutscene 0.3. Those are sensible per-map values, and with the tables the maps read as before. Two problems:
- The desert-afternoon map with its height image gives pointy, cone-like tops.
- The stratus of desert-morning comes out as puffy lumps.

Both are table and height interactions rather than bottom-type problems. See "Left".

**Sheets** (on the 16 km tile; step 2 was done before the tile change):
- `work/captures/F2/s2/sheet.jpg`: bottom type 0 everywhere (F1), F1's stopgap, the map default, the map with correlation 0, bottom type 1 everywhere.
- `work/captures/F2/s2/maps/sheet.jpg`: the five painted maps.
- Local only: `artifacts/research/f2/s2-bases.jpg`, beside NE p.26, 27 and 28.

**What shows:**
- In the default, some areas carry tall, rounded-under puffs (ragged bottom type) beside flat-based bodies. This is the per-area variety the map was missing.
- Bottom type 1 everywhere makes every cloud a rounded, towering puff.
- A range of 0.6 or less was nearly invisible, which is why the range is 1.5.

**Timing:** within run noise of bottom type 0.

## The 32 km tile (owner request, bce14c7)
**What changed:**
- `ndf.tileMeters` 16000 → 32000 and `resolution` 512 → 1024. The texel stays 31.25 m.
- Every cell count is doubled so the features keep their size (W1 found this equivalent):
  - coverage 9 → 18, islands 2 → 4, types 2 → 4
  - Worley 6 → 12, gate 3 → 6, bottom type 3 → 6
  - heights: new setting `ndf.heightCells` 6, which was a fixed 3 in the generator
- **To get 16 km back:** `HZD_NDF_TILE_16KM` in code, or the URL in `work/HZD-BENCH.md`.
- The desert maps need `hzn_tileMeters=16000`; the README now says so.

**Cost:**
- Generating the map, measured in Chrome over 12 runs including submit and wait: 1.3-1.6 ms → 4.0-4.5 ms. This happens once per map change, never per frame.
- The NDF texture goes from 2 MB to 8 MB (1024² × 2 layers × rgba8).
- skyLayer, 2 interleaved reps: ground 13.7-15.5 → 15.5-16.8, gp7 19.4-19.9 → 19.6-20.2, horizon 12.5-13.9 → 15.2-15.3, above 16.6-17.3 → 16.5-16.6, side about the same. It is a different map, so these are not a like-for-like cost.

**Sheet:** `work/captures/F2/s2-tile/sheet.jpg`. It includes a 9 km-up "world" view, where the 16 km repeat is gone.

## Step 3: tables as the default (5c9d888)
**What I checked:**
- **`tableTypeCumulus`:** 0.4 (NE) stays. 0.6 and 0.75 give tiered, towering cumulus with more surface texture, at about +1 ms. These are in the sheet for your pick.
- **Table floor:** 17/64 stays.
  - 0.2 thins the bodies.
  - 0.33 lifts and softens the bases and costs +2 to 5 ms.
- **Erosion:** raising `erosionStrength` and `wispyStrength` to 0.45 brings texture back, but it crumbles the upper flanks into foam pellets, which is worse at 0.6. Unchanged (`work/captures/F2/s3/tex`).
- **`densityPow`** 0.7 makes the bodies softer. Unchanged.
- **Veil:** unchanged. It looks right with the tables. Turning it off (`veilWidth 0`) cuts the sky layer from 16-18 ms to 7-10 ms in one run. The veil is about half the frame and should go into the perf pass.

**Lost surface texture (F1's finding):**
- The cause is that the tables' profile reaches 1 at only one height, while the Nutshell trapezoids hold 1 across the middle. Densities near the surface are lower, so flanks light soft and smooth.
- Raising `extinction` brings the texture back without the pellets. 0.7 and 0.8 both work, and 0.8 gives the crispest lit ridges.
- **Changed `hzd.extinction` 0.5 → 0.8.** For H6's look, use `hz_profileMode=nutshell&hz_extinction=0.5`.

**Timing** (3 interleaved reps, lighter load), skyLayer ms:

| arm | ground | gp7 | horizon | side | above |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H6 (Nutshell, 0.5), same map | 13.4-16.2 | 15.2-20.3 | 13.6-16.5 | 16.2-19.8 | 13.4-17.5 |
| F2 default | 15.4-17.7 | 17.3-21.2 | 15.2-17.4 | 18.7-20.8 | 16.9-20.4 |
| Cu 0.6 | 16.8-18.1 | 18.4-22.4 | 15.8-17.9 | 19.9-21.5 | 15.9-18.3 |
| Cu 0.75 | 17.0-17.6 | 20.7-22.5 | 15.9-17.9 | 20.4-20.8 | 16.2-16.6 |

From the best run of each: the default is +1.6 to 2.5 ms over H6 on the ground, gp7, horizon and side views, and +3.4 ms above. That is just over the 1-2 ms target. The lookup is about 1.5 ms of it, and the denser, more translucent table bodies are the rest.
