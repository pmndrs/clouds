# W2: influence mask and weather states

Branch `worktree-agent-a55653b9bb186cd7c`. Sheet: `work/captures/W2/W2-sheet.jpg` (row 1: generated / painted-replace / painted-influence;
row 2: clear to overcast frozen at t = 0, .25, .5, .75, 1; row 3: a live 12 s transition).

## What changed

**Part 1, influence mask (NE22 p.69, p.75).** A painted map is blended OVER the generated one by a painted mask (0 generated, 1 painted).
- Runtime: `procedural/hzd/ndf.ts` + `ndf.wgsl.ts` (`HZD_NDF_COMPOSE_WGSL`, `HZD_NDF_PAINT_IMAGE_WGSL`, `composeHzdNdf`, `applyHzdPaintImages`),
  `procedural/index.ts` (paint texture, composed texture, `setNdf` with `fields.influence`).
- The trace still reads ONE texture (the 16-texture binding limit is why there is no second NDF read). When a mask or a weather transition is on,
  a compose pass writes the served NDF; otherwise the served texture is the generated one, exactly as before.
- W1's replace path (`ndf.image` overwriting the generated layers) is kept, selectable with `paintBlend: 'replace'`.

**Part 2, weather states.** `procedural/weather/` (`states.ts`, `blend.ts`, `weather.test.ts`): five states (clear, cumulus, towering,
stratocumulus, overcast), each = generator overrides (`maxHeight`, `heightVariation`), additive coverage bias and type bias, cirrus cover and veil.
`WeatherScheduler`: `go(state, seconds)`, queued if one is under way, optional auto cycle (hold, then the next). Bench: `bench/weather.ts`,
sidebar section "Weather states".

## Settings (all `hzn_<key>` in a bench URL)

| key | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ndf.paintBlend` | `influence` | `replace` = W1 behaviour (painted fields replace the generated layers). `influence` = blended by the mask. Only differs from replace when a mask exists; without one nothing changes. |
| `ndf.maskImage` | `''` | mask PNG, R channel, same centring and span as `ndf.image` (needs `ndf.image`). |
| `ndf.paintCoverage`, `paintTypes`, `paintHeights` | 1 | per-field gain on the mask (0 keeps that field generated). |

Weather has no persistent settings: URL `wx=<state>` (settled), `wxfrom=<state>&wx=<state>&wxt=<0..1>` (frozen), `wxauto=1&wxdur=20&wxhold=40`,
bookmark variant `high@wx:clear:overcast:0.5`; `window.__weather.go('overcast', 12)` in the console.

## How to use

**Influence brush.** Open the painter (M). "influence mode" (Channels panel, on by default): every stroke with paint, raise, lower, noise, smudge or
gradient also stamps the new **influence** channel under the brush, so only the painted region leaves the generated sky (map view tints it magenta).
Tick **influence** with value 0 and paint to give a region back to the generated map; value 1 to extend it. Example, a hole by the sun: tick
coverage only, set it to 0, paint over the sun's spot. Save writes `<name>.png`, `-height.png` and, if anything was masked, `<name>-mask.png`;
loading a map with a mask in the dropdown ("+ mask") restores the channel. Turn influence mode off for the old replace behaviour.

**States.** Sidebar "Weather states": click a state to move to it over the "transition" slider (2 to 60 s, default 20; a click during a transition is
queued), "auto cycle" with a hold slider, "off" returns to the plain generated map. Starting from "off" the neutral state is `cumulus` (= the settings'
own map), so engaging weather itself changes nothing.

## Transition: two generated maps lerped in a compose pass, not a shader read, not regeneration

- A second NDF read in the trace needs a 17th sampled texture (the pass already binds 16) and costs +0.3 to +0.8 ms on every ray sample.
- Regenerating on every step re-runs the generator (about 4 ms at 1024^2, a hitch) and cannot do bias morphs.
- Chosen: generate the two end maps once per state change (cached by settings key, at most 4 kept), then per eased step of 1/512 one compose pass
  (lerp, biases, painted influence) into the served texture. Nothing else is touched: no `version` bump, so the sky layer's temporal history is NOT cut
  (that cut is what would show as a pop); the history just follows the slowly changing map. The largest per-frame change of the lerped bias in a
  20 s transition at 60 fps is under one 8-bit step (unit test), and a live run's frame-to-frame difference is a smooth bell with no spike
  (`work/captures/W2/c-live/live.json`; mean diff 0.1 to 4.6 on a 0..255 scale, peak at the middle where the ease is fastest).

## Cost

- Compose pass: 0.49 ms wall per step including submit and drain round trip (measured in the page, `window.__weather.cost(60)`), at most ~20 per second
  while a transition runs, so about 0.1 ms per frame amortised during a transition and 0 otherwise. A state change generates up to two maps (about 4 ms each, once).
- Painter influence: one compose per upload (80 ms cadence while painting), then 0.
- Frame GPU totals in the captures are noise from the sky content (7.5 to 13.7 ms at 1280x720, ultra), not from this change; there is no per-sample cost.
- Memory: composed texture 8 MB, paint texture 12 MB, up to 4 cached state maps 8 MB each (all only while used).

## Versus NE22

Different, and the main gap closed: NE's influence NDFs and weather states (p.69, 75, 77-78) now exist as an influence mask, a state set and a transition.
Better than the board's plan: the transition is exact per texel and costs nothing in the trace. Worse or different: NE layers several named regional
NDFs; we have one painted layer with one mask (per-field gain, not per-field masks). A transition is a cross-dissolve of two maps plus bias, so with
different layouts clouds fade in and out rather than grow; our states keep the seed, so they mostly morph. The `overcast` top looks flat from above
(low `maxHeight`, type bias -0.75); the states' numbers are by eye, not tuned against photos.

## Known limits

- Weather applies to the generated map: with weather on, `ndf.image` in replace mode is ignored; painted influence blends over the weather result.
- Hero shadow and light-grid caches refresh with the wind offset, not with the weather lerp (the lerp must not bump `version`); with `ndfMotion static`
  and animSpeed 0 their shadow of procedural clouds can lag a transition.
- The scheduler drives `cirrus.coverage` and `cirrus.veil` while weather is on (overwrites those values; the cirrus layer is off by default).
- The painter's "sky" source reads back the served texture, so reopening it after painting starts from the blended sky with an empty mask.
- `scripts/gpu/w2-transition.mjs` is the live-run tool; `window.__paint` and `window.__weather` are scripting hooks.
