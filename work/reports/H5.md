## The six faults, in order (each a WIP commit 999e4ca..a4d662d)
1. **Erosion / popcorn** (999e4ca). Cause: with gp7's `* coverage` the density is `base + cov - 1`, under 0.15 at our NDF (coverage mean 0.33, p99 0.57), so the fixed 0.2 erosion cap ate small clouds. Fix: `coverageMultiply: false`, the remap as HFW p.17 and hag eq 12 print it. Stage 2 → 3 now keeps the bodies (`sheet-step1.jpg`). GP7's own map is ~0.8 coverage everywhere (Fig 4.12, measured 220/255), so HZD never sat in that regime.
2. **Fuzzy/torn silhouettes → rounded lobes** (79fc09e). `typeBlend: 'curves'` (GP7 p.103's "weighted sum"; separate bodies, rounded tops), shape tile 4 → 6 km (ours), NDF `coverageCells` 6 → 9 (ours). Lobe/body band energy 0.21 → 0.13 (GP7 Fig 4.16: 0.09); (edge crispness is in Measures, end to end). Extinction 1-2 and `densityPow` 0.27-0.5 (NE p.105) crisp further but go grainy and flat: left at 0.5 / 1.
3. **Faceted flanks** (c4ddd57). Not R's carved pits: noise-bake A/Bs (carve 0, fine 0, smooth-min Worley, 2-3x supersampled bake, rgba16f) and no light-tap reuse all leave the "contour lines". They are trilinear texel kinks (31 m shape texels, 12.5 m detail texels) turned into terraces by the coverage threshold. Fix: tricubic B-spline reads (8 taps) of shape and detail within 30 km (ours); cost +0.25 ms skyLayer at ultra. The bake options stay as settings (default = the tool).
4. **Far-field mat** (9fb6c7a). HZD 2015 s45's horizon ring: coverage → 0.4 (our scale for "about 50 %") and type → cumulus over 15-35 km, nothing beyond 35 km, coverage faded to 0 over the last 20 % (ours, no line). The 20-120 km stack that formed the mat is gone; a haze band shows below the clouds as in Fig 4.16 (`crops-step4.jpg`). a4d662d fades the ring out as the camera climbs to the layer base (ours).
5. **`above` cotton / streak / contrast** (e27d918). The vertical streak went with step 2. Detail recipe per GP7 p.102 / Fig 4.9 ("three more Worley octaves"): one inverted F1 octave per channel at 4/8/16 cells (std 0.17 vs H1's ours-recipe 0.10), now default; it puts visible billows on the edges (Fig 4.14 → 4.16). Self-shadow contrast is lighting: front-lit (sun behind the camera); ambientGain 0.6 / lightScale 2 / octaveDiffuse 0.25 / extinction 0.1-0.3 barely move it (lit:belly 1.49-1.56). Not changed.
6. **GP7 `p*0.1`** (cc37279). Literal = detail tile 10x the shape tile (60 km): erosion becomes a near-constant offset and the Fig 4.16 edge billows vanish (`sheet-step6.jpg`). 600 m (shape x 0.1) ≈ 400 m. Kept 400 m.
