**Recipe that works** (sim unit 100 m): lens 600 × 1300 × 100 m (≈ EmberGen's 5 : 11.5 : 1) with noisy outline; smoke filled, heat uneven (floor 0.7, ~170 m cells); one 12-frame (0.5 s) burst, temperature source **Maximum** (the default Pull keeps cooling the source region after the burst). T0 = 1, T1 = 2, lift 1.2, cooling 0.2, heat diffusion 0.1, **density gravity 0.22**, disturbance 0.25 / 40 m blocks, turbulence 0.15. **Closed Y ground plane at the lens base** = the condensation level. No lid, wind, stretch or warp.
**Key finding:** heavy smoke without a floor sinks: the whole cloud fell 1.3 km below its seed by frame 200 (cooling removes lift, density gravity keeps pulling). EmberGen does the same: its base sits on the domain floor (z index 11), well below its emitter (z ≈ 48 voxels if the .ember origin is the domain floor). With the floor the base stays exactly at seed height and the plume stalls by itself (top peaks at f90–130, then slumps and spreads). Smoke weight is the lever: 0.3 → flat skirt + early stall (pyramid profile); 0.1–0.15 → taller, still rising at f130, a waist forms (mushroom tendency); 0.22 is the compromise.

- Flat base at seed height: **pass** (all four, 4 m = half a voxel; no mass below the seed even before the base cut).
- Width ≈ seed width: **pass at the base** (1.0–1.1×; cool 1.36 on the short axis); the body bulges above it, 1.1–1.2× on the long axis but 1.7–2.1× on the short one.
- Turrets upward, no chimney: **pass** for base, wide, cool. **hot fails**: a strong burst makes a mushroom (cap on a stem, area 0.19 at 10 % H). That is the burst ceiling, kept as the anti-example.
- Top stalls at ~3× thickness: **no**, 5–7× (EmberGen ≈ 5×); cool is the lowest.
What still looks wrong (honest):
- vs EmberGen: their bodies are solid blocks with near-vertical walls widest at 25–50 % H; ours are looser heaps, narrower above mid-height (area 0.28 at 75 % vs 0.15–0.56), with a waist at 10 % in base/hot and a thin skirt at the floor where the heavy smoke spreads.
- vs the owner's mediocris/congestus (photo-species-specs §2–3): photos show 2–4 big stacked domes with deep notches; ours have 30–40 similar small turrets (~160–190 m spacing) and no dominant dome, and some detached puffs float above the crown. Hot lacks a congestus' cone/pedestal; it reads as an explosion.
- In the runtime render the clouds are soft and evenly lit (little self-shadow between lobes, mushy base); that is render/NVDF mapping, not the sim (the sim projections are crisper).
