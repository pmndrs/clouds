What changed
- Light: tau = 2 in-line samples of the real density (0-16 m, 16-48 m, no HF fold) + lightGridSample(p+sun*48).x * lightDensityScale. Grid is built with densityScale = extinction. Runtime owns the grid, encodes it before the march, picks the bind group by grid.frontIndex.
- Sky: a 1-thread pass per frame writes sunRadianceAt / skyAmbient (volume centre) to a small storage buffer; the march reads it. Ambient = pow(1-P,0.5) * mix(lower, upper*exp(-tauUp), 0.3+0.7h) with tauUp from grid.y. Aerial perspective via applyAerialPerspective at the transmittance-weighted mean distance, applied in the march. Group 2 = atmosphere bind group (CloudFrame.atmosphere; runtime option atmosphereLayout). Flagship wires createSkyBridge, sky.updateAerialPerspective(), bridge.update(camera). The sunRadiance/ambient fields and the 6-tap march are gone.
- Phase: mix(HG(0.2), HG(0.85), silverStrength 0.4). New knobs: ambientStrength, silverStrength, phaseG, silverG, ambientExtinction (all `?s.<name>=`). Defaults now densityScale 0.2, lightDensityScale 1.5, ms/ambient knobs 1 with fixed gains MS 3/(4pi) and AMBIENT 1.5 in the shader.
- Exposure: renderer toneMappingExposure default 0.35 (`?exposure=`); at 1 the sky was white and washed out. No literal colours.
- Fixes: #1 step grows after 256 iterations and an unfinished thin remainder terminates; #2 radiance clamped to 60000; #5 plain-scene present until runtime.ready, camera world position, wind integrates velocity*animSpeed, encode after dispose no-op.
- Bookmarks: cloud base 500 m, camera distances scale with core radius; cameras on a ring around the sun azimuth (ground 35 deg, above 55, edge 95); backlit looks along the sun azimuth with the sun 15 deg above the view.
Look vs Tidewater (P0-04) (honest)
- ground (front-lit): white bright tops, soft grey interior, deep saturated blue sky, ~30% of frame width. Close to P0-04 a in feel, less wispy/less contrast than Tidewater.
- edge: white lobes, dark blue-grey cleft in the middle; undersides are only slightly blue-grey, Tidewater undersides are bluer/darker. Stormbird edge/above are nearly uniformly white (sun high).
- above: bright white top on green ground, readable lobes.
- backlit: dark blue-grey body with bright white rim (silver lining) and a bright sky around the sun. Core stays dark: thick cloud, Nubis relax only helps deep inside. Stormbird backlit shows a blue-lit glow in the lobes (inner glow visible); Parkouring only a rim.
- Dark edges with the sun behind the camera: weak; edges look soft and bright, not dark (no powder term, per Nubis). Sky-tinted ambient: visible as blue-grey in shaded lobes.
- inside: Parkouring is uniform pale grey fog (as P1-01, little structure); Stormbird inside shows grey volumetric structure with a horizon seam.
Measures (1920x1080, march at 960x540, ms): march P1-01 -> P1-05
- ground 0.43 -> 1.18 (cloud now fills ~4x more frame), edge 1.57 -> 0.98, above 0.87 -> 0.89, backlit 0.46 -> 1.16, inside 9.4 -> 8.06. Stormbird: ground 1.72, edge 2.41, inside 7.57, above 0.86, backlit 1.02.
- edge and inside dropped, as required; ground/backlit are higher mostly because of framing plus 2 extra density samples per lit step.
- lightGrid: 0.05-0.10 ms steady slice update; the first full build reads 0.14 ms (startup only, not steady).
