## What was built
1. **Noise bake** (GPU compute at load, ~30-40 ms warm, ~0.35-0.7 s with a cold shader compile): 128³ RGBA shape, 32³ RGBA detail, 128² rgba16float curl, full mips (2x2x2 box). Recipe = the Nubis noise-generator HDA read from its network (`sources/noise.md`), with exact periodic tiling (periodic Perlin, Worley with wrapped cell indices and 3 independent jitter hashes). Houdini's units were **calibrated, not guessed**: W = 0.65·F1² and Perlin ×0.68 reproduce the cooked tool volume (`X/noise/out/vol128_f32.npy`): ours R mean 0.503 std 0.107 (tool 0.504 / 0.105, p1/p99 0.25/0.74 vs 0.25/0.75), G/B/A 0.695 / 0.105 (tool 0.686 / 0.104). Seam ratio (wrap difference / interior neighbour difference) 0.95-1.02 on all shape axes. Gains 1.7/2.3 give non-integer cells; rounded (17,32,43,63,9 / 23,44,57,85,12). Detail 32³: same Worley recipe at gains 0.188/0.32/0.432 so the finest octave sits at Nyquist (16 cells) — not in any source, ours. Curl: curl of three 2-octave periodic Perlin fBMs, quintic fade (the cubic fade creased the derivatives along lattice planes), z plane between lattice planes. `noiseFormat: 'rgba16float'` variant (SD8) included.
2. **NDF** per `work/specs/ndf.md`, read only through `ndfSample(xz)`. **Deviation:** stored as one rgba8unorm 2-layer array (layer 0 = ndfA, layer 1 = minH/maxH) instead of rgba8 + rg8: the procedural trace already binds 16 sampled textures (WebGPU default limit). Generator: 4-octave fBM coverage + 2-octave island gate, mapped **linearly** (a gradual field: a near-binary map made extruded NDF footprints instead of noise-shaped clouds); very-low-frequency top type 0.4-1; bottom type 0; min/max height 0 / 0.8 ±0.1. `uploadHzdNdf` / `proc.setNdf` for authored maps. Motion: `ndfMotion: 'drift'` (default, map at 0.5× the wind offset) or `'static'` (Nubis); shape/detail noise scroll at `shapeSpeed`/`detailSpeed` with GP7's upward bias.
3. **`hzd` shaper** (`?impls=hzd` or `?model=procedural&shaper=hzd`): GP7 chain verbatim — 4-channel dilation, Nutshell trapezoids, HFW coverage sign + gp7 `*cov` (switch), base fade before erosion, `h*10` / `*0.2` detail (Häggström law as a switch), curl on detail only with `(1-h)`, wind skew 500 m, `hzdAnvil` hook for S7. `hz_stage=1|2|3` shows the build stages.
4. **S6** in the Takram shaper: turbulence no longer moves the shape lookup (`takram.turbulenceOnShape`, default false). Fewer torn fragments in `s6-*/ground`; otherwise the same clouds.

## Choices no source publishes (all settings, all "ours")
Type-blend law (`typeBlend: 'points'` lerps the stops; `'curves'` = GP7's weighted sum). Base fade `remap(h, 0, 0.2, 0.3, 1)`. Tiles: shape 4 km, detail 400 m, curl 3 km / 150 m. Layer 1.5-5 km (GP7 p.119). Extinction 0.5 /m at density 1 (the chain's densities stay under ~0.3, so σ ≈ 0.02-0.15 /m; 0.05-0.2 read as fog). NDF tile 16 km (NE). GP7 reads the detail at `p*0.1` of the shape coordinate, which would make the 32³ texture 10x coarser than the 128³ one; we give it its own tile instead.

## Stage by stage vs the references
- **Fig 4.13 / hzd15 p037 (base × gradient, coverage remap only)** — `stage1`. Match: separated bodies with flat bases and rounded multi-lobe tops, noise-shaped not map-shaped. Differs: our lobes are more faceted (the Nubis R channel's carved Worley pits show as flat facets on shaded flanks); theirs are smoother blobs. Coverage similar.
- **Fig 4.14 / hzd15 p038 (with coverage, base fade)** — `stage2`. Match: same silhouettes as stage 1, bases thinner, small clouds dimmer (`*cov`). Differs: theirs reads softer/blurrier overall (their figure is lower-res and heavily filtered); ours has crisper rims.
- **Fig 4.16 / hzd15 p039 (with detail)** — `stage3`. Match: erosion sharpens the billows, wispy fragments at bases, clear sky between bodies, cloud count and spacing close at default coverage. Differs: ours erodes more mass than theirs (stage 2 → 3 visibly removes small clouds and thins bodies, by eye; GP7 4.14 → 4.16 keeps them), because our densities after `* cov` are small (0.05-0.3) against the 0.2 erosion cap; theirs look denser. Lighting (ours bluish-white octave model vs their warm, high-contrast HZD lighting) dominates the remaining difference and is out of scope.
- **N3 s37 `image55/56` (HFW in-game)**: far richer (Nubis-Cubed detail, crisp cauliflower at metre scale); the HZD floor does not reach it — expected; it is the target for S4/SD work, not for this shaper.
- Horizon/sunset (`hzd/`): far field fills to the horizon in rows; sunset gives dark bases with lit rims. Above (3 km, inside the 1.5-5 km layer) shows tall extruded flanks: the cumulus trapezoid's flat core makes vertical walls when seen from the side.

Measures (1920x1080, high, Apple GPU): procedural trace (`skyLayer`) 2.2-2.7 ms (Takram 1.8-2.4 at the same views); clouds total 3.9-4.4 ms. Bake 30-40 ms warm, one-off.
