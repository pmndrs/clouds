## Cause: the wispy channels, not an inverted channel
- **The noise is the pack's, bit for bit.** `assets/clouds/noise/noise.bin` mip 0 equals the pack TGAs. The TGAs correlate 1.000 with the pack's own `NubisVoxelCloudNoise.vdb` grids (lf/hf_curly_worley, lf/hf_alligator, R G B A in that order; dumped with hython). 8-bit error against the VDB floats is at most 0.0022 (half an LSB), so quantisation is not a factor.
- **What the channels look like:** B/A (Alligator) are dark rounded cells with a bright web (mean 0.39). Under ValueErosion, low n survives, so the cells stand out as convex bumps: the right sign for billows. R/G (Curly-Alligator) are mostly near 0 (half the voxels are below 1/16, mean 0.13) with isolated bright curled blobs that reach 1. Wherever the profile is above the fringe, they erode only those blobs, which leaves **round holes: the pits**.
- **Renders confirm it** (frog `diag-*`): type forced to 1 removes the pits, type 0 covers everything in them. Inverting B/A flattens the bumps into a grainy cellular skin; inverting R/G erodes the bodies down to dark cores. So the pack's sign is right for both pairs. The sharpen pow and S^4 are the same in pitted and clean renders, so they are not the cause.
- **Slide discrepancy (noted, not acted on):** the N3 s90/s89 tiles show all four channels as 1 - pack (bright Alligator cells, mostly-bright Curly). They match the notes' words ("inverted alligator ... web-like"), but with the shipped sampler only the pack's sign gives billows. Read the tiles as a display convention.
## Why our clouds go wispy where Guerrilla's don't
- In Guerrilla's data, wispy goes with thin: Parkouring's surface type has a median of 0 with S^4 0.14-0.29, so its holes are faint. The s98/s100 comment says "blend to wispy as the density scale decreases".
- Ours put opaque density where the type is wispy:
  - Frog: tops have T = 1, but the camera-facing flanks sit at T ≈ 0.6, with S 0.86-0.92 (S^4 0.6-0.75).
  - R1 closed volumes: T ≈ 0 with `hconst:1` scale.
- **Edge dilution:** our converter writes T = S = 0 outside the profile, but the pack dilates them (Parkouring's outside shell has T 0.645, S 0.628). Trilinear fetches at the surface, which is exactly the erosion band, are pulled toward wispy and thin. A3's `frog_fo` (fields outside) has visibly fewer pits (`diag-fields-outside.jpg`). That fix belongs in the converter (C2's file).
## Fix (behind a setting, default off)
- `typeFromScale` (0..1): `T' = mix(T, max(T, S), k)`, so dense regions cannot go wispy.
- At k = 1 the pits are gone on the Frog and on the R1 sponge, and the result is close to forcing type 1. Parkouring and CloudPack 02 are nearly unchanged (S is low there; mean |Δ| 0.3-1.1/255).
- It deviates from the sampler code: it follows the s98/s100 comment, not the code. Since it is not the source-correct behaviour, the **default stays 0**. I recommend 1 for authored/sim imports if the judges agree.
- `noiseInvert` and `detailTypeOverride` are diagnostics (defaults 0 and -1).
