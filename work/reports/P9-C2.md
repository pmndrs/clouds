Causes and fixes:
1. **Ac lattice.** Equal round cells on a 0.3-jittered lattice gave an acf peak of 0.21. Now:
   - Full jitter, a domain-warped lattice and a skewed cell size.
   - Cells combine as a soft union, so they merge into clumps and rows.
   - Roll bands run across the wind, with each cell stretched along its crest.
   - A clump fBm sets dense, merged zones and thin scatters.
2. **Cylinder towers and the near "boxes"/"marshmallows"** (also stratocumulus side). The main cause was the f-unit billow. On a tall row, f = s − h^p is almost flat over the low part, so the billow lifted a column by its own height or more. The field is cut at the footprint rim, so that lift ended in a vertical wall. Fixes:
   - The outward billow is capped at `BILLOW_LIFT·p·s`, so a column lifts at most about half its height. Erosion is not capped. The sky layer applies the same cap.
   - `tallMass` keeps small cells of tall rows low.
   - The congestus mass profile is peaked (emul 0.45 → 1.15, top exponent 1.9 → 1.6), with no plateau cells.
   - Height is bounded per cell at 0.7–2× its width.
   - `towerShift` displaces the walls in metres, so the flanks bulge and neck at different heights.
   - All of these are gated on the visible row depth, so a 512 m volume is unchanged.
3. **Stratus box outline.**
   - Sheets now hand off by a density cross-fade over 500 m (`NvdfParams.edgeFade` with sky `densityFade`).
   - A thin line along every box edge came from the sky trace split: front/back was split per jittered ray, and the composite of the averaged parts let the background through. The split now uses the pixel-centre ray.
   - The voxel top showed the up-rez noise tile as a regular dimple grid. `surfacePatch` now varies the profile law and the detail flip.
4. **Streaks** come from the **profile**, not the detail type (debug views 5 and 7). The bilinear coverage gradient (from a gather) jumps at every 25 m texel, and the depth f/|∇f| jumped with it up the walls. It is now a continuous finite difference of two bilinear samples.
