## Other technical measures
- **No stalls:** the light grid rebuilds over 8 frames (0.05–0.1 ms per frame). `animateTime=1` gives p95/p50 = 1.0 at the 60 Hz cap. ✅
- **First frame:** assets load in 50 ms; the first cloud frame is 90 ms after load (`work/captures/scratch/firstframe`). ✅
- **Density correctness:** WGSL vs the CPU port of `VoxelCloudSampler.cg` differs by 2.8e-7 on formula and 4.3e-3 end to end (hardware filter weights) (P1-04). ✅
- **Flicker:** wind frozen, same view captured 3× consecutively at 1280×720: inside mean |Δ| 0.08/255 (p99 1 level); edge and ground 0. Animated near jitter is not visible. ✅
- **Fly-in:** 17 positions from edge into Stormbird (`path:t` bookmarks). Brightness changes smoothly while outside. The drop on entering the cloud is spread over about 120 m of travel and the frames are visually continuous: no pop at the 200 m near/far split. ✅

## Look (vs Tidewater P0-04 and the Nubis slides)
- ✅ Billowy tops, wispy fragments, Nubis detail on authored shapes.
- ✅ Silver lining and storm-dark backlit body (Stormbird backlit).
- ✅ Sky-driven sun and ambient; deep blue sky; soft ground shadows.
- ⚠️ Inner glow: strong on Stormbird, weak on Parkouring.
- ⚠️ Dark edges with the sun behind the camera: weak, because the ambient term brightens edges.
- ⚠️ Undersides less blue-grey than Tidewater; undersides could be wispier.

These are tuning knobs (`msStrength`, `ambientStrength`, phase lobes, detail type bias), not architecture, so they go to P1-09 rather than being deferred.
