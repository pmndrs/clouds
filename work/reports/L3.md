# L3: mid-level layers (altocumulus, altostratus) as a 2.5D layer

## What changed
- A second `createCirrusLayer` instance, `runtime.midLayer`, shares the cirrus layer's baked noise tile (no second 16 MB bake; `cirrus.bakeNoise` drives the bake when the cirrus layer is off). The composite merges it before the cirrus layer (extra binding pair 19/20, `COMPOSITE_MID_BINDING`).
- The cirrus shader gained: a weather-map (NDF) read per area (`localLook`: coverage from map coverage, type from the map's top type, veil down where the map is cumuliform), a distance cross-fade against a volume (`fadeStart`/`fadeEnd`), an edge ramp (`edgeSoftness`, hides the 8-bit stair-step where the tile is magnified, off for cirrus), and the L6 coverage-fade switch. Cirrus defaults are unchanged.
- The HZD shaper's mid sheet takes a volume share (new ProcParams vec4 `hzMix`, so the LUT offset moved +4): full volume / fades with distance / none.
- The procedural model exposes `midLayer` (`MidLayerState`); the runtime copies it into `midLayer.settings` each frame (altitude = slot bottom + half thickness, wind from the volume's drift, streak angle from `windDirection`, water-droplet phase, no halo).
- Volume path untouched: `midLayerMode: 'volume'` is the old behaviour. Only the mid slot's altocumulus / altostratus is routed (a stratus form there and the deck slot stay volumes).

## Settings (bench tune panel, group "Mid-level layer and low deck")
`hzd.midLayerMode` volume | layer | hybrid (default **hybrid**; only acts when `midForm` is altocumulus / altostratus, which is still off by default), `midLayerFadeStart` 12000 m, `midLayerFadeEnd` 22000 m, `midLayerNdf` 1, `midLayerOpticalDepth` 4, `midLayerCoverageBoost` 0.4. Cirrus group: `cirrus.coverageFadeNe22` (L6).
URL keys: `hz_midLayerMode=...`.

## What I saw (work/captures/L3/sheet.jpg: rows horizon, high, ground, AS horizon, AS ground; columns volume, layer, hybrid; horizon-band.jpg is a 1:1 band)
- Volume mode carpets the sky in cells up to the march limit and stops on a straight line near the horizon (visible in horizon-band.jpg, top). Hybrid and layer have no line: the cells thin out into the layer, which continues to the horizon.
- Hybrid is indistinguishable from volume close in; far away the layer is **fainter and sparser** than the volume's carpet (just different, a bit worse in density: the layer's cells are a baked round noise, not the volume's flat-based domes). The altostratus layer is a smooth sheet where the map is stratiform.
- Layer-only: fine from the ground at distance, but at grazing angles (the `high` view, camera about 1 km under the layer) it smears into streaks, and close to the zenith the cells are soft. That is why hybrid is the default.
- Layer mode has no ground shadow (the volume's mid sheet shadowed the ground; the layer's `shadowStrength` is 0). Hybrid keeps the near shadows only.
- Versus NE22 p.62-68: the model is the same (streaky/wispy/round noise by type, exponent coverage, sun taps); type and coverage are now read from the map per area, as NE22 does. Better than the volume for the clip line and cost, worse for close views and layer-only density.

## Cost (GPU ms from capture.json; the machine was at load 96 so absolute values are inflated; compare within a row pair)
midLayer pass 0.3-0.55 ms. Sky layer volume to layer: horizon 21.6 to 19.8, high 23.7 to 21.2, ground 20.1 to 18.0 (AS ground 26.0 to 23.2): about -1.8 to -2.8 ms for +0.4. Hybrid saves roughly 0 to 1 ms (it keeps the near volume). Net: layer mode -1.5 to -2.5 ms, hybrid -0.3 to -1.

## L6: cirrus `cov^3` fade
The code is not `cov^3`. It is `ValueRemap(cov^2, 0, 0.36)`: full at cov 0.6. NE22 p.65 is `ValueRemap(cov^3, 0, 0.5)`: full at cov 0.79. At cov 0.5 ours scales density by 0.69, NE22 by 0.25; ours fades in much earlier, so the same coverage reads denser. Left as is; `cirrus.coverageFadeNe22 = true` switches to NE22 (cirrus-fade-ab.jpg: fewer streaks at 0.5). Owner to judge; if NE22's is wanted, coverage defaults would need raising (0.55 now).

## Problems and left
- The layer's coverage mapping (`midCoverage + boost`) and cell scale (cell size x 280) are by eye, not matched to the volume's statistics. Optical depth 4 is a guess.
- The volume's cross-fade distance (12-22 km) was not tuned against the march limit per view.
- No ground shadow from the layer; no layer lightning or fractus.
- `pnpm test` timed out once on the unrelated Disney VDB test under machine load; it passes alone.
