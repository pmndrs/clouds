Design:
- Storm cell (weather.ts `storm`, WEATHER_WGSL `stormCell`): life cycle on `age/growSeconds` (default 120 s sim): tower dissipates from 1.7x, anvil lives to 3.6x. The shape stage reads one [base, top] + mass per column, so the cell is a tower (core + 6 steep turrets, base on the ground, top = turrets, or >= the anvil top once there is an anvil), an anvil plate (tropopause, underside flaring down to the tower, tapered rim, carried downwind) and the low field (suppressed under the anvil), blended by power weights (continuous). Needs `useMapHeights` (evolution sets it when `storm` is given) and a tall volume: the demo uses 256x256x128 at 16 m (4 km x 2 km). Weather map uniform grew 96 -> 160 bytes; without `storm` the map is unchanged. No new volume format.
- Lightning (lightning.ts + march): CPU flash list with 2-4 return strokes + a dim continuing current over `duration`; `runtime.flash({position, radius, intensity, duration, color?, steady?})` and `settings.lightning = {rate, region, ...}` (Poisson flashes in a cylinder). One flash rendered at a time (the brightest). Frame uniform +32 bytes (flash pos+radius, colour*envelope). March: `if (frame.flash.w > 0.0)` uniform branch per lit sample, accumulating `flashAcc` apart from the sun terms (edge darkening does not touch it). Glow = Nubis `pow(1-d/r,12)*(1-sat(rho*5))` plus (deviation) a diffuse term `(1-d/r)^2 / (1+(d/180)^2) * exp(-0.012*sigma*d)`: the Nubis term alone is zero wherever rho > 0.2, i.e. in all of a Cb. Brightness is in units of the scene's own light (luminance of sun + ambient), so it follows exposure; colour is a parameter (default cold white-blue). Dome and distant sky layer ignore the flash.
- Temporal: no history reset (a cut would trace every pixel at full cost for the whole flash). While a flash is up, and one frame after, the resolve weight of fresh samples is max(temporalBlend, 0.85); the neighbourhood clip pulls history toward the fresh samples anyway. Flash is not in the cut index list.
Measures (2560x1440 high, storm page, sim 90, ground bookmark, `cloudsTotal` from capture.json, 2 runs each): lightning off 3.88 / 3.60 ms; steady flash on 3.49 / 3.64; storm cell off + no lightning 3.18 / 3.63. Automatic lightning run (1080p, play): cloudsTotal mean 2.11 during flash frames vs 1.98 calm (p95 3.6 vs 3.1, includes flash frames that coincide with generation swaps). Storm cell itself costs ~+0.2-0.4 ms (more cloud on screen), not a flash cost. Open issues:
- The storm is stylised: the tower is a wide bucket-like body with vertical flanks and a flat base; the heightfield column model (one interval per column) cannot make true overhangs, so the "anvil over the tower" is a plate whose underside flares into the tower. A regular dimple texture shows on the tower's flanks (profile noise correlation is 3 voxels = 48 m at 16 m voxels). Fixing these needs the shape stage to take a second field (volume-generator.ts, not owned here).
- Smear was not measured with a numeric diff; judged by the weighting design and one play capture (`auto/`) with no visible ghosting in the still. Reviewer: step through flash frames with `?step=1&rate=3`.
- Flash only lights the volume in the march (not the reflection dome or distant sky layer). One flash at a time. Hold-flash captures use a hand-placed radius 1500 / intensity 1.2 at dusk (`sunEl=3&exposure=0.5`); in full daylight a flash of intensity 1 is barely visible against the sunlit cloud.
- Pages: `?sim= ?play=1 ?step=1 ?timeScale= ?flash=hold|x,y,z,r,i ?lightning=0 ?rate= ?storm=0 ?bookmark=ground|distant|top|inside ?sunEl= ?exposure=`; key `f` fires a flash. The `inside` bookmark is inside solid cloud (white-out), not tuned.
