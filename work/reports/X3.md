Does:
1. **Lighting models, all switchable.**
   - Added `cloudOctaveLight` to `LIGHTING_WGSL`: 8 Wrenninge octaves with a = b = c = 0.5 (Frostbite H16 eq. 19-20, Takram `clouds.frag`), a two-lobe HG of 0.7 / −0.2, and powder.
   - Octave 0 keeps the sun. For octaves 1 and up, C1's low-sun handoff applies: the sun share scales with `body.x` / `body.w`, and the environment pair takes over isotropically.
   - Ambient is the sky irradiance / 4π along Hogfeldt's [0.5, 1] height gradient. From below it uses `skyLight[6]` (ground bounce), as Takram's GROUND_BOUNCE does; this also retunes the procedural ambient X1 measured as 4–6× too strong.
   - Per-altitude sun and the earth's shadow come from `sunRadianceAt` / `sunRadianceAtHeight`.
   - Switches: `CloudSettings.lightingModel` (default `'nubis'`) for the voxel march, the NVDF sky layer and the dome; `ProceduralSettings.lightingModel` (default `'octave'`).
   - Our N3 model is untouched and still selectable for the procedural model (`?light=nubis`).
2. **Hybrid** (`densityModel: 'hybrid'`).
   - The voxel march runs, and the procedural trace replaces the NVDF sky layer.
   - Each layer pixel reads the nearest voxel mean distance (the march's near/far history depth words, over its footprint) and splits its samples there into front and back parts. The composite's existing front-over-voxel-over-back merge then orders them.
   - Densities add where the two overlap (union). The procedural light taps also integrate the voxel field, so heroes shade the procedural cloud around them.
   - `boxCarve` (metres) fades the procedural field out inside the box instead; compare and open-water use 400 m for their generated field.
   - Pages opt in with `?model=hybrid` and `?model=procedural`. Onsen hybrid drops its generated cumulus and keeps the congestus hero, the fair-weather stamps and the valley fog inside a procedural sky.
3. **Shadows and reflections.**
   - The model now exposes its density WGSL and bindings (`densityWgsl` and related). It no longer reads `frame`; the camera and wind come in ProcParams.
   - The shadow map integrates the model with 64 midpoint steps over its shell. The plane drops to the shell base. The map refreshes on model changes and when the wind has moved the clouds about one 8 m texel.
   - The dome marches the model outside the box: cheap density, 4 sun taps and 2 up taps, in the model's own lighting.
4. **"Drip".** It is not the inverted base detail. The isolation views show it is a thin tongue of a layer-0 cloud sitting fully in the shadow of a layer-1 cloud, at about 5 % light: the octave sum dies as exp(−τ/128).
   - Fix: a diffusion tail, `sun · ISO · w / (1 + 0.11 τ)`, the two-stream diffuse transmittance, with w = 0.5 (`octaveDiffuse`).
   - Captures in scratch, `x3-drip*`: w = 0 drip, 0.5 soft grey, 1 too flat at midday.

Measures:
- The default NVDF path is unchanged. Against the pre-X3 runtime files swapped back, mean |Δ| is 0.10–0.18 for ground, horizon, sunset and dusk (p99 2–3); run-to-run noise is 0.14–0.50. For above it is 1.64, against 1.69 noise.
- Octave vs nubis on the procedural density (`sbs/`):
  - At sunset and dusk octave gives dark bodies with gold rims, as Takram does; nubis is soft mauve and hazy.
  - Dusk body median: octave about #3A383D, close to Takram's dark bodies, darker than the photo targets (#6B5C7E, #45556E).
- Shadows from procedural clouds: visible in `above` (procedural-*, hybrid) and on the onsen terrain.
- Hybrid ordering: in onsen, procedural clouds behind the hero tower are hidden by it and the ones in front cover it.
- Cost: GPU ms were very noisy under load (NVDF cloudsTotal read 23–26 ms here, against 6–9 in X1), so these are only rough. Shadow map with procedural 0.7–1.2 ms per refresh frame (NVDF 0.3 in P10-A). Dome 0.8–1.5 ms. Procedural trace 3.4–4.7 ms.

Open issues:
- **Mismatched lighting in one frame.** In hybrid, the NVDF hero is nubis-lit (bright white or lilac) next to darker octave procedural clouds. `?vlight=octave` makes them match (`onsen-hybrid-octave/`). The default needs the owner's call.
- **Heroes are not shadowed by procedural clouds.** The light grid (`light-grid.ts`, not mine) only sees the NVDF sky field. A procedural tau above the box in the light-grid bake would fix it.
- **Shadow-map cost.** Its procedural pass is slower than the NVDF one; 32 steps or skipping empty weather would be the next lever.
- **Pre-existing NVDF artefact.** In open-water `cruise`, a blocky distant voxel cloud (a "spike") also shows on the NVDF path with the sky layer's density at 0. It is not from X3.
- **Untested path.** The procedural uniform-lighting path (no sky library) uses `skyLight[3]`/`[4]` from the voxel box.
- **Research index.** The Takram ground bounce and BSM notes are in this report; the index row was not updated (I don't own `docs/`).
