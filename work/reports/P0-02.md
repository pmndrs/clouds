Verified:
- `npx vitest run scripts/nubis` → 10 tests pass (TGA raw/RLE/top-left flip/truncation, EDT vs brute force, sphere, box, r16 encode, crop, mips).
- Ran `convert.mjs` twice, `shasum` of all outputs identical.
- `pnpm typecheck` shows nothing for scripts/nubis (plain .mjs, not type-checked).
- Run time: 3.1 s total including the pack check.
Measures:
- Voxel size 8 m fits. |d|<200 m samples: Parkouring n=708k median 4.39 m, p90 10.4 m, sign mismatch 0/644k beyond one 17.1 m quantum. Stormbird n=626k median 4.89 m, p90 11.8 m, mismatch 529/540k, all but 25 at grid z<2 (504 on z<2), where the pack SDF is 0 at the floor cut.
- Other sizes (isotropic, median): 4 m 55/42, 6 m 27/21, 10 m 27/25, 12 m 55/47, 16 m 110/92 (Parkouring/Stormbird); anisotropic 8x8x4, 8x8x16, 16x16x8 also worse. 8 m is the clear optimum. Check config: SDF surface at half voxel from centres, mask = profile > 0.
- Sizes: parkouring 108x108x51, offset (167,103,13), nvdf 2,379,456 B + sdf 1,189,728 B = 3.57 MB. stormbird 83x116x62, offset (212,198,0), nvdf 2,387,744 + sdf 1,193,872 = 3.58 MB. Both under 4 MB. noise.bin 9,586,980 B (128^3 x4 + 8 mips).
- Axis facts: modeling and field TGAs have origin bit bottom-left (rows bottom-up); decoder normalises to bottom-up so +y is up in the source image. Slice 001 = z 0 = bottom. Occupied: Parkouring x175-266, y111-202, z21-57; Stormbird x220-286, y206-305, z0-53.
- Pack SDF encoding confirmed: byte 15 = 0 m ((0+256)/4352*255).
Open issues:
- SDF decode in the runtime: `m = -256 + v*4352`; `v` is r16unorm so no extra scaling. Rounding down means the decoded value can be up to 0.07 m too low (conservative).
- Stormbird is cut by the grid floor (z=0); no margin below there.
- The 8 m check is also run inside the converter against the full pack path (hard-coded default, `--pack` overrides).
- Noise mips are plain 8-texel averages (wrap only matters for odd sizes, none here).
