## Summary
Connects the rendering equation to the linear Boltzmann transport equation used in neutron and nuclear physics. Imports neutron-transport techniques into Monte Carlo ray tracing, including Russian roulette for unbiased path termination and splitting, and frames the ray-tree versus path-tracing question. Argues that neither pure ray trees nor pure paths are optimal and that a hybrid may be more efficient.

## Use for us
Gives the theory behind Russian roulette and splitting for budgeting volumetric path and shadow rays, and the path-versus-tree trade-off relevant to multiple scattering in clouds.
