## Summary
From the title and the session listing, DMCR-GAN is an adversarial denoiser for Monte Carlo renderings. It combines residual attention networks with hierarchical modulation of auxiliary buffers, which is the same family as XZW19. The listing names authors from the Center for Future Media at UESTC. I could not read the method, the results or the timing, so I cannot say how it differs from XZW19 beyond the residual attention blocks.

## Use for us
Low priority. It adds little beyond XZW19 for us unless the full text shows a clearly cheaper attention block. Revisit only if we go to a GAN-style denoiser.
