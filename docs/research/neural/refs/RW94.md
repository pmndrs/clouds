## Summary
A non-linear filter for noisy rendered images that smooths sample noise while keeping salient features. Each sample's value is spread to neighbours through variable-width kernels, normalised so the total energy (sum of radiance) is preserved. The HTML version covers the filters, the rendering equation context, filter design and example applications.

## Use for us
Energy preservation matters for HDR cloud radiance: a denoise pass must not darken or brighten the cloud, so check our filter conserves the sum.
