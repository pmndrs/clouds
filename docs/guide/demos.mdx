The lab pages are the proof of every claim in these docs. Start the dev server and open them:

```sh
pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
pnpm dev            # http://127.0.0.1:5173/  (a WebGPU desktop browser)
```

Each page installs the capture contract (`window.__clouds`), so it can be shot headlessly on the real GPU:

```sh
node scripts/gpu/capture.mjs --serve --page three-adapter/ --shots ground,edge,backlit --out work/captures/scratch/mine
```

`--help` lists the options. The harness waits for `ready`, settles 90 frames per shot and writes a JPEG plus a `capture.json` with the
page's report (GPU ms per pass, frame times, console errors). Evidence from the phases is committed under `work/captures/`.

Lines in the pages tagged `// INTEGRATION` (or `// CLOUDS`) are what clouds cost the scene. Lines tagged `// CONTENT` build the cloud data.

## three-adapter

[`examples/lab/three-adapter/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/three-adapter/main.ts). The smallest `@clouds/three` scene: a ground plane, the sky and the Parkouring cloud.
Eight lines are tagged `// INTEGRATION`, one of them the optional haze (plus the `applyShadow` line for [cloud shadows](./api.mdx#cloud-shadows), switched off by `?cloudshadow=0`). Matches the flagship to a mean 0.11 to 0.38 of 255 per channel on the same bookmarks [P2-02].

Query: `?quality=low|medium|high|ultra|mobile|auto`, `?uniform=1` (no sky: uniform lighting), `?haze=1` (`sky.applyHaze` before the node),
`?objects=1` (adds bookmarks `objects`, `pierce`, `front`, `behind`), `?s.<setting>=N` (any `CloudSettings` value), `?exposure=`.
Bookmarks: `ground`, `edge`, `inside`, `above`, `backlit`, `shadow`, `frontlit`.

**Depth sorting.** With `?objects=1` a 1300 m tower pierces the cloud, a sphere floats in front of it and one sits 650 m behind: the cloud hides the tower where it is in front,
the tower shows above and below, and a 3x zoom shows no halo at the silhouettes [P2-05].

## r3f

[`examples/lab/r3f/main.tsx`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/r3f/main.tsx). The same scene with `<Canvas>`, `<Sky>` and `<Clouds>`; the clouds themselves are one line. It matches
the three-adapter page to a mean 0.03 to 0.07 of 255 [P7-A]. `?haze=1|late|own` puts `<AutoHaze />` before, after, or `<Clouds haze>` (all three compose and the haze is applied once),
`?uniform=1`, `?bookmark=`, `?quality=`, `?s.<setting>=N`.
`?stress=1` runs a 16-step stress script (it also covers `<Cloudscape>` standalone and nested): volume swap, settings set and removed, quality changes, three remounts, `<Cloudscape>` standalone, nested and a preset change.
Result: 0 GPU errors, 0 device lost, and live GPU objects return to baseline (53 textures, 85 buffers after 292 and 364 created along the way). Use `--settle 1500` in the capture for it to finish.

## open-water

[`examples/lab/open-water/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/open-water/main.ts) with the reusable water in [`examples/lab/scenes/ocean/`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/tree/main/examples/lab/scenes/ocean). Open sea to the horizon
(layered Gerstner-style waves in TSL, Fresnel reflection of the sky, a sun glint from the sky's sun, a camera-following grid with distance LOD) under a generated cumulus field.

- **Reflections.** `clouds.reflection(dir)` is hooked into the ocean material's environment node (`radiance = radiance * T + rgb`; 11 lines in the ocean module). Subtle on the rough default sea, clear on calm water (`?wave=0.05`): mean luma of the lower 590 px with and without reflections 42.3 vs 62.4 at sunset, 120.6 vs 117.4 at the horizon [P2-05].
- **Shadows.** Cloud shadows fall on the water through the shadow map: `applyShadow(sun.light)` with `receiveShadow` on the ocean mesh, so only the direct sun and glint are dimmed (`?cloudshadow=0` returns to the composite path). `?shadowmap=0` falls back to the light-grid shadow, `?shadowdebug=1|2` outlines the map's contour on the ground or the cloud base, `?sm_<setting>=N` sets a `shadowMap.settings` value, `?sunel=` and `?sunaz=` move the sun.
- **Cost.** 60 fps at 2560x1440 `high` with a p95 frame of 16.7 ms and 5 to 6 ms of whole-frame GPU in steady state [P2-06].
- Query: `?preset=cumulus|congestus|stratocumulus`, `?seed=`, `?reflect=0`, `?refgain=N` (debug gain on the cloud rgb), `?wave=`, `?cloud=<pack>` (a pack instead of the generator), `?quality=`.
- Bookmarks: `shore`, `horizon`, `sunset`, `flight`.

## plains-flight

[`examples/lab/plains-flight/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/plains-flight/main.ts). A scripted 40 s flight over plains through a **streaming** GPU-generated cumulus field: ground, up through the base,
through the clouds, above. One `setVolume` call per window swap; the volume belongs to the streamer. Cloud shadows move across the fields with `applyShadow` (`?cloudshadow=0` for the composite path).

- Window swaps are invisible: weather and shape noise are world-anchored, so two overlapping windows are byte-identical in the interior of the overlap (0 occupancy mismatches in 1.57 M voxels) and the swap changes the image by 0.2/255 [P3-D].
- Generation of a window takes 48 frames at 2 jobs a frame, with a worst encode and submit of 0.2 ms; the swap frame spikes about 1 ms (light grid) [P5-A].
- Cloud passes along the path (2560x1440, `high`): 2.1 to 3.4 ms with k = 4 temporal amortization, 2.4 to 6.1 ms without [P5-B].
- Query: `?t=<s>` jump to a time, `?play=1` real-time playback, `?bookmark=t0..t40|swapA|swapB`, `?preset=`, `?seed=`, `?quality=`, `?jobs=`, `?border=`, `?k=<near>,<far>` (temporal rates), `?sunDrift=<deg/s>`.

## onsen

[`examples/lab/onsen/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/onsen/main.ts) with the terrain in [`examples/lab/scenes/terrain/`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/tree/main/examples/lab/scenes/terrain). An onsen on a mountainside at golden hour over a misty valley.
A 512x512x96 generated cumulus region at 12 m voxels is stamped with 10 valley-fog banks, 5 fair-weather clouds and one tower, the SDF is rebuilt once, and the whole thing is one `setVolume`.
Haze comes from `sky.applyHaze`; cloud shadows are multiplied onto the heightfield's baked sun shadow with `applyShadow` (call it after the terrain installs its shadow node; `?cloudshadow=0` turns it off); the heightfield's sun shadow is baked into per-tile textures and refreshed when the sun moves.

Whole frame 9.2 to 14.3 ms at 2560x1440 (60 fps vsync, p50 16.7 ms), of which terrain, pool and rocks are about 4 ms of scene and the clouds 5 to 10 ms before the P5 optimisations [P4-D]. Ten `// INTEGRATION` lines.
Query: `?time=<hours>` (default 18.6), `?bookmark=vantage|valley|sunset`, `?fogd=` (valley-fog density), `?skylayer=1` (the distant layer), `?clouds=0`, `?terrain=0`, `?pool=0`, `?shadow=0`, `?haze=`, `?pos=x,y,z&az=&pitch=`.

## vdb-drop

[`examples/lab/vdb-drop/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/vdb-drop/main.ts). Drag a `.vdb` onto the page, or pick one, or `?url=<path>` for a file the dev server can reach (`/@fs/<abs path>.vdb`; `fs.allow` is `../..` relative to the checkout that serves it).
It overlays the grid table, the mode picked, dims, timings, and frame/GPU ms, and builds `ground`, `edge`, `backlit`, `above` bookmarks from the imported bounds. Errors are shown in the page (for example
the Blosc codec hint), not thrown.

- The Nubis pack Parkouring VDB imports byte-exact against the pack conversion in 64 ms (`?compare=parkouring` reads the NVDF back and diffs it).
- The Disney WDAS cloud and the OpenVDB bunny cloud import as fog volumes and render.
- Query: `?mode=auto|pack|fog|sdf`, `?voxel=8`, `?scale=<world scale>`, `?width=<metres>`, `?iso=0.05`, `?grid=<name>`, `?seed=1`, `?quality=`, `?haze=1`.
- Without `?width` or `?scale`, a cloud narrower than 600 m is scaled to 1500 m wide (artist VDBs are in real-world units). The library itself never auto-scales.

## flagship

[`examples/lab/flagship/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/flagship/main.ts). The reference page, built on the **raw runtime** with its own bridge to three
([`three-bridge.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/flagship/three-bridge.ts)), so it is the worked example of using `@clouds/runtime` without the adapter. Orbit and free-fly camera, a time slider,
sliders for `CloudSettings`, and a HUD with tier, scale, cost versus budget and memory.

- `?cloud=` any of `parkouring` (default), `stormbird`, `houdini-test`, `heroes/tower`, `heroes/valley-fog`, `heroes/fairweather`, `heroes/sim-cumulus`, `heroes/sim-congestus`, `disney`, `local/bunny-cloud`, `local/cloud-0001`. The `local/*` assets are git-ignored.
- `?quality=ultra|high|medium|low|mobile|auto` and `?budget=<ms>` (`'auto'`), `?trace=<frames>` to record the auto-quality convergence in the report, `?time=<hours>`, `?s.<setting>=N`, `?pan=<deg/frame>` (a motion test for the temporal history).
- Bookmarks `ground`, `edge`, `inside`, `above`, `backlit`, `shadow`, `frontlit`.

## cloudscape-gen

[`examples/lab/cloudscape-gen/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/cloudscape-gen/main.ts). A GPU-generated 4 km cloudscape (512x512x64 at 8 m) over a ground plane. `?preset=cumulus|congestus|stratocumulus`, `?seed=`, `?jobs=`,
`?bookmark=ground|horizon|above|seamEast|seamSouth|seamNorth|seamHigh`, `?sky=1` for the distant sky layer (and `sky_<setting>=N` for its `SkyLayerSettings`), `?shadowmap=0`, `?shadowdebug=1|2`, and any `NvdfParams` field as a query parameter (`w_<option>` for the weather map's).
It showed the presets read as domed, cauliflower-lobed clouds with flat bases (no vertical walls or stacked shelves) and the sky layer continues the same field to the horizon with no visible seam, for about 1.0 ms amortized at 2560x1440 [P3-B, P3-C1, P3-P4-acceptance].

## evolve

[`examples/lab/evolve/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/evolve/main.ts). Realtime cloud evolution: cumulus that form, drift, merge and dissipate over minutes at a small steady cost.
`createEvolvingCloudscape` regenerates the field every `cadence` seconds in amortized jobs into a back volume and `swapVolume` takes it without cutting the history; see
[Evolve a cloudscape over time](./api.mdx#evolve-a-cloudscape-over-time).

- Query: `?timeScale=<n>` (simulated seconds per real second, default 10), `?t=<s>` (freeze at a demo time), `?play=1`, `?step=1` (deterministic playback, 1/60 s a frame), `?bookmark=ground|above|horizon|top`, `?preset=`, `?seed=`, `?life=<sim s>`, `?wind=x,z`, `?cadence=`, `?evolve=0` (A/B for the added cost), `?inplace=0` (swap with `setVolume`), `?diff=1` (mean frame-to-frame change and the swap frames).

## storm

[`examples/lab/storm/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/storm/main.ts). A cumulus field grows a cumulonimbus over about two minutes of simulated time (a leaning column of turrets under an overhanging, downwind anvil),
and lightning lights it from inside. The volume is 256 x 256 x 128 at 16 m (4 km by 2 km). See [Storm cell](./api.mdx#storm-cell) and [Lightning](./api.mdx#lightning). A held flash adds within noise of nothing (3.49 against 3.64 ms), and the cell
itself costs about 0.2 to 0.4 ms because there is more cloud on screen [P6-B, P6-C].

- Query: `?timeScale=<n>` (default 4), `?sim=<s>` (freeze at a simulated time; `?play=1` plays from it, `?step=1` plays deterministically), `?flash=hold` (a steady flash at the cell) or `?flash=x,y,z,r,i`, `?lightning=0`, `?rate=<per s>` (automatic flashes; default 0.25 once the cell is a congestus),
  `?storm=0` (the same field without the cell), `?bookmark=ground|distant|top|inside|anvil`, `?wind=x,z`, `?height=`, `?radius=`, `?anvil=` (the cell's mature size in metres), `?p_<NvdfParams key>=N`, `?sunEl=`, `?sunAz=`, `?exposure=`. The `f` key fires a flash.
- A flash in daylight is faint; the captures use `?sunEl=3&exposure=0.5`. The `inside` bookmark is inside solid cloud and is not tuned.

## species

## sunset

[`examples/lab/sunset/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/sunset/main.ts). The layered sunset: long stratocumulus bands with gaps under a broken altocumulus layer at about 1.6 km, haze at the horizon, and the sun low in the west behind the bands, shot from the east of the
field so the bands lie across the view as silhouettes. It is the page for the [low-sun lighting](./api.mdx#low-sun-lighting) and [horizon haze](./api.mdx#horizon-haze) settings.

- Query: `?bookmark=golden|sunset|afterglow|side|zenith` (the sun at +4, +1 and -1 degrees, +1 with the sun to the left, and looking up the sun side at -1; each bookmark has its own absolute exposure), `?sunel=`, `?sunaz=`, `?lookaz=`, `?pitch=`, `?fov=`, `?exposure=`, `?seed=`, `?quality=`,
  `?sky=0`, `?c_<setting>=N` (any `CloudSettings` number, for example `c_lightDebug=1..6`), `?sky_<setting>=N`, `?w_<key>=N`, `?<NvdfParams key>=N`.

## library

[`examples/lab/library/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/library/main.ts). The 17 [authored clouds](./authoring.mdx#the-authored-library) stamped into one 6.1 km, 1.5 km tall region (512 x 512 x 128 at 12 m), 36 instances with seeded yaw, scale and position.

- Query: `?seed=N` (layout, default 7), `?count=N` (36), `?spacing=F` (gap between footprints as a fraction of their radii sum, 0.55; lower merges more), `?repeat=M` (minimum distance between two copies of the same cloud, 1800 m), `?only=a,b`, `?gen=1` (the generator's cumulus underneath, untuned), `?sheet=1`
  (contact-sheet mode: one cloud at a time, side-lit; the bookmarks are the cloud names), `?quality=`, `?exposure=`, `?clean=1`. Bookmarks `ground`, `horizon`, `above`.

## compare

[`examples/lab/compare/main.ts`](https://github.com/pmndrs/clouds/blob/main/examples/lab/compare/main.ts). The same scene, camera, sun and exposure through several cloud implementations, each in its own canvas: `ours` (this library), `takram` (`@takram/three-clouds`) and `skypro` (a vendored copy of
Tidewater's Sky Pro clouds), plus a `photo` pane for a reference photograph. It is how the look is judged; the Takram and Sky Pro packages are dev dependencies of the repository only.

- Query: `?impls=ours,skypro,takram,photo` (any order; default `ours,takram`), `?layout=rows|cols`, `?bookmark=ground|horizon|sunset|dusk|afterglow|above`, `?coverage=0..1`, `?exposure=`, `?fov=`, `?sunel=`, `?sunaz=`, `?lookaz=`, `?pitch=`, `?hud=0`, `?clean=1`, `?clouds=0` (a clear sky in every pane),
  `?quality=`, `?seed=`, `?sky=0` (ours), `?c_<setting>=N` (ours: a `CloudSettings` number; `c_lightDebug=1..5` splits the light terms), `?phase=mie`, `?takram_quality=`, `?stbn=<url>`, `?shadows=1`.
- **Photo pane.** `?impls=ours,photo&photo=<ref>` where `<ref>` is `/@fs/<absolute path>`, a bare absolute path (rewritten to `/@fs/...`) or an http(s) or blob URL; `?photofit=cover|contain`. You can also drop an image on the page or use the bar's "photo file" button, and
  `?photooverlay=1&photoopacity=0.5` (or the `P` key) lays the photo over the renders. The dev server serves files only inside the repository and the research folder (`CLOUDS_RESEARCH_DIR` adds one). Every pane's label shows the sun elevation and bookmark. Keep photo captures out of version control.
- Lessons it taught: the horizon needs aerial perspective and haze, low sun needs a body that goes sky-lit (both now in the runtime), and altitude sets apparent cloud size (Takram's first layer is at 750 m; `?base=` moves ours).

## runtime-tests

Each is a page plus a report that the capture harness exits non-zero on if a check fails.

| Page | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| `/runtime-tests/density/` | GPU density against the CPU port of Guerrilla's `VoxelCloudSampler.cg`: formula max error 2.8e-7, end to end 4.3e-3. |
| `/runtime-tests/atmosphere/` | The atmosphere WGSL against the sky's own sun radiance and ambient (5 percent per channel). |
| `/runtime-tests/light-grid/` | CPU-march parity on a synthetic sphere; the 8-frame sun-change behaviour; update cost. |
| `/runtime-tests/dome/` | The reflection dome as an equirect panorama, the sun-change refresh and a frame-stall check. |
| `/runtime-tests/generate/` | Generated SDF against an exact CPU EDT over every voxel of 256x256x32 and each preset (never over, no sign flips); overlap byte-identity of two windows; timings. |
| `/runtime-tests/stamp/` | Stamp resampling against a CPU reference; the SDF after stamping against a CPU EDT over 14.2 M voxels (never over: max -4 m, 0 sign flips); per-stamp cost. |
| `/runtime-tests/generate/sky-bake/` | The sky layer's tiled weather bake in the running runtime: no frame spends more than 1 ms in bake passes (worst 0.37 to 0.51 ms, against 13 to 16 ms for the one-shot bake), the old window renders until the new one is complete, the swap adds no full-resolution trace frame. `?rows=2048` is the old one-shot bake; `?changes=0` with the `wallEast`, `wallSouth` and `wallEastTop` bookmarks looks along a box wall for seams. |
| `/runtime-tests/hero-fine/` | The [fine hero overlay](./api.mdx#fine-hero-overlay): the brick pool against a dense trilinear volume within 1/255 (0 measured) with every brick and with a random half, bumps visible at 4 m and not at 8 m, and the all-fine march cost against dense (1.14x at `'high'`). `still.html` renders a fixed-clock scene for byte-for-byte A/B of a tree against another. |

## Review loop

`node scripts/review-scenes.mjs --label <name>` re-shoots every scene target (sunset, compare, species, open-water, plains-flight, onsen: 15 shots in 6 groups, about a minute on a desktop GPU) with fixed bookmarks through the capture harness, and writes
`work/captures/review/<date-time>-<name>/` with a contact sheet (`sheet.jpg`) and, when an earlier review folder exists, a before/after sheet (`vs-previous.jpg`). `--from <folder> --photos` rebuilds the sheets without shooting and adds side-by-sides with reference photos
into `work/captures/scratch/review/` (never committed); `CLOUD_PHOTOS` overrides the photo folder. Run it before and after any change to lighting, haze or generation to see what moved.
